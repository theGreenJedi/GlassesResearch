# Community & Development

GlassesResearch covers the **entire smart-glasses ecosystem**. This directory links directly to real communities, developer groups, forums, repositories, and project spaces across manufacturers and platforms. A resource belongs here because it is concrete and useful, not because a category ought to exist.

## Cross-vendor and open smart-glasses communities

- [Mentra Community — GitHub](https://github.com/Mentra-Community) — open-source smart-glasses OS, SDK, applications, hardware projects, and cross-device development.
- [Mentra Discord](https://mentra.glass/discord) — developer and user community linked by the MentraOS project.
- [Open Source Smart Glasses](https://github.com/Mentra-Community/OpenSourceSmartGlasses) — open hardware/software smart-glasses project and community lineage from Team Open Smart Glasses / H20 Smart Glasses Community.
- [r/SmartGlasses](https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/) — independent general smart-glasses discussion covering devices, ownership, development, troubleshooting, and releases.

## W610 / HeyCyan-compatible ecosystem

The W610 is one device family within GlassesResearch, not the center of the project.

- [HeyCyanSmartGlassesSDK — GitHub](https://github.com/ebowwa/HeyCyanSmartGlassesSDK) — community SDK and BLE implementation for HeyCyan-compatible glasses.
- [CyanBridge / Alternative HeyCyan App and SDK — GitHub](https://github.com/FerSaiyan/Alternative-HeyCyan-App-and-SDK) — alternative Android companion stack.
- [CyanBridge releases](https://github.com/FerSaiyan/Alternative-HeyCyan-App-and-SDK/releases) — release artifacts and notes.
- [CyanBridge v2.0.0 discussion — r/SmartGlasses](https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/comments/1sg1fyr/alternative_heycyan_app_massive_release_v200/) — community discussion of the alternative stack.

## Community inclusion rule

Community links are **not limited to models we own or have tested**. As verified communities are found for RayNeo, Rokid, Solos, Even Realities, Vuzix, TCL, Lenovo, Amazon, Google, and other manufacturers represented in [The List](../models/THE_LIST.md), they belong here.

Do not add generic text such as “search Reddit” or “look for a Discord.” Add the actual community, project, forum, repository, or support route—or add nothing until one is found.
