# Meta Smart-Glasses Lineage

**Whole-manufacturer audit:** 2026-09-05  
**Audit packet:** [Manufacturer Completeness Wave 02](../research/investigations/MANUFACTURER_COMPLETENESS_WAVE_02_2026-09-05.md)

This lineage covers Meta's consumer smart-glasses platform across Ray-Ban, Oakley and Meta-branded eyewear. The relationship is a corporate/software/platform lineage; frame-brand differences do not erase the shared Meta platform relationship, but materially named electronics generations remain distinct.

## Meta Glasses style boundary

Wave 02 specifically audited the 2026 Meta-branded family because first-party pages expose several prominent names. Meta describes **Adventurer, Fury and Starfire/Kylie styling as styles within the Meta Glasses collection**, not as separately identified electronics generations.

GlasssesResearch therefore does **not** mint extra GLS IDs for Fury or Starfire merely because each has its own frame identity. `GLS-0165`, originally admitted from exact Adventurer manufacturer/retail evidence, remains the canonical purchaser-history anchor for the shared Meta Glasses platform while sibling styles are preserved in the lineage/registry layer.

If future regulatory, teardown or manufacturer evidence proves materially different electronics between those styles, the identity decision can be reopened. Marketing names alone are insufficient.

## Lineage evolution

The lineage evolves from Ray-Ban Stories camera/audio capture toward increasingly capable AI eyewear, sport-specialized Oakley implementations, Meta-branded frame families and visible-display capability with Meta Ray-Ban Display. Its clearest strengths are mature consumer distribution, polished companion software, eyewear-brand partnerships and a large installed-user ecosystem.

Its main GlassesResearch tradeoff is openness: compared with developer-first lineages, the platform exposes less low-level owner control and is more dependent on Meta software/services. That tradeoff must still be measured per generation rather than inherited wholesale.

## Completeness result

Wave 02 collision-checked the current Meta smart-glasses population and the prominent 2026 style names. **No additional missing electronics generation crossed the canonical admission threshold.** The audit's value here is anti-inflation: it prevents style proliferation from becoming false model proliferation.

Meta remains monitored for future named generations, display branches, regional regulatory identities and substantive electronics changes.

## Best-fit use cases

Everyday camera/audio capture, voice-assistant use, mainstream consumer wear, sport-oriented variants and users who prioritize an integrated consumer experience over low-level development access.

## Research questions

GlassesResearch tracks generational changes in cameras, battery, AI functions, privacy behavior, display capability, software/account dependencies, service survival, local residue and developer access across the lineage.
