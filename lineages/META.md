# Meta Smart-Glasses Lineage

This lineage covers Meta's consumer smart-glasses platform across Ray-Ban and Oakley branded products. The relationship is a corporate/software/platform lineage; frame brand differences do not erase the shared Meta platform relationship.

## Lineage analysis

The lineage has evolved from camera/audio capture toward increasingly capable AI eyewear and, with Meta Ray-Ban Display, visible-display capability. Its clearest strengths are mature consumer distribution, polished companion software, eyewear-brand partnerships, and a large installed-user ecosystem. Its main research tradeoff is openness: compared with developer-first lineages, the platform exposes less low-level owner control and is more dependent on Meta's software/services.

## Best-fit use cases

Everyday camera/audio capture, voice-assistant use, mainstream consumer wear, sport-oriented variants, and users who prioritize integrated consumer experience over low-level development access.

## Research questions

GlassesResearch tracks generational changes in cameras, battery, AI functions, privacy behavior, display capability, software dependencies, and developer access across the lineage.
