# Technology Lineages

Smart glasses are often sold under brand names that hide shared software, protocols, hardware platforms, ODM relationships, and developer ecosystems. GlassesResearch therefore tracks **technology lineages** alongside manufacturers and individual models.

A lineage does **not** assert that every member has the same manufacturer or identical hardware. It records a documented family relationship and states what kind of relationship is actually supported.

## Lineage relationship types

- **Software lineage** — shared companion application, service, operating environment, or software platform.
- **Protocol lineage** — shared or demonstrably compatible device communication behavior.
- **Firmware lineage** — shared firmware family or update artifacts.
- **Hardware lineage** — component, PCB, optical, mechanical, or reference-platform evidence.
- **ODM/OEM lineage** — documented manufacturing or design relationship.
- **Corporate lineage** — product generations maintained by the same company/platform owner.
- **Retail-rebrand lineage** — the same or related platform appears under multiple retail brands.

Each relationship is reported separately. Membership in a software lineage is never treated as proof of common hardware or manufacture.

## Confidence

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Direct primary, regulatory, technical, source-code, or hands-on evidence establishes the relationship. |
| **High confidence** | Multiple independent technical indicators agree, but one decisive link remains unavailable. |
| **Moderate confidence** | Several characteristics align; useful as a research relationship, not an identity claim. |
| **Hypothesis** | Plausible lead retained for investigation; not presented as fact. |

## Lineage research

Each lineage is analyzed as a research subject in its own right: current models, evolution, shared characteristics, development ecosystem, compatibility evidence, strengths, weaknesses, use cases, communities, and unresolved relationships.

### Current lineage chapters

- [HeyCyan](HEYCYAN.md) — shared companion/software ecosystem spanning W610 and retail-branded devices including Anko.
- [Meta smart glasses](META.md) — Ray-Ban and Oakley smart-glasses generations built around Meta's wearable platform.
- [Google Glass](GOOGLE_GLASS.md) — Explorer Edition through Enterprise Edition 2; a foundational corporate/product lineage spanning consumer experimentation and enterprise assisted reality.
- [Vuzix](VUZIX.md) — corporate lineage split into the Z100 phone-peripheral branch and standalone Android wearable-computer branches including M400/M4000, Blade 2, Shield, and LX1.
- [XREAL / Nreal](XREAL.md) — Nreal/XREAL personal-display and spatial-computing glasses.
- [Brilliant Labs](BRILLIANT_LABS.md) — Monocle, Frame, and Halo open/developer lineage.
- [Snap Spectacles](SNAP.md) — camera-glasses and later AR Spectacles generations.
- [Solos AirGo](SOLOS.md) — AirGo audio/camera glasses and developer ecosystem.
