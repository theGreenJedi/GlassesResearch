# Snap Spectacles Lineage

**Whole-manufacturer audit:** 2026-09-05  
**Audit packet:** [Manufacturer Completeness Wave 02](../research/investigations/MANUFACTURER_COMPLETENESS_WAVE_02_2026-09-05.md)

This lineage covers Snap's Spectacles generations from consumer camera glasses through developer-oriented AR eyewear and the 2026 consumer spatial-computing generation.

## Canonical models in GlassesResearch

- `GLS-0040` — Spectacles (1st Gen)
- `GLS-0041` — Spectacles 2
- `GLS-0042` — Spectacles 3
- `GLS-0067` — Spectacles (2021, 4th Gen)
- `GLS-0068` — Spectacles (2024, 5th Gen)
- `GLS-0146` — SPECS (2026 consumer generation)

## Spectacles 2 style boundary

Wave 02 specifically rechecked **Nico, Veronica and Original** because those names can look like separate product identities in surviving support material. Snap's own purchase/support pages group all three under **Spectacles 2**, and regulatory material identifies Nico and Veronica under model number `002`.

They therefore remain frame/style variants of `GLS-0041`, not extra canonical generations. A named frame is not sufficient for a new GLS ID when the manufacturer itself groups it under the same generation/model family.

## Lineage evolution

The lineage shows a clear transition from consumer camera capture toward standalone augmented-reality development hardware and, with SPECS, back toward a publicly orderable consumer spatial computer. Its strengths are continuity of a recognizable product family, a mature creator/developer environment around Lens Studio and increasingly capable AR interaction.

Its recurring tradeoff is physical: later spatial generations are substantially more headset-like in weight and dependency than ordinary optical glasses, even while remaining eyewear-form devices.

## Completeness result

Wave 02 collision-checked Snap's six canonical generations against surviving first-party product, purchase, support and regulatory material. **No missing purchaser-history generation crossed the admission threshold.** Nico and Veronica resolve cleanly as Spectacles 2 styles rather than countable generations.

Snap remains monitored for future generations, developer-access changes and any new consumer delivery milestone around SPECS.

## Best-fit use cases

AR prototyping, Lens Studio development, creator experimentation, spatial interaction research and historical study of the shift from camera glasses into wearable AR.

## Research questions

GlassesResearch tracks access models, camera/display evolution, interaction methods, Lens Studio capabilities, developer restrictions, account/cloud dependencies, owner control and the boundary between consumer and developer editions.
