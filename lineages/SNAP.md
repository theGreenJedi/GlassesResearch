# Snap Spectacles Lineage

This lineage covers Snap's Spectacles generations from consumer camera glasses through developer-oriented AR eyewear.

## Models in GlassesResearch

- GLS-0040 — Spectacles (1st Gen)
- GLS-0041 — Spectacles 2
- GLS-0042 — Spectacles 3
- GLS-0067 — Spectacles (2021, 4th Gen)
- GLS-0068 — Spectacles (2024, 5th Gen)
- GLS-0146 — SPECS (2026 consumer generation)

## Lineage analysis

The lineage shows a clear transition from consumer camera capture toward standalone augmented-reality development hardware and, with SPECS, back toward a publicly orderable consumer spatial computer. Its strengths are continuity of a single product family, a mature creator/developer environment around Lens Studio, and increasingly capable AR interaction. Its recurring tradeoff is physical: even the much lighter SPECS remains substantially heavier than ordinary glasses.

## Best-fit use cases

AR prototyping, Lens Studio development, creator experimentation, spatial interaction research, and historical study of the shift from camera glasses into wearable AR.

## Research questions

GlassesResearch tracks access models, camera/display evolution, interaction methods, Lens Studio capabilities, developer restrictions, and the boundary between consumer and developer editions.
