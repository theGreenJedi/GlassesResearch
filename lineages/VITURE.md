# VITURE XR-glasses lineage

VITURE is tracked as a **corporate personal-display/XR eyewear lineage** with multiple named hardware generations plus accessory compute/dock systems. GlassesResearch does not count neckbands, docks, adapters, collector finishes, or bundled host hardware as additional glasses unless evidence establishes distinct eyewear hardware.

**Relationship type:** corporate lineage with generation-specific optical/electronic branches  
**Confidence:** Confirmed

## Canonical eyewear population

- `GLS-0076` — VITURE One
- `GLS-0077` — VITURE One Lite
- `GLS-0078` — VITURE Pro
- `GLS-0079` — VITURE Luma
- `GLS-0080` — VITURE Luma Pro
- `GLS-0081` — VITURE Luma Ultra
- `GLS-0082` — VITURE Beast
- `GLS-0188` — VITURE Pro 2

## 2026 gap resolved — VITURE Pro 2

VITURE's first-party 5th Anniversary material and current store now sell **VITURE Pro 2** as a named eyewear product for $299. The company describes it as an evolved Pro generation with UltraClarity 3.0 optics, 50° FOV, 1600-nit claimed brightness, 0 to -5.0D built-in diopter adjustment, approximately 63 g weight, and a slimmer/lighter mechanical package than Pro.

The direct ordering route and generation-specific hardware claims establish a distinct purchasable identity, so Pro 2 is admitted as `GLS-0188` rather than being folded into VITURE Pro.

## Configuration boundary — Phantom Beast

VITURE also markets a **VITURE × Phantom Blade Zero “Phantom Beast”** collector edition. Current evidence supports a branded/themed Beast configuration with custom presentation, OSD/theme material and accessories. GlassesResearch therefore preserves it as a Beast variant/configuration rather than minting another GLS row unless a later hardware comparison establishes a material electronics/optics delta.

The same rule applies to accessory packs: Pro Neckband, Mobile Dock, Mobile Dock Mini, charging adapters and console/phone packs are system components or bundles, not additional glasses identities.

## Branch interpretation

VITURE's lineup evolves quickly and product naming combines generation, optical tier and bundle terminology. Hardware, display FOV, electrochromic behavior, diopter range, spatial features, host compatibility and firmware dependence must stay model-specific. A current store bundle offering several glasses choices is not evidence that those models share identical electronics.

## Completeness status

The family remains **audit in progress** after the Pro 2 admission. Remaining work includes:

1. reconstruct One-era regional/crowdfunding SKUs and determine whether every named edition is hardware-distinct;
2. map Pro, Luma and Beast generation boundaries against internal model/regulatory numbers;
3. resolve co-branded/collector editions such as Phantom Beast as hardware variants versus cosmetic configurations;
4. separate glasses firmware from SpaceWalker, neckband and dock software dependencies;
5. preserve historical purchase pages and pricing for discontinued generations;
6. monitor Helix industrial AI safety glasses without admitting them until the acquisition/delivery threshold is actually crossed.
