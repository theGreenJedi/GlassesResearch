# Everysight Maverick Lineage

**Lineage update:** 2026-09-07  
**Evidence packet:** [Competitor Completeness Sweep](../research/investigations/COMPETITOR_COMPLETENESS_SWEEP_2026-09-07.md)

Everysight's current Maverick family separates sport-focused commercial AR eyewear from the newer everyday AR + AI branch. GlassesResearch preserves those product identities separately while tracking their shared platform, developer surface and unresolved production boundaries.

## Canonical eyewear population

- `GLS-0147` — Maverick Sport — current retail
- `GLS-0221` — Maverick AI — paid crowdfunding/preorder
- `GLS-0222` — Maverick AI Pro — paid crowdfunding/preorder; adds native eye tracking / GazeIntent

## Why AI and AI Pro are separate

Everysight itself presents Maverick AI and Maverick AI Pro as distinct models. AI Pro adds native eye tracking and GazeIntent rather than merely changing a frame color or lens. Paid campaign/backer fulfillment evidence now establishes both as purchaser-history identities even though production fulfillment is still pending.

## Development ecosystem

Everysight publishes Maverick SDK material for iOS, Android and wearable-host workflows. Current documentation exposes content rendering and device capabilities while retaining an Everysight-controlled certificate/runtime-key path. A public SDK therefore raises Openness but does not automatically establish unrestricted Owner Control, firmware access or service independence.

Developer source: https://www.everysight.com/pages/developer

## Preorder / production boundary

Maverick AI and AI Pro remain development-stage products. A successful Kickstarter accepted paid backers, and Everysight support now maintains backer surveys, VIP/preorder payments and fulfillment guidance. Current support says production timing moved later in 2026. GR therefore records acquisition separately from shipment and does not treat prototype/preproduction specifications as hands-on production validation.

## Research priorities

- first production shipment and independent fulfillment confirmation;
- production-unit weight, optics, battery and thermal behavior;
- AI Pro eye-tracking calibration and practical GazeIntent behavior;
- runtime-certificate expiration and server-unavailable behavior;
- local/offline capability versus account/app/cloud dependencies;
- SDK approval policy and whether third-party apps remain functional without continuing Everysight authorization;
- repairability, firmware recovery, debug and bootloader surfaces.
