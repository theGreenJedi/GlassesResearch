# Solos Smart-Glasses Lineage

**Whole-manufacturer audit:** 2026-09-05  
**Audit packet:** [Manufacturer Completeness Wave 02](../research/investigations/MANUFACTURER_COMPLETENESS_WAVE_02_2026-09-05.md)

Solos has two materially different eyewear eras: an early **see-through sport-HUD branch** and the later **display-free AirGo branch**. The earlier site treatment began at AirGo 2 and therefore hid both the pre-AirGo history and the first AirGo generation.

## 1. Sport HUD branch

### `GLS-0214` — Solos Smart Glasses, first HUD generation

The original Solos/Kopin product crossed the purchaser-history threshold in 2016 through a $499 preorder path and later completed first-generation shipments. It used a pupil-scale see-through HUD for cycling/performance data and depended on paired-device/sensor inputs.

### `GLS-0215` — Solos Smart Glasses, second HUD generation

Kopin explicitly described the 2018 redesign as a **new generation**, announced Q1 availability at $499, and later reported that shipments had begun. It expanded the performance-glasses concept with revised audio, running support and communication functions.

These two generations remain separate from AirGo. Display presence, interaction model and sport-HUD architecture materially change the report-card questions.

## 2. AirGo branch

### `GLS-0216` — AirGo 1

The 2020 crowdfunding program and surviving 2021 manual establish the first AirGo generation as the architectural pivot to display-free Bluetooth smart glasses with interchangeable frames/temples and phone-hosted assistant/fitness functions.

Existing canonical generations:

- `GLS-0026` — AirGo 2
- `GLS-0027` — AirGo 3
- `GLS-0028` — AirGo Vision
- `GLS-0029` — AirGo V2

### `GLS-0217` — AirGo A5

Solos currently calls AirGo A5 its **5th Generation of Solos Audio AI Smartglasses** and offers it through a manufacturer retail/backorder path. That makes A5 a distinct named generation rather than a frame style.

## Architecture and naming discipline

AirGo generations may share companion-software, modular-frame and audio concepts, but camera availability, network paths, sensors, assistant behavior and cloud dependence vary materially. No generation inherits scores automatically from another.

Frame shapes, lens packages and interchangeable fronts/temples do not receive separate GLS identities unless the electronics platform itself changes and acquisition evidence establishes a separately marketed hardware generation.

## Completeness result

Wave 02 reconstructed Solos from its original HUD products through the current AirGo family. The audit adds four missing purchaser-history identities—two HUD generations, AirGo 1 and AirGo A5—while preserving the existing AirGo 2/3/Vision/V2 records.

Solos remains monitored because its naming has already shown that generation numbers, product-family names and retail frame names can diverge.

## Best-fit use cases

The HUD generations belong to sport/performance assisted-reality history. AirGo is better understood as conventional-looking audio/AI eyewear, with camera-assisted AI on supported generations and a recognizable developer/companion ecosystem.

## Research questions

GlassesResearch tracks SDK/API access, BLE and Wi-Fi transport, camera availability, sensors, battery, frame compatibility, local-versus-cloud AI behavior, and which software capabilities genuinely carry across AirGo generations.
