# Solos AirGo Lineage

This lineage covers Solos' AirGo smart-glasses generations.

## Models in GlassesResearch

- GLS-0026 — AirGo 2
- GLS-0027 — AirGo 3
- GLS-0028 — AirGo Vision
- GLS-0029 — AirGo V2

## Lineage analysis

The AirGo lineage spans audio-first smart glasses and later camera-capable generations while maintaining a recognizable developer ecosystem. Its strengths include conventional-eyewear styling, modular smart-glasses development resources, and a progression from audio interaction toward vision-assisted use cases. Its tradeoffs are that capabilities vary materially by generation, so compatibility and feature claims must be made model by model.

## Best-fit use cases

Audio/assistant interaction, camera-assisted AI on supported models, developer experimentation, and users seeking smart functions in a relatively conventional glasses form.

## Research questions

GlassesResearch tracks SDK/API access, BLE and Wi-Fi transport, camera availability, sensors, battery, frame compatibility, and which software capabilities carry across AirGo generations.
