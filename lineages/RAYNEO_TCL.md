# RayNeo / TCL / Thunderbird smart-glasses lineage

RayNeo is tracked as a **corporate/product lineage spanning TCL's NXTWEAR display eyewear, Thunderbird/RayNeo regional naming, the Air personal-display family, standalone X-series AR glasses, camera/audio V3, and the 2026 iO and GT families**. These branches must not inherit hardware, software, camera, AI, or owner-control claims from one another merely because the corporate relationship is established.

**Relationship type:** corporate lineage with branch-specific hardware/software relationships  
**Confidence:** Confirmed for the corporate/product continuity; model-to-model technical inheritance remains evidence-specific.

## 2026 availability reconciliation

GlassesResearch already verified RayNeo's August 21, 2026 announcement of **iO, GT and GT Max**, but correctly withheld canonical admission while the pages still described a future September 4 availability milestone. Rechecked September 5, RayNeo's first-party store now exposes all three with active purchase controls and prices. That crosses the purchasing-history threshold without changing their previously verified product identity.

RayNeo currently lists:
- **iO** as an in-stock display-equipped everyday smart-glasses product with direct checkout and prescription ordering;
- **GT** as a 46° FOV, 68 g host-driven display with native 3DoF;
- **GT Max** as a materially different 59° FOV, 76 g display with three precision IPD size classes and larger virtual-display modes.

GT and GT Max therefore remain separate marketed hardware identities rather than one model plus an accessory bundle.

## Naming boundary

TCL, Thunderbird Innovation and RayNeo naming has changed by market and generation. GlassesResearch preserves the real-world names and regulatory/model identifiers where supported rather than assuming that every TCL NXTWEAR or Thunderbird-branded product is an alias of a later RayNeo product.

The existing `GLS-0154` V3 record is the precedent: Thunderbird V3 / RayNeo V3 and model `XRGF50` are retained as nomenclature on one demonstrated product identity, not counted twice.

## Unresolved completeness work

This chapter establishes the corporate surface and reconciles the obvious current-store gap, but the family remains **audit in progress**. Highest-value remaining work:

1. reconstruct the China-market Thunderbird generations before and during the RayNeo rename;
2. map every NXTWEAR regional name and model number against Air-family hardware;
3. recover regulatory/manual identifiers for the GT family and iO;
4. separate Pocket TV / Pocket TV Pro and other compute accessories from eyewear identities;
5. confirm whether Batman and other co-branded Air editions contain any hardware delta beyond finish/accessories;
6. check retired/current regional stores for generations absent from the global catalog;
7. preserve SDK, ADB, firmware and companion-app boundaries separately for X, V3, iO and display-only branches.
