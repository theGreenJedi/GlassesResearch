# RayNeo / TCL / Thunderbird smart-glasses lineage

RayNeo is tracked as a **corporate/product lineage spanning TCL's NXTWEAR display eyewear, Thunderbird/RayNeo regional naming, the Air personal-display family, standalone X-series AR glasses, camera/audio V3, and the 2026 iO and GT families**. These branches must not inherit hardware, software, camera, AI, or owner-control claims from one another merely because the corporate relationship is established.

**Relationship type:** corporate lineage with branch-specific hardware/software relationships  
**Confidence:** Confirmed for the corporate/product continuity; model-to-model technical inheritance remains evidence-specific.

## Air-family historical reconciliation

The September 10, 2026 competitor-triggered forensic pass recovered two China-market generations that earlier whole-lineage work had missed.

### Air 1S — GLS-0241

RayNeo's October 20, 2022 first-party announcement explicitly calls Air 1S a **new generation** of consumer XR glasses and states that paid presales opened on JD and Tmall at CNY 2,499. The manufacturer documents changes from the original Air in the Sony MicroOLED generation, audio system, chassis/ergonomics, controls, 2D/3D switching and detachable magnetic cable. RayNeo's current browser firmware service still recognizes Air 1S as its own supported device identity.

Primary sources:
- https://www.rayneo.cn/xinwen/352.html
- https://ota.rayneo.com/

### Air Plus — GLS-0242

A surviving RayNeo product page preserves Air Plus as a separately named product, exposes a direct purchase route, and documents its own 49° / up-to-120 Hz product surface. RayNeo's current OTA service recognizes Air Plus separately from Air 1S and Air 2. TCL Electronics' 2023 interim filing independently records that the Group launched the upgraded Air Plus XR smart glasses in the PRC during the first half of 2023.

Primary/corporate sources:
- https://test.rayneo.cn/airplus/
- https://ota.rayneo.com/
- https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2023/0825/2023082500287.pdf

The Air Plus + RayNeo Pocket bundle does not create another eyewear identity; Pocket remains a compute/media accessory.

## 2026 availability reconciliation

GlassesResearch already verified RayNeo's August 21, 2026 announcement of **iO, GT and GT Max**, but correctly withheld canonical admission while the pages still described a future September 4 availability milestone. Rechecked September 5, RayNeo's first-party store exposed all three with active purchase controls and prices. That crossed the purchasing-history threshold without changing their previously verified product identity.

RayNeo currently lists:
- **iO** as a display-equipped everyday smart-glasses product with direct checkout and prescription ordering;
- **GT** as a host-driven display with native 3DoF;
- **GT Max** as a materially different larger-FOV display with three precision IPD size classes and larger virtual-display modes.

GT and GT Max therefore remain separate marketed hardware identities rather than one model plus an accessory bundle. Manufacturer display/fit specifications remain manufacturer claims until independently measured.

## Prototype history — X2 Lite

RayNeo publicly demonstrated **X2 Lite** at CES 2024. The recovered first-party evidence describes a demonstration/development milestone and future crowdfunding intent, not a completed paid acquisition route. X2 Lite therefore remains lineage/registry context rather than consuming a `GLS-` identifier.

Primary launch evidence: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/rayneo-demos-ultralight-x2-lite-ar-glasses-for-the-first-time-announcing-crowdfund-campaign-for-the-rayneo-x2-ar-glasses-at-ces-2024-302028818.html

## Naming boundary

TCL, Thunderbird Innovation and RayNeo naming has changed by market and generation. GlassesResearch preserves the real-world names and regulatory/model identifiers where supported rather than assuming that every TCL NXTWEAR or Thunderbird-branded product is an alias of a later RayNeo product.

The existing `GLS-0154` V3 record is the precedent: Thunderbird V3 / RayNeo V3 and model `XRGF50` are retained as nomenclature on one demonstrated product identity, not counted twice.

Air 1S and Air Plus demonstrate why the China-market history must be reconstructed rather than inferred from the later global naming sequence: both are manufacturer-recognized device identities and neither is safely reducible to a later Air number from naming alone.

## Unresolved completeness work

This chapter now closes the known Air 1S / Air Plus omissions, but the family remains **audit in progress**. Highest-value remaining work:

1. map every NXTWEAR regional name and hardware/model number against Air-family identities;
2. recover regulatory/manual identifiers for Air 1S, Air Plus, GT family and iO;
3. separate Pocket TV / Pocket TV Pro and other compute accessories from eyewear identities;
4. confirm whether Batman and other co-branded Air editions contain any hardware delta beyond finish/accessories;
5. check retired/current regional stores for further generations absent from the global catalog;
6. preserve SDK, ADB, firmware and companion-app boundaries separately for X, V3, iO and display-only branches;
7. retain X2 Lite as a prototype unless later historical evidence establishes an acquisition route.
