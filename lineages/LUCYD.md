# Lucyd / Innovative Eyewear smart-eyewear lineage

Lucyd, operated by Innovative Eyewear, has a longer commercial smart-eyewear history than the existing Lyte-era GlassesResearch catalog suggested. The lineage includes **pre-Lyte Loud generations, Lyte generations, safety eyewear, and licensed fashion/sport collections**. GlassesResearch separates electronics generations from frame styles and licensed branding so fashion breadth does not inflate the hardware-model count.

**Relationship type:** corporate lineage with generation-specific Bluetooth/audio hardware and licensed retail implementations  
**Confidence:** Confirmed for the corporate chronology; licensed-collection hardware boundaries remain under audit.

## Historical gap resolved — Lucyd Loud

A surviving first-party July 2020 corporate update reconstructs the pre-Lyte commercial sequence:

1. **Lucyd Loud 1.0** — Lucyd's first Bluetooth audio glasses; approximately 800 units were made and sold, mostly in 2018.
2. **Second edition of Lucyd Loud** — designed in-house and built in Q1 2019, offered in multiple colors/patterns; the smaller Slim style was later renamed Youth. The manufacturer described this edition as still available in July 2020.
3. **Loud 2020** — a Bluetooth 5.0 generation developed in seven styles and launched in January 2020.

These are named successive commercial electronics generations, not merely three frame colors, and therefore meet the canonical purchasing-history standard.

## Licensed-collection boundary

Innovative Eyewear has released smart eyewear under licensed names including Nautica, Eddie Bauer and Reebok. A licensed collection or frame style is **not automatically a new canonical model**. The audit asks whether the collection introduces a materially distinct electronics platform, controls, battery/audio system or manufacturer-defined generation.

Corsair already has its own canonical identity because it is a named purchasable model. Other licensed styles remain collection/platform evidence until hardware identity is resolved.

## Open questions

The current first-party corporate/press record also refers to a **Lucyd Lyte 2025 collection** launched in December 2024 and later compares 2026 hardware against that collection. That is a strong generation lead, but GlassesResearch is not assigning an ID until model-level launch/specification evidence cleanly separates the hardware generation from collection/year marketing.

The family remains **audit in progress** because the licensed catalogs are large and naming is frame-heavy.

Priority work:

1. recover product pages/manuals for Loud 1.0, second-edition Loud and Loud 2020;
2. establish exact electronics/model numbers across Lyte, Lyte 2.0, Lyte XL and the reported Lyte 2025 collection;
3. map Nautica, Eddie Bauer and Reebok models to underlying Lucyd electronics generations;
4. distinguish named frame models from true electronics generations;
5. preserve SEC/company records where retail pages have disappeared;
6. audit firmware/app behavior and service survival separately from standard Bluetooth audio residue.
