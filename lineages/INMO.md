# INMO smart-glasses lineage

INMO is tracked as a **corporate smart-eyewear lineage with at least three materially different branches**: standalone Air AR glasses, translation-first GO glasses, and the photography/AI-oriented X branch. Corporate continuity is established; hardware, software, display, camera and developer properties remain model-specific.

**Relationship type:** corporate lineage with branch-specific software/hardware relationships  
**Confidence:** Confirmed at product-family level; INMO X identity remains under acquisition-evidence review.

## Historical gap candidate — INMO X

INMO's own company-history page states that the company debuted an **INMO X-series 5G all-in-one AR glasses concept/product family in May 2021** and, more importantly for the purchasing-history threshold, that **INMO X series AI photo glasses were released in November 2024** alongside Air 3 and GO2. The same first-party history states that the first batch of blind orders exceeded 10,000 and lists **INMO X AI+Camera Glasses** under the company's products.

That is strong evidence of a real marketed identity and possible preorder/acquisition route, but GlassesResearch is retaining INMO X as an **active canonical-admission candidate** until a preserved order/product page or equivalent model-specific acquisition record resolves exactly which X generation/name crossed the purchase threshold. The audit does not turn a corporate timeline sentence into an invented stable model row.

## Architecture boundaries

Air generations are display-heavy standalone AR products. GO products emphasize discreet information/translation behavior. The X family is camera/photography oriented. These categories imply different privacy, visual-AI, display, cloud-dependence and owner-control questions, so scores and technical facts must not propagate across the corporate lineage.

INMO's developer/support material is unusually important because Air2, GO2 and Air3 expose different ADB/flashing/SDK paths. A public development path on one INMO generation is not proof of the same path on another.

## Completeness status

INMO remains **audit in progress**. Priority work:

1. recover the November 2024 INMO X order/product page and exact model nomenclature;
2. determine whether the 2021 X-series 5G device and the 2024 X AI+Camera product are one generation, successor generations, or unrelated use of the X name;
3. recover regional model numbers/regulatory identifiers for Air and GO generations;
4. map INMO app, ADB, flashing and SDK support by exact hardware generation;
5. distinguish preorder/bundle names from actual eyewear hardware identities;
6. check China-market products that may not appear on the current global store.
