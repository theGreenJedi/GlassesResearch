# Google Glass lineage

Google Glass is one of the foundational smart-glasses lineages and is tracked here as a **corporate/product-generation lineage** spanning the original Explorer program and the later enterprise generations.

## Canonical members

| ID | Product | First acquisition era | Role in lineage |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `GLS-0043` | Google Glass Explorer Edition | 2013 | Public paid Explorer program; established the original Glass hardware/software interaction model. |
| `GLS-0044` | Glass Enterprise Edition | 2017 | Enterprise-focused continuation of Glass through solution partners. |
| `GLS-0045` | Glass Enterprise Edition 2 | 2019 | Second enterprise generation; Android/AOSP-based assisted-reality wearable. |

## Evolution

The lineage moved from a highly visible early consumer/developer experiment toward a narrower enterprise assisted-reality platform. Enterprise Edition 2 used Qualcomm XR1 compute, Android Open Source Project 8.1, an 8 MP camera, 640×360 display, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0 and USB-C. Google stopped selling Glass Enterprise Edition on March 15, 2023 and ended software support on September 15, 2023; existing devices can continue to operate.

## Development and owner control

Glass Enterprise Edition 2 is especially relevant to GlassesResearch because Google documented standard Android development, ADB/fastboot access, APK installation, system images, and migration away from the older Glass Developer Kit toward Android APIs. It did not include Google Mobile Services or Google Play services.

That makes the later Glass lineage useful historically not only as wearable hardware, but as an example of an enterprise wearable with unusually conventional Android development and debugging paths.

## Why this lineage matters

Google Glass helped establish many of the questions that still define smart eyewear: social acceptability, camera privacy, glanceable displays, voice-first interaction, hands-free field work, prescription frames, application ecosystems, and the tension between consumer ambitions and enterprise utility.
