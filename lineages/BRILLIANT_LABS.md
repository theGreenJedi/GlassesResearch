# Brilliant Labs Lineage

This lineage covers Brilliant Labs' developer-oriented wearable-computing products.

## Models in GlassesResearch

- GLS-0050 — Monocle
- GLS-0051 — Frame
- GLS-0052 — Halo

## Lineage analysis

Brilliant Labs stands out for treating hardware and software openness as a primary product characteristic rather than an afterthought. The lineage spans compact developer hardware through increasingly glasses-like products while retaining a strong public development ecosystem.

## Strengths

Open-source resources, developer documentation, hardware/software experimentation, community projects, and relatively direct access to the platform.

## Tradeoffs

Developer-oriented products may require more technical involvement than mainstream consumer glasses, and product maturity, availability, and feature completeness can vary by generation.

## Best-fit use cases

Wearable-computing development, local-AI experiments, open hardware/software research, custom applications, and users who value inspectability and programmability.

## Research questions

GlassesResearch tracks architecture changes, firmware and SDK openness, on-device compute, display/audio evolution, community projects, and compatibility between generations.
