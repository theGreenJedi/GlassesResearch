# Connectivity Inventory Framework

A radio being present does not establish what an owner can do with it. Research should record connectivity at the hardware, supported-feature, and owner-access levels.

For each model, capture documented Bluetooth and Wi-Fi capabilities, wired connector and data capabilities, display-input standards, standard audio support, NFC or cellular capability, and manufacturer-specific transport where documented.

For every connection method distinguish hardware presence, manufacturer software support, public developer support, community-demonstrated use, and GlassesResearch-verified use. Also record whether normal setup depends on a manufacturer application or account and whether ordinary operating-system interfaces remain available.

Do not infer product functionality from component specifications alone. A component may support a feature that product software does not expose. These findings inform Openness, Owner Control, Cloud Independence, and Hackability.
