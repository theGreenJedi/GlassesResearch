# Eyewear Compatibility Framework

Smart glasses are eyewear as well as computers. Research should record whether a model can practically meet a wearer's vision needs and remain serviceable over time.

For each model, capture direct corrective-lens support versus an insert, documented correction range, single-vision and progressive support, available clear/tinted/photochromic options, replacement availability, regional service differences, and whether an ordinary optical shop can service the frame. Also record whether special blanks, carriers, geometry, displays, cameras, or calibration constrain replacement.

Use descriptive states: ordinary optical service, specialist optical service, insert based, vendor dependent, limited, not supported, or unknown.

Do not infer compatibility merely because lenses appear removable. Distinguish a vendor-offered option from independent serviceability. Preserve exact documented limits rather than reducing them to a yes/no field. Community modifications remain community evidence until reproduced.

These findings should inform Wearability, Value, Owner Control, and long-term survivability while preserving the underlying evidence.
