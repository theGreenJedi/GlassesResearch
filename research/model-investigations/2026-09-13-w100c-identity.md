# W100C identity resolution — 2026-09-13

**Status:** resolved for canonical catalog admission  
**Existing related identity:** `GLS-0157` — Shared W100 / Ear Dance OEM ecosystem, W100  
**Conclusion:** W100C is a distinct named OEM/retail model in the same Ear Dance family, not merely a searchable alias for W100.

## Why this investigation was reopened

GlassesResearch had already admitted W100 once as `GLS-0157`, but the exact `W100C` designation used by project-held specimens was not searchable in the canonical catalog. A fresh identity check found first-party OEM evidence that Goodway markets **W100** and **W100C** as separate models, alongside W100B and W100D, rather than using W100C as a seller nickname for W100.

## Identity evidence

### Goodway W100C — first-party OEM

Goodway's dedicated W100C page identifies the model as **W100C** and documents:

- AB5712F chipset;
- Bluetooth/BLE 6.0;
- IP67 rating;
- dual 85 mAh batteries, 170 mAh total;
- metal frame with TR temples;
- Ear Dance companion app;
- Hall-effect automatic power behavior;
- AI translation/dialogue and Bluetooth audio functions.

Source: https://www.goodwaytechs.com/goodway-w100c-ai-bluetooth-smart-glasses-for-real-time-translation.html

Goodway's current smart-glasses index lists W100C and W100 as separate named products, along with W100B and W100D.

Source: https://www.goodwaytechs.com/best-sell.html

### Goodway W100 — first-party OEM comparison

Goodway's W100 material identifies a related but separately named product using AB5712F while advertising Bluetooth 5.4, IP65, and a PC-frame construction. Those published differences are sufficient to prevent silently collapsing W100C into W100 while hardware equivalence remains unproven.

Source: https://www.goodwaytechs.com/products_list5.html

## Acquisition threshold

W100C has current commercial purchase evidence in addition to first-party OEM identity evidence. Multiple retailers expose W100C as an independently named product with a direct purchase path; GlassesResearch also has project-held specimens acquired under the W100C identity.

Commercial corroboration examples:

- https://oriwhiz.com/products/w100c-smart-glasses-support-ai-conversation-ai-intelligent-translation-bluetooth-call
- https://www.sunsky-online.com/p/SYA002860001A/W100C-Smart-Glasses-Support-AI-Conversation-AI-Intelligent-Translation-Bluetooth-Call-Black-.htm

This satisfies the purchaser-history requirement independently of the project's own specimens.

## Canonical decision

- Preserve `GLS-0157` as **W100**.
- Admit **W100C** separately as the next available stable GLS identity.
- Record `shares_oem_with / same Ear Dance family` relationship to W100 without inheriting W100 specifications, firmware behavior, or scores.
- Treat project-held W100C observations as W100C hands-on evidence, not W100 evidence.

## Evidence boundary

This resolution establishes that W100C is a separately named and separately sold model with published specification differences. It does **not** yet prove every internal component differs from W100, nor does it establish that all reseller products called W100C are electrically identical. NDI and bench work should continue to document the exact project-held specimens.
