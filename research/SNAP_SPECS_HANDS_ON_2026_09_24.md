- **Source:** UploadVR, independent hands-on
- **Source URL:** https://www.uploadvr.com/snap-specs-hands-on-minimum-viable-consumer-true-ar-glasses/
- **Evidence class:** independent firsthand observation
- **Affected model:** Snap SPECS
- **Use:** model dossier / future Report Card evidence; not a GlassesResearch lab measurement

## Observations worth preserving

UploadVR reports a strong separation between tracking quality and optical presentation.

The hands-on describes positional tracking, hand tracking and continuous scene meshing as robust during the tested session, including challenging low-light and crowded conditions. It also reports significant optical limitations: rainbow artifacts, uneven color, reflections from real-world lighting, and a visible field that can make virtual content appear clipped at the display boundary.

A particularly important evidence warning is that captured or mirrored output can show more rendered content than the wearer actually sees. Promotional or screen-captured footage therefore should not be treated as a faithful representation of the in-eye experience.

## GlassesResearch interpretation boundary

These are credible independent observations from a hands-on session, not instrumented optical measurements or long-duration ownership evidence. Preserve them as evidence for later Hardware, Wearability and Visual/Spatial evaluation without changing a score solely from this source.
