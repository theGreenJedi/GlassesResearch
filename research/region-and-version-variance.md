# Region and Version Variance Framework

A product name does not guarantee identical hardware, software, or services everywhere or forever.

Record region, hardware revision, firmware version, companion application version, and observation date whenever they materially affect a finding. Track regional availability, feature rollout differences, language support, regulatory variants, service availability, account restrictions, prescription options, and model-number differences when evidence supports them.

Do not silently overwrite an older verified behavior when an update changes it. Preserve dated states so the record can explain what a device did at a particular time. Likewise, do not assume a feature announced for one country or revision exists on all devices carrying the same marketing name.

When evidence conflicts, first test whether region, revision, firmware, or rollout date explains the disagreement before marking the underlying sources irreconcilable.
