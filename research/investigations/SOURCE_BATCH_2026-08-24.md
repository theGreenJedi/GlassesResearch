### INV-SB-005 — Human factors and long-duration visual load

- **Question:** Which display placements, virtual image distances, workloads, ages, prescriptions, and duty cycles predict fatigue or near-vision problems?

- **Alert trigger:** a new verified study or correction that materially changes safety, fitting, or testing guidance.

### INV-SB-006 — MIT platform engineering extraction

- **Evidence needed:** linked publications, bills of materials where public, sensor placement, weight/fit results, power architecture, data access, and licensing.
- **Alert trigger:** a newly published platform, open design artifact, dataset, or method with direct smart-eyewear consequence.
