# External Catalog Completeness — AR Compare Wave

**Date:** 2026-09-08  
**Question:** Which identities in AR Compare's 81-model public catalog are not already represented by GlassesResearch, and which of those differences are legitimate canonical omissions rather than taxonomy/scope differences?

## Method

AR Compare is used only as a discovery tripwire. Its public 81-model catalog was compared against GlassesResearch canonical identities, known aliases, adjacent wearables, and the 2026-09-07 competitor sweep. Every candidate below was then checked against independent manufacturer or commercial evidence before disposition. A competitor listing by itself is never sufficient for canonical admission.

## Confirmed already represented / intentionally different scope

The overwhelming majority of the 81 AR Compare entries are already represented by existing GLS identities, aliases, or prior reconciliation work. Known deliberate scope differences include Meta Fury (style/variant rather than separate electronics generation), Magic Leap 2 and Microsoft HoloLens 2 (adjacent headsets), Rokid Station 2 (non-eyewear host), and announced identities such as OPPO Air Glass 3 and Warby Parker intelligent eyewear that remain outside the purchaser-history count until acquisition evidence exists.

## Count impact

**No count change is made in this evidence packet.** It identifies products that appear admission-eligible, but GlassesResearch should mint stable IDs only in a dedicated reconciliation change that propagates each admitted identity through the canonical ledger, editorial profile layer, generated model pages, public dataset, and catalog consistency validation.

The existing `231` population remains the authoritative count until that propagation is complete. Stable IDs are not a contiguous-count proxy: the ledger currently reaches `GLS-0232` while still containing 231 active canonical purchaser-history rows.
