# Mentra — platform and Mentra Live research

**Evidence lane:** manufacturer-primary platform research  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-23

Mentra is relevant to GlassesResearch for two separate reasons that should not be collapsed: **MentraOS**, which the company describes as an open-source smart-glasses operating system, and **Mentra Live**, its own smart-glasses hardware.

## Current direction

Mentra's current company material says it initially expected developers and consumers to be the major market for Mentra Live, but enterprise pilots pulled the company toward frontline work in automotive, field service, manufacturing, logistics, construction, and pharma. The company now describes its applications around guided work, remote experts, automatic documentation, and AI assistance.

That makes Mentra useful evidence for the broader market split between general consumer AI eyewear and specialized work systems.

## Openness and interoperability

Mentra describes MentraOS as open source and as a control layer spanning hardware, software, AI, and data. GlassesResearch should test those claims at the artifact level rather than treating "open source" as a blanket conclusion.

Investigation priorities:

1. Map which MentraOS components are public and under what licenses.
2. Distinguish MentraOS portability across third-party glasses from Mentra Live-specific functionality.
3. Map sensor, display, audio, camera, notification, and AI APIs.
4. Identify account/cloud requirements and what remains functional without Mentra-operated services.
5. Establish firmware/update ownership and recovery boundaries for Mentra Live.
6. Track miniapp distribution and whether owner-built software can be sideloaded or self-hosted.
7. Preserve enterprise-management controls separately from ordinary owner control.

## Evidence boundary

Mentra's descriptions of deployments and capabilities are manufacturer claims until independently corroborated. An open-source OS does not automatically establish open firmware, open hardware, cloud independence, repairability, or unrestricted owner authority.
