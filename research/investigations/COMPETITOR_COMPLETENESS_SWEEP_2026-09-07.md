# Competitor Completeness Sweep — Smart Glasses Decoded + GlassBench

**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Scope:** use two specialist external sites as discovery leads, then independently resolve every apparent gap against manufacturer, platform, store, support, or other primary acquisition evidence before changing the canonical catalog.

## Method

Smart Glasses Decoded and GlassBench are discovery sources, not canonical authorities. A competitor-listed device is admitted only when GlassesResearch independently recovers evidence that it satisfies the purchaser-history boundary: public sale, paid preorder/reservation, or documented enterprise/developer procurement. Product styles, lens variants, bundles, prototypes, invite forms, and unsupported marketplace identities do not inflate the canonical count.

## Structural holes exposed by the sweep

### ActiveLook was a lineage-level blind spot

The missing ENGO3 record exposed a larger platform family. MicroOLED's ActiveLook is an open-API, BLE-driven Light AR platform shared across ENGO, Julbo, Cosmo and ActiveLook Enterprise eyewear. The platform relationship is more important than any one model and now warrants a dedicated lineage chapter.

### Compatibility knowledge existed but was not assembled

GR already records DisplayPort Alt Mode, host power and phone/app dependencies at model level, but Smart Glasses Decoded demonstrates the value of expressing those facts as a structured host-to-glasses compatibility matrix. The remedy is structured evidence, not SEO-targeted compatibility articles.

### Specialist teardown/component sources were underrepresented

GlassBench surfaced specialist near-eye-display sources such as Karl Guttag / KGOnTech. GR already preserves teardown evidence, but should durably watch specialist optics, waveguide, microdisplay, projection-engine and reference-design sources rather than waiting for finished-product coverage.

## Monitoring decision

Retain Smart Glasses Decoded and GlassBench as durable discovery watches. Their claims remain leads only. The daily discovery system should also retain the newly identified manufacturer/platform pages, Monako/Raven/Helix status pages, and specialist optics sources so a later status change is caught without weakening verification or publication standards.
