# Failure and Aging Framework

A useful device record should describe how products age, not only how they behave when new.

Track documented hinge problems, battery degradation, charging failures, lens or optical degradation, display defects, heat-related issues, microphone and camera failures, button/touch failures, connector wear, case failures, software abandonment, account failures, and loss of cloud-dependent functions.

For every reported problem, preserve source type, date, approximate device age when known, sample count, whether the failure was reproduced, and whether repair or replacement resolved it. One report is a report, not a failure rate.

Separate physical aging from service aging. A mechanically sound device can become degraded because an application, account server, certificate, API, or cloud feature disappears. Those events should also feed the discontinued-product and Cloud Independence records.

Do not calculate reliability percentages from informal reports unless the denominator and sampling method are credible. Prefer patterns described with explicit evidence and sample scope.
