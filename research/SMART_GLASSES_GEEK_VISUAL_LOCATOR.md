# Smart Glasses Geek visual-locator policy

Smart Glasses Geek may be used as a **visual locator/reference**, not as the publication source of record.

## Allowed use
- identify the exact glasses/model visually
- confirm that a candidate image appears to depict the intended model
- use the comparison page to reach or confirm the manufacturer/rightsholder website
- record the locator URL in the internal acquisition ledger

## Not allowed by this shortcut
- hotlink Smart Glasses Geek image files
- publish image bytes merely because they appear on Smart Glasses Geek
- treat Smart Glasses Geek as the rights holder unless it explicitly is
- replace manufacturer/source provenance with a third-party catalog citation

## Acquisition path
1. Locate the model on Smart Glasses Geek.
2. Confirm the model identity.
3. Continue to the manufacturer/rightsholder source already recorded in the canonical model ledger or independently verified from the locator page.
4. Preserve the selected original locally.
5. Record the manufacturer/rightsholder source, asset URL, credit, rights basis, retrieval date, and model-specific alt text.
6. Pass normal visual QA and publication validation.

The shortcut removes blind image hunting. It does not lower the publication gate.
