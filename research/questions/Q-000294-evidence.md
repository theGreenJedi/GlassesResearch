# Q-000294 — What does full-color mean in smart glasses, and which optical designs provide it?

**Original observed question:** “Anyone else paying attention to full-color smart glasses?” [Source observation](https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/comments/1xims16/anyone_else_paying_attention_to_fullcolor_smart/), preserved from the existing registry; reviewed 2026-10-31. The source establishes demand, not the truth of implied product claims.

## Claim-level evidence ledger

- **RayNeo X3 Pro specification:** [Source](https://www.rayneo.com/products/x3-pro-ai-display-glasses?variant=51473095033119) — Manufacturer claims full-color MicroLED, binocular diffractive waveguides, 640×480 and 30° FOV; not independent optical measurement.
- **XREAL One-series specification:** [Source](https://tutorials.xreal.com/docs/glasses/one-series/spec/) — Manufacturer lists XREAL 1S as a Sony Micro-OLED display, 3840×1200 binocular, 52° FOV; a video-display path, not equivalent to the X3 Pro's see-through HUD.
- **Even G2 specification:** [Source](https://support.evenrealities.com/hc/en-us/articles/13499229138959-Specifications) — Manufacturer explicitly lists a green binocular waveguide, illustrating monochrome versus full color.
- **Community question:** [Original source](https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/comments/1xims16/anyone_else_paying_attention_to_fullcolor_smart/) — not independent product verification; original wording and observation remain in registry.
