# Q-000136 — Pixelated lens versus focused virtual image

**Question origin:** [Electrical Engineering Stack Exchange, February 9, 2022](https://electronics.stackexchange.com/questions/607852/text-projection-in-smart-glasses). The actual question asks whether attenuation-only controllable pixels in ordinary eyeglass lenses could make text appear 10 meters distant, or whether wavefront/phase control is necessary. The title is an abbreviated topic, not an answer.

**Independent technical source:** [Nature Reviews Electrical Engineering, Microdisplay technologies in augmented reality and virtual reality headsets, published August 13, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44287-025-00199-x). Describes microdisplay light engines integrated with free-space, freeform, and waveguide combiners. Supports that virtual-image optics require more than a transparency mask.

**Qualified conclusion:** Dimming-only pixels near the eye do not by themselves produce a sharp virtual image at a remote focal distance. This does not imply actively controlling optical phase is the only path; refractive and reflective optics can also form images.
