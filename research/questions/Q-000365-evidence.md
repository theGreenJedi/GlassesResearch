# Q-000365 — Why do caption glasses cause so much eye strain?

**Original observed question:** Why do caption glasses cause so much eye strain?

**Original observation:** [Community discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/comments/1xrqjc2/why_do_caption_glasses_cause_so_much_eye_strain/) observed **2026-11-09** via `reddit_rss`. The observation is a question, not a clinically verified symptom or product defect. Keep the original registry observation and ID unchanged.

## Claim-level provenance

| Claim | Evidence | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sustained digital-display use can cause dry or uncomfortable eyes; breaks, blinking and brightness adjustments may help | [American Academy of Ophthalmology](https://www.aao.org/eye-health/diseases/computer-usage) | General screen guidance, not a controlled caption-glasses trial |
| Vergence–accommodation conflicts can contribute to discomfort in stereoscopic displays | [Peer-reviewed experimental study](https://doi.org/10.1167/11.8.11) | Cannot generalize to monocular or fixed-depth caption glasses or diagnose an individual |
| Head-mounted displays present multiple possible perceptual/optical discomfort factors | [Human Factors literature review](https://doi.org/10.1518/001872006778606877) | Review-level mechanisms; no device-specific measurements |
| Blue light from digital screens is not established as the cause of eye strain | [AAO blue-light guidance](https://www.aao.org/eye-health/tips-prevention/should-you-be-worried-about-blue-light) | Does not rule out individual light sensitivity or clinical conditions |
| Checking fit, prescription, brightness, breaks and symptom patterns is a prudent troubleshooting approach | Practical inference from sources above | Not an established treatment protocol or wearer study |
