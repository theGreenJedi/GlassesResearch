# Q-000172 — Can smart glasses display the live camera view from my phone?

**Original observed wording:** “Are there smart glasses that can display my phone's camera feed?” [Original community question](https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/comments/1xdg4df/are_there_smart_glasses_that_can_display_my/), observed 2026-10-23. Reviewed 2026-10-31.

## Claim-level provenance

- **Google primary support:** [Pixel screen projection](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/2865484) describes **Pixel 8 and later** wired external-display mirroring and the required on-phone “Mirror display” prompt. It documents screen output, **not** every camera-app preview.
- **XREAL primary instructions:** [XREAL One series connection](https://tutorials.xreal.com/docs/glasses/one-series/first-use/connect-device/) requires USB-C DisplayPort video output and 5V/1A from a compatible host; it is a video-display path, not a general BLE notification HUD feature.
- **Reasoned conditional workflow:** open the phone camera preview, enable screen mirroring, then check that the particular app actually renders on the glasses at usable latency and orientation. This is an engineering test recommendation, **not** a verified working phone/glasses combination.
- **Original observation:** [Community request](https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/comments/1xdg4df/are_there_smart_glasses_that_can_display_my/) is the demand source, not proof of a product's compatibility.
