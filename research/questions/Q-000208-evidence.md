# Q-000208 — Does Dymesty offer mind maps from meetings, and is that verified to work well?

**Original observed wording:** “Anyone use dymesty’s mind map feature for work? How is it?” [Original community question](https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/comments/1xem0tu/anyone_use_dymestys_mind_map_feature_for_work_how/), observed 2026-10-25. Reviewed 2026-10-31.

## Claim-level provenance

- **Developer release-note statement:** [Apple's Dymesty App Store history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dymesty/id6745878367) lists **iOS v2.3.8, July 17, 2026**, with an added mind-map feature. This supports a developer-advertised feature, not independently tested accuracy, output structure, retention, current access or Android parity.
- **Developer product description:** [Dymesty on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dymesty.dymestyandroid) describes meeting transcription, speaker identification, summaries and action items; that does **not** independently confirm the iOS mind-map feature on Android.
- **Weakness of public experience evidence:** App Store public rating count is sparse; one listing review is not a representative reliability study. The [original question](https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/comments/1xem0tu/anyone_use_dymestys_mind_map_feature_for_work_how/) asks for experience, not a verified feature benchmark.
- **Recommended independent test, not performed:** consented meeting, compare output to transcript for omitted decisions, wrong attribution, dates, terminology, export and privacy controls. No claim that Dymesty passes.
