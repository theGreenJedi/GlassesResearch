# Q-000284 — Can UK buyers get lenses fitted into frames bought separately?

**Original observed question:** Has anyone ever bought frames and lenses separately in the UK?

**Original observation:** https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/comments/1xh768j/has_anyone_ever_bought_frames_and_lenses/ (observed 2026-10-28; reviewed October 30, 2026). The observation is a question, not verified performance testimony.

- **UK patient choice for where to have a prescription dispensed; no guarantee of any practice accepting a third-party electronic frame.** Source: https://www.college-optometrists.org/clinical-guidance/guidance/knowledge%2C-skills-and-performance/sale-and-supply-of-spectacles/taking-the-prescription-elsewhere
