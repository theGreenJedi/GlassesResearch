# Q-000179 — Is a slim fold-over glasses case protective enough for electronic smart glasses?

**Original observed wording:** “Slim fold-over glasses case vs a proper hard shell, what do you actually carry?” [Original community poll](https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/comments/1xe2jgh/slim_foldover_glasses_case_vs_a_proper_hard_shell/), observed 2026-10-24. Reviewed 2026-10-31.

## Claim-level provenance

- **Manufacturer charging requirement:** [Even G2 charging instructions](https://support.evenrealities.com/hc/en-us/articles/13754885105295-How-to-Charge) document its dedicated case and correct contact alignment. They do **not** provide independent case-impact ratings.
- **Manufacturer charging requirement:** [Ray-Ban Meta FAQ](https://www.ray-ban.com/usa/c/frequently-asked-questions-ray-ban-meta-smart-glasses) says glasses charge only in the charging case. This is a model-specific example, not a universal claim about all smart glasses.
- **Engineering inference, clearly bounded:** a rigid shell resists compression better than a thin pouch when packed under other objects, but no standardized drop/compression tests of particular cases were performed. Neither manufacturer's charging instructions prove impact protection.
- **Original observation:** [Reddit poll](https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/comments/1xe2jgh/slim_foldover_glasses_case_vs_a_proper_hard_shell/) asks about carrying preferences; it is not a representative survey.

## Limits and publication gate
