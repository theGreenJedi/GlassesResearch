# Q-000158 — What is using Even G2 smart glasses every day likely to involve?

**Original observed wording:** “How are G2 Day to Day?” [Original community question](https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/comments/1x390fl/how_are_g2_day_to_day/), observed 2026-10-21. This is an experience request, **not** an independent product test. Reviewed 2026-10-31.

## Claim-level provenance

- **Manufacturer specification:** [Even G2 consumer specifications](https://support.evenrealities.com/hc/en-us/articles/13499229138959-Specifications) describe a binocular green waveguide, 640×350 resolution, 27.5° field of view, four microphones, 192mAh eyewear battery, and BLE 5.4. Vendor claims, not GlassesResearch measurements.
- **Developer primary documentation:** [Even Hub app-submission overview](https://hub.evenrealities.com/docs/reference/app-submission) describes no camera or speaker, phone-hosted plugins, four microphones, and a **576×288 per-eye** render path with **BLE 5.2**. Its display resolution and BLE revision conflict with the consumer spec page; no independent resolution of the discrepancy. It is possible they refer to different paths or revisions, but that is hypothesis, not fact.
- **Manufacturer service dependence:** [Even Conversate support](https://support.evenrealities.com/hc/en-us/articles/14273795154319-Conversate) describes phone/internet dependence for the named function. Do not generalize this to every G2 feature.
- **Original observation:** [Reddit owner-experience request](https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/comments/1x390fl/how_are_g2_day_to_day/) supplies the question, not verified day-to-day satisfaction.
