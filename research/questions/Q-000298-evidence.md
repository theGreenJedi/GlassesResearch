# Q-000298 — What does Brilliant Labs Halo actually offer to developers and buyers?

## Claim-level provenance

- **Official Halo hardware documentation lists color panel, 256x256 app drawable area, camera, microphones, speakers, CPU/NPU, BLE and battery; panel pixels must not be conflated with app canvas.** Source: https://github.com/brilliantlabsAR/docs/blob/main/halo/hardware.md
- **Official public Halo firmware repository documents Zephyr, Lua runtime and BLE OTA.** Source: https://github.com/brilliantlabsAR/halo-firmware
- **Official Noa assistant repository exists, but its publication does not establish all cloud, CAD and boot interfaces as open.** Source: https://github.com/brilliantlabsAR/noa-assistant
