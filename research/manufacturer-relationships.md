# Manufacturer Relationship Framework

GlassesResearch should distinguish the companies involved in bringing a smart-glasses product to market instead of treating the consumer-facing brand as the entire lineage.

For each model, record documented roles such as brand, manufacturer, design partner, assembler, optical supplier, display supplier, chipset vendor, software platform, distributor, and regulatory applicant.

Relationships should use clear labels such as manufactured by, designed by, assembled by, branded by, supplied by, distributed by, or uses platform from.

Each relationship should carry a source, confidence level, and verification date. Visual similarity or copied marketing language can be a research lead, but should not by itself establish that two products are the same device.

When multiple brands sell closely related hardware, preserve each commercial identity as well as the shared lineage. Regional versions, firmware differences, applications, accessories, and support arrangements may differ even when the physical platform is closely related.

The eventual manufacturer map should allow researchers to move from a device to the documented companies behind it, and from a company to the products and roles associated with it. Unknown relationships should remain unknown until evidence supports them.
