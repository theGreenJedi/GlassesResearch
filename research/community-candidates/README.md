# Community candidate intake

This directory is the durable handoff for public smart-glasses and wearable-computing community discoveries.

Candidates use the same normalized candidate contract as news/discovery intake and are consumed by `scripts/triage_news_reviews.py`. Every candidate must retain its direct public source URL and source/community type. Private or closed Discord/Facebook material must not be represented as scanned evidence.

Provenance controls delegated action:

- primary regulatory/manufacturer/developer artifacts may become attributed evidence;
- community reports may become explicitly labeled community signals;
- independent/secondary reporting remains review-gated unless another policy grants bounded authority;
- no intake candidate can authorize a GlassesResearch conclusion, lineage resolution, Report Card score, or canonical identity by itself.

Community scour findings therefore enter the same durable chain:

**discover → community candidate → classify → triage → delegated evidence actuator → repository evidence**

Equivalent URLs deduplicate at triage rather than generating repeated findings.
