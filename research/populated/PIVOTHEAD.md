# Pivothead — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/LINEAGE_PIVOTHEAD.md` (2026-08-12) and cited commercial/archival sources.

## Lineage
Two principal hardware generations: first-generation Pivothead video-recording eyewear and later Pivothead SMART / Architect Edition. Style names such as Recon, Aurora, Durango and Moab remain frame/style variants unless archival evidence proves different electronics.

## Generation 1
Commercial camera sunglasses with documented 8 MP Sony CMOS sensor, 1080p30 / 720p60 recording, 8 GB internal storage, microphone, micro-USB, 440 mAh battery and desktop/mobile configuration. Contemporary evidence records ~75 g weight and ~$349 retail pricing.

These are camera glasses, not HUD glasses. Their historical importance is local point-of-view capture built directly into socially wearable eyewear.

Report-card anchor: H5.5 W7.0 VAI N/A S5.0 O3.5 OC7.5 CI9.0 Hack4.0 HUD N/A V7.0.

## Generation 2 — SMART / Architect Edition
Commercial listing establishes a distinct later platform with 1080p capture, touch/swipe control, LED/audio feedback, swappable/prescription-compatible lenses, optional streaming accessories and a more explicit modular/developer story. Current evidence is insufficient to inherit first-generation internals across all fields.

Current anchors: W7.0, VAI N/A, S6.0, O5.0, OC7.0, CI8.0, Hack5.5, HUD N/A; Hardware and Value not yet graded.

## Ownership interpretation
Local capture/storage and direct file access give the first generation strong cloud independence and owner control. SMART adds more connectivity and development potential but also more service/accessory complexity.
