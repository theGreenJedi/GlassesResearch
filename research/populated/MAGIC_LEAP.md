# Magic Leap — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/LINEAGE_MAGIC_LEAP.md` (2026-08-12) and its cited Magic Leap primary sources.

## Taxonomy
Magic Leap belongs on the adjacent head-worn AR shelf, not in the ordinary smart-glasses count. The commercial lineage separates first-generation Magic Leap One / Magic Leap 1 from Magic Leap 2.

## Magic Leap One / Magic Leap 1
Authorized purchases ended 2022-12-31. Support, OS updates and cloud services ended 2024-12-31; Magic Leap states that core functionality and apps reached end-of-life. Surviving primary archival material was insufficient in the research packet to reconstruct full hardware/software grades without guesswork.

Current anchors: Wearability2.5, Owner Control4.0, Cloud Independence3.0; other dimensions remain not yet graded where primary archival evidence is incomplete. The important preservation fact is direct evidence that vendor-service shutdown materially reduced long-term ownership value.

## Magic Leap 2
Distinct second-generation enterprise AR system with headset, compute pack and controller. Existing research documents 16 GB LPDDR5, 256 GB NVMe, dedicated CV/ML compute, Wi-Fi 6, up to 3.5 hours battery life, 6DoF controller, hand tracking, eye tracking, voice, on-device spatial mapping and Dynamic Dimming.

The OS is AOSP-based and the platform exposes SDKs, OpenXR, Unity/Unreal/native workflows, samples and supported OS flashing. This creates substantial developer and owner control despite proprietary hardware/platform boundaries.

Direct sales ended 2026-03-31; authorized reseller delivery may continue through 2026-12-31, so the platform is commercially winding down rather than unreleased.

Report-card anchor: H9.0 W3.0 VAI8.0 S9.0 O7.5 OC8.0 CI8.5 Hack8.0 HUD9.5; Value not yet graded.

## Lifecycle interpretation
The two generations are a useful cloud-survival contrast: first-generation shutdown is documented as materially destructive, while Magic Leap 2 supports substantial local/on-device operation. Do not infer that second-generation survivability guarantees future service continuity.
