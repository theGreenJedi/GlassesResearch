# Lenovo ThinkReality A6 — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/LINEAGE_LENOVO_THINKREALITY_A6.md` (2026-08-12) and cited Lenovo primary sources.

## Identity
ThinkReality A6 is a distinct commercial generation, separate from later A3 hardware. Lenovo launch/support records establish limited Q3 2019 release, regional purchasing part numbers, manuals, service parts and production support.

## Hardware
Existing research documents Snapdragon 845, Intel Movidius vision processing, Lumus binocular waveguides, 1080p per eye, >40° diagonal FOV, SLAM, 13 MP RGB camera, depth sensing, 6DoF inside-out tracking, gesture/voice/object recognition and an external Android compute box. Headset mass is under 380 g and prescription-eyeglass wearers are supported.

## Software / ownership
Lenovo published Android open-source code and a developer platform. Managed deployments also used Lenovo's ThinkReality Cloud Portal for registration and adding applications/content, and deeper documentation was access-controlled. This is meaningful developer access with real vendor-management dependence rather than fully open ownership.
