## Generation notes
Alto/Rondo are ~45 g Bluetooth audio sunglasses with up to ~3.5 hours playback, interchangeable lenses and the historical Bose AR motion-sensor concept using head orientation plus the paired phone's location. Tempo/Tenor/Soprano improved sound, microphones, controls and battery; Tenor is documented around 49.9 g with Bluetooth 5.1, beamforming microphones, touch/buttons, IPX2 and up to 5.5 hours battery.

## Ownership interpretation
Core Bluetooth playback/calls remain useful without a Bose cloud. Historical Bose AR created a limited developer-facing sensor experience but did not open firmware/hardware. Later generations became more polished consumer audio products and less developer-oriented, explaining lower openness/hackability despite improved hardware.
