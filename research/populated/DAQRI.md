# DAQRI Professional AR — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/LINEAGE_DAQRI_PROFESSIONAL_AR.md` (2026-08-12) and cited DAQRI/Flex primary or partner sources.

## Family boundary
DAQRI spans multiple HCI forms. Smart Helmet is adjacent industrial head-worn AR, Smart Glasses are the canonical glasses product, and Smart HUD is a non-wearable/vehicle display platform. Shared software does not make them one model type.

## DAQRI Smart Glasses
Shipped worldwide to customers beginning 2017-11-07 with a documented $4,995 direct/channel purchase route. Existing research documents 6th-gen Intel Core m7 compute, Intel RealSense LR200 depth camera, ~44° diagonal FOV, transparent stereoscopic displays, tracking camera, HD color camera, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, SLAM/sparse mapping, remote-expert workflows, guided work and 3D-content visualization.

Report-card anchor: H8.0 W4.5 VAI4.0 S7.5 O6.0 OC7.0 CI8.0 Hack6.0 HUD8.0 V4.0.

## DAQRI Smart Helmet
Earlier production-ready industrial AR helmet with Intel Core m7-6Y75, RealSense depth sensing, wide-angle tracking, RGB and thermal imaging, Wi-Fi, integrated audio, see-through AR display and an API/developer-tool story. It remains adjacent head-worn industrial AR, not part of the smart-glasses count.

Report-card anchor: H8.5 W2.5 VAI5.0 S7.5 O6.5 OC7.0 CI8.0 Hack6.5 HUD8.0 V3.0.
