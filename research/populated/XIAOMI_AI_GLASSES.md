# Xiaomi AI Glasses — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/HIGH_THROUGHPUT_BATCH_01.md` and Xiaomi primary product material cited there.

## Hardware
The completed batch research documents a 40 g frame, 12 MP Sony IMX681 camera, dual speakers, five-microphone array, Snapdragon AR1 plus a low-power Vela OS chip, 263 mAh battery and up to 8.6 hours use. Electrochromic options are documented in the product family.

## AI and software
Multimodal AI, livestreaming, translation and transcription are first-class product functions. Camera input participates directly in object/scene-oriented AI workflows rather than existing only for media capture.

Report-card anchor: H8.5 W8.5 VAI9.0 S8.0 O3.0 OC3.5 CI4.5 Hack3.0 HUD N/A; Value not yet graded.

## Ownership interpretation
Hardware integration is strong, but the current evidence set does not establish a broad public SDK, replaceable assistant/model endpoint, firmware access or owner-selectable local-AI stack. Headline AI functions are materially service-connected even though capture/storage and some device processing are local.

## Evidence gaps
Exact local-vs-cloud processing boundaries, account/companion requirements, regional feature variance, prescription/lens service, repairability, battery aging, BLE/Wi-Fi developer exposure, firmware/update policy and long-term service survival require deeper evidence.
