# Recon Jet — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/LINEAGE_RECON_JET.md` (2026-08-12) and its cited Recon/Intel/teardown sources.

## Lineage
Recon Jet is treated as Jet → Jet Pro → Jet Pro+. Snow2/MOD Live belong to an earlier HUD branch and are not silently merged into Jet.

## Recon Jet
Shipped sport-focused monocular wearable computer. Existing research documents Android-based software, a below-right-eye high-contrast HUD, POV camera, smartphone connectivity, ANT+, GPS/activity metrics, caller/text display, Recon Engage services, and a public SDK/open-platform positioning.

Owner-control evidence is strong for its era because third-party apps and external sensors were supported. Long-term cloud independence is materially weakened by Recon Engage shutdown and activation/login dependence after Intel discontinued the ecosystem.

Report-card anchor: H7.0 W5.5 VAI N/A S7.5 O7.5 OC8.0 CI4.5 Hack7.0 HUD6.5 V7.5.

## Jet Pro
Intel explicitly recognizes Jet Pro as a shipped customer product. Existing teardown evidence records model RI-JET, 85.3 g and a ~$991.99 acquisition price. Generation-specific technical evidence is insufficient to inherit Jet hardware/software scores. Current anchor: CI4.0, Value5.5; other fields not yet graded, Visual AI N/A.

## Jet Pro+
Intel separately names Jet Pro+ as a discontinued customer product. Admission is evidence-backed; hardware inheritance from Jet Pro is not. Current anchor: CI4.0; most other fields not yet graded, Visual AI N/A.

## Lifecycle
The lineage is historically important because it demonstrates a real shipped SDK-capable wearable whose vendor service shutdown later impaired ownership. Preserve both facts: strong original developer access and poor present-day service survivability.

## Evidence gaps
Recover archival product sheets/manuals for Pro and Pro+, determine local functionality after service loss, document battery/repair/parts survivability, and separate activation dependence from functions that still operate offline.
