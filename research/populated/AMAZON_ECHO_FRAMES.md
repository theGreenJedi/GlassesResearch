## Lineage
Echo Frames Gen 1 → Gen 2 → Gen 3, plus Carrera Smart Glasses using Amazon's 2023 smart-audio architecture. All are screenless, camera-free smart-audio eyewear built around phone connectivity and Alexa.

## Generation notes
Gen 1 began as a Day 1 Editions prescription-ready Alexa/audio product with no camera/display. Gen 2 matured materials, battery and retail availability while keeping the same closed architecture. Gen 3 introduced seven Echo/Carrera styles, redesigned audio with materially stronger bass, improved speech recognition, IPX4, multipoint pairing, wireless charging, up to six hours continuous media/talk and up to fourteen hours moderate use. Carrera Cruiser/Sprinter are fashion/frame implementations of the same 2023 architecture rather than an unrelated compute platform.

## Ownership interpretation
Ordinary Bluetooth audio gives some local utility, but Alexa is the defining intelligence layer and is materially service-dependent. Consumer settings and microphone controls do not amount to replaceable assistant/platform ownership. No broad public device SDK, open firmware or low-level protocol path is established.

## N/A rule
Visual AI and Display/HUD are genuinely N/A because the architecture intentionally has neither outward-facing camera nor wearer display. Those omissions are not negative scores; openness, owner control and cloud dependence are still graded normally.

## Evidence gaps
Historical/current value, Bluetooth profile detail, firmware/update survival, functionality after Alexa/service loss, battery aging/replacement, prescription service pathways, repairability and remaining support horizon.
