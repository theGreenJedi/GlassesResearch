Source basis: `docs/report-cards/HIGH_THROUGHPUT_BATCH_01.md` and its Huawei primary sources.

## Lineage scope
This pass covers the HUAWEI × GENTLE MONSTER Eyewear first generation and Eyewear II as audio-first smart-eyewear generations. Do not infer camera, HUD, or visual-AI capability from later Huawei wearable products.

## HUAWEI × GENTLE MONSTER Eyewear
Early fashion-first open-ear audio eyewear with microphones/controls. Primary specification depth in the current packet is thinner than for the successor, so unsupported technical detail remains unknown.

Report-card anchor: H5.5 W8.5 VAI N/A S4.5 O2.5 OC4.0 CI8.5 Hack3.0 HUD N/A; Value not yet graded.

## Eyewear II
Huawei documents 44–48 g variants, Bluetooth 5.2, dual dynamic drivers, dual microphones, 85 mAh battery, up to five hours playback and IP54. The product remains audio-first with no outward-facing camera or wearer display established in this packet.

Report-card anchor: H6.5 W8.5 VAI N/A S5.5 O3.0 OC4.0 CI9.0 Hack3.0 HUD N/A; Value not yet graded.

## Ownership interpretation
Core audio/call behavior is fundamentally Bluetooth-local, giving both generations relatively strong cloud independence. AI Life integration adds vendor software convenience but does not make core audio dependent on a Huawei cloud. No meaningful public SDK, firmware-replacement route or low-level hardware interface is established.
