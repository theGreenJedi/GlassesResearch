## Lineage scope
This record covers HUAWEI × GENTLE MONSTER Eyewear, Eyewear II, and Huawei Eyewear 2 as audio-first smart-eyewear generations. Do not infer camera, HUD, or visual-AI capability from unrelated Huawei wearables.

## HUAWEI × GENTLE MONSTER Eyewear
Early fashion-first open-ear audio eyewear with microphones/controls. Primary specification depth remains thinner than later generations.

## Huawei Eyewear 2 — GLS-0021
2023 region-limited/current generation. Huawei documents multiple optical/sunglass styles, ~36.6–43 g depending on frame/lenses, dual open-ear drivers, call noise reduction, touch controls, wear detection, Bluetooth 5.3, dual-device connection, IP54, accelerometer/gyroscope/capacitive sensing, 110 mAh per temple, up to 11 hours music, nine hours calling and ~50-minute full charging. No camera or display.

## Ownership interpretation
Core audio/call behavior is Bluetooth-local across the family, making cloud independence consistently strong. AI Life/gesture software adds convenience but does not turn core playback into a vendor-cloud function. No broad public SDK, firmware replacement or low-level owner interface is established.

## N/A rule
Visual AI and HUD remain N/A for these documented audio-only generations.

## Evidence gaps
Regional availability/value, exact Bluetooth codecs/profiles, firmware/update behavior, independent optical/prescription service, battery replacement/aging, repairability and community reverse-engineering.
