## Lineage scope
The completed packet covers INMO Air, Air 2, GO and Air 3 as distinct generations/product branches inside the INMO wearable-display ecosystem. Generation-specific claims remain separated.

## INMO Air — GLS-0057
Delivered in April 2022 and described by INMO as an early mass-produced wireless all-in-one AR device. Standalone wireless AR architecture is established, but complete first-generation specifications remain an archival target.

## INMO GO — GLS-0059
Wireless translation-focused HUD line with real-time on-screen translation, hands-free operation and phone connectivity. Current support preserves the feature set but not all original hardware details. No wearer-view camera is established for original GO.

## INMO Air 3 — GLS-0060
Current commercial all-in-one AR generation. INMO documents 1920×1080 binocular Sony Micro-OLED waveguides, 36° FoV, up to 600 nits, 120 Hz, Snapdragon 8-core compute, 8 GB RAM, 128 GB storage, 16 MP 120° camera with EIS, microphones/speakers, sensors, 660 mAh battery, Android app compatibility, developer mode and SDK/API support.

Report-card anchor: H9.0 W6.5 VAI8.5 S8.5 O8.0 OC8.5 CI8.0 Hack8.0 HUD9.0 V7.5. Price basis: $1,099, checked 2026-08-12 in the completed batch.

## Ownership interpretation
INMO's standalone Air generations offer materially more local autonomy than pure phone-display peripherals. Air 3's developer mode and SDK/API support are strong owner/developer signals, but they do not automatically establish open firmware, bootloader access or unrestricted sensor control. GO is a different, translation-first architecture and should not inherit Air camera/compute claims.
