# OrCam MyEye — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/LINEAGE_ORCAM_MYEYE.md` (2026-08-12) and cited OrCam primary sources.

## Lineage
Assistive wearable-camera modules that attach to ordinary eyeglass frames. Strongest evidence supports MyEye 2 / MyEye 2 Pro and MyEye 3 Pro. Earlier first-generation hardware remains archival work pending better model-specific specifications.

## MyEye 2 Pro
Existing research documents a ~22.5 g eyeglass-mounted device focused on text reading and recognition of faces, products, colors and currency. Current source packet lists ~$4,250 pricing and ~1.5–2 hours active use. Core recognition is repeatedly described as local/offline, an unusually strong cloud-independence attribute for camera-AI wearables.

Report-card anchor: H7.0 W9.0 VAI8.0 S8.0 O2.0 OC6.5 CI9.5 Hack2.0 HUD N/A V4.0.

## MyEye 3 Pro
Successor retaining eyeglass-mounted form while adding Smart Magnifier and an interactive AI assistant. Existing pricing: $4,490 list / $4,250 promotional in the reviewed source. Core recognition remains device-native, while the interactive assistant can introduce optional network/external-data dependence.

Report-card anchor: H7.5 W9.0 VAI8.5 S8.5 O2.0 OC6.5 CI8.5 Hack2.0 HUD N/A V4.0.

## Ownership interpretation
These products score highly for wearability and practical offline visual assistance, but low for openness/hackability because no meaningful public SDK, firmware source or replaceable-AI pathway was established.

## Evidence gaps
Recover first-generation MyEye archives; document repairability, battery replacement, companion/account requirements, endpoint control, prescription/frame compatibility and exact offline/online feature boundaries for MyEye 3.
