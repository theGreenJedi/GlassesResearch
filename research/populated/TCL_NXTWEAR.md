## Architecture / ownership
The lineage matures the same basic USB-C host-driven display architecture: increasingly good Micro-OLED optics, audio and ergonomics, with limited reason to award high system openness because firmware and optics remain proprietary. Practical Owner Control is nevertheless strong because the attached phone/PC/console determines content and compute.

Cloud Independence is correspondingly high: normal display operation does not require a TCL cloud service. That local peripheral behavior should not be confused with firmware-level openness.
