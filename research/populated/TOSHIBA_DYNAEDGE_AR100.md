# Toshiba / Dynabook dynaEdge AR100 — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/LINEAGE_TOSHIBA_DYNAEDGE_AR100.md` (2026-08-12) and cited Dynabook/Toshiba primary sources.

## Identity
One clear glasses generation: AR100 HMD paired by USB-C with the DE-100 Windows 10 Pro mini PC. Vision DE Suite revisions and mounting options are software/accessory evolution, not new hardware generations.

## Hardware
Existing research documents a 47 g AR100 HMD with 640×360 PixelPure display, 5 MP autofocus camera with 1080p30 capture, speaker, dual microphones, touchpad/buttons, proximity/ambient-light sensors, gyroscope, accelerometer, compass and GPS. The DE-100 provides x86 compute, up to 16 GB RAM, M.2 SSD, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, microSD and TPM. Multiple mounts and prescription-compatible safety frames were offered; AR100 can reach IP63 with connector cover.

## Acquisition / development
Dynabook documented complete kits at $2,399.99 and $2,899.99 and an earlier $1,899.99 starting price. Developer kits included a complete reference SDK, while Windows 10 Pro broadened owner/developer control beyond the vendor suite.

## Ownership interpretation
Core Windows applications, local documents and camera functions are not inherently vendor-cloud dependent; remote-expert/cloud dependence varies by chosen software. Proprietary HMD firmware/hardware caps openness.
