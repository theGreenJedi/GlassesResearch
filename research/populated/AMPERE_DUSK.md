# Ampere Dusk — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/HIGH_THROUGHPUT_BATCH_02.md` and Ampere product/manual sources cited there.

## Hardware and eyewear function
Ampere documents ~26 g mass, electrochromic polarized lenses, on-frame or app tint control, open-ear speakers, dual MEMS microphones, Bluetooth 5.0, IPX4, roughly four hours active listening and seven days of paired tint adjustment.

This is adaptive/audio smart eyewear, not a wearer-display system: tint-changing lenses are not a HUD, and no outward-facing camera is established.

Report-card anchor: H6.5 W9.5 VAI N/A S5.5 O3.0 OC6.0 CI9.5 Hack3.5 HUD N/A; Value not yet graded.

## Ownership interpretation
Physical tint controls preserve useful operation without the companion app, making core lens functionality unusually cloud-independent. Standard Bluetooth supports ordinary audio use. No public SDK, open firmware or deep low-level hardware interface is established by the current packet.

## Evidence gaps
Current purchase/value basis, independent prescription/lens service, electrochromic lens aging, battery replacement, app/account dependence, Bluetooth profile detail, firmware updates, repairability and long-term parts availability remain open.
