# ODG R-Series — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/LINEAGE_ODG_R_SERIES.md` (2026-08-12) and its cited ODG/Qualcomm/archival sources.

## Lineage
The best-documented late lineage is R-7 → R-8/R-9. Earlier R-series hardware remains archival work. R-7 crossed the acquisition threshold; R-8 and R-9 remain announced/development products pending stronger proof of completed sale or procurement.

## R-7
Commercial self-contained enterprise AR computer. Existing research documents Snapdragon-class mobile compute, Android/ReticleOS, binocular see-through display, cameras/sensors, audio, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth and Android/ODG application development. Core local operation did not inherently require an ODG cloud.

Report-card anchor: H7.0 W3.5 VAI N/A S7.0 O6.0 OC7.5 CI9.0 Hack6.5 HUD7.5 V4.5.

## R-8
Announced CES 2017 smaller model using Snapdragon 835, >40° FOV and target price below $1,000. Evidence supports announcement and planned availability, not a sufficiently clean completed acquisition route. Do not inherit R-7 or R-9 scores.

## R-9
Announced higher-end 2017 platform using Snapdragon 835, ~50° FOV 1080p display, 6DoF-related capabilities and planned Vuforia support, with intended ~$1,799 pricing. Sale/procurement evidence remains insufficient for ledger admission.

## Lifecycle
ODG's disappearance materially affects support and practical hackability but does not erase the original local-compute/developer architecture. Preserve both historical capability and present-day serviceability limitations.

## Evidence gaps
Recover storefronts, manuals, SDK archives, firmware/serviceability evidence, parts/battery data, and explicit shipment/procurement records for R-8/R-9 before promotion.
