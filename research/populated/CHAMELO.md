# Chamelo — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/LINEAGE_CHAMELO.md` (2026-08-12) and its cited Chamelo primary sources.

## Lineage
Chamelo is a smart-lens eyewear maker with distinct branches rather than one camera/AI lineage: Dusk/Eclipse tint-adjustable eyewear, Music Shield tint + audio sport eyewear, and Aura/Prismatic color-changing eyewear. Licensed NBA editions and colorways remain variants unless electronics change.

## Dusk / Eclipse
Electrochromic/tint-adjustable sunglass with optional audio and app-enabled tint control. It is smart eyewear, not visual AI or a HUD platform.

## Music Shield Gen 2
Electronic tint + open-ear audio in sport-shield form. Existing research documents 51 g, 62-hour tint-control battery life, 6-hour audio battery life, IPX4 and ~0.1-second tint transition. The core tint function is not inherently cloud dependent.

## Aura
Electronically controlled Prismatic color-changing lenses with four color states, ~35 g weight, IPX4, ~47-hour lens-control battery life and prescription-ready configurations. Audio and non-audio SKUs are configurations, not automatic generations.

## Ownership interpretation
Wearability and cloud independence are unusually strong because core functionality remains conventional-eyewear-like and local. Openness/hackability remain low because no meaningful public SDK, firmware source, or hardware-development interface was established in the lineage packet.

## Evidence gaps
Independent optical serviceability, battery aging, repairability, protocol access, replacement-part availability and exact regional/version variance still require dedicated evidence.
