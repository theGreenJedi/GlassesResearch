# Lenovo ThinkReality A3 — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/HIGH_THROUGHPUT_BATCH_06.md` and Lenovo A3 support/release material. A3 remains separate from the materially different A6 generation already recorded elsewhere.

## Shared hardware
Both A3 editions use Qualcomm XR1, binocular 1080p displays, 8 MP RGB camera, dual fisheye tracking cameras, 6DoF tracking, microphones, speakers, USB-C/DisplayPort and an approximately 130 g glasses assembly.

## ThinkReality A3 PC Edition — GLS-0114
Emphasizes host-driven virtual monitors and PC display/spatial workflows.

Report-card anchor: H8.5 W6.5 VAI6.5 S8.0 O7.0 OC8.0 CI9.0 Hack7.0 HUD8.5 V7.0.

The host-driven architecture makes local operation and owner-selected compute especially strong; camera/tracking capability is real, but the product should not be scored as a consumer conversational visual-AI device merely because spatial sensing exists.

## ThinkReality A3 Industrial Edition — GLS-0115
Layers Lenovo ThinkReality managed deployment, remote assistance, guided workflows and 3D visualization over the shared A3 hardware.

Report-card anchor: H8.5 W6.5 VAI7.5 S8.5 O7.5 OC8.0 CI8.5 Hack7.5 HUD8.5 V7.0.

The additional enterprise platform increases contextual software/visual utility and development surface, while managed/cloud workflows create somewhat greater vendor-service dependence than the PC Edition.

## Ownership interpretation
ThinkReality includes SDK and enterprise deployment tooling, creating meaningful application control. Hardware/firmware remain proprietary. The two editions are configurations around shared hardware with materially different deployment/software emphasis, not evidence for unrelated physical generations.

## Evidence gaps
Exact edition-specific firmware, account/portal requirements, current support horizon, repairability, tether/host compatibility, prescription/fit options, battery/power behavior and acquisition/current value should be retained as model-specific fields.
