## Software / ownership
SpaceWalker adds multi-screen, productivity, 3DoF and 2D-to-3D features across supported hosts. Standard USB-C/host-driven architectures create strong practical owner control and service independence, but the glasses firmware/software stack itself remains proprietary. Interoperability is not the same thing as open hardware.

## Optical serviceability
Built-in myopia adjustment is a meaningful usability feature, but it is not equivalent to full prescription serviceability. Exact correction ranges, astigmatism support, lens insert options, independent optical service and IPD/fit constraints should remain model-specific research fields.

## Research priorities
Current/legacy price checks where ungraded, exact host compatibility by model, firmware/update behavior, repairability, myopia/IPD limits, prescription options, long-duration wear and accessory dependence.
