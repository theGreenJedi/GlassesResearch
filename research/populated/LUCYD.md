## Lyte XL — canonical successor candidate
Launched October 2023 as six new smart-eyewear styles. Innovative Eyewear describes flexible hinges for wider fit, improved speaker and microphone quality, thinner/more ergonomic temples and updated packaging. This is the next named commercial product step established after Lyte 2.0.

A new report card should be created from exact XL specifications rather than inheriting all Lyte 2.0 values.

## Optical serviceability — confirmed
EV-0035 materially resolves a major ownership field. Innovative Eyewear states that its frame fronts are designed for easy lens fitting **by any optician**, and that its frames can be fitted with prescription, sunglass, reading and blue-light lens formats. This supports the descriptive state **ordinary optical service** for the documented Lyte family rather than merely `vendor prescription available`.

This is unusually strong owner-control evidence for consumer smart eyewear because long-term vision correction is not restricted to a proprietary lens vendor.

## Ownership interpretation
Standard Bluetooth interoperability gives practical owner choice across phone assistants and audio services, and core calls/audio do not require a Lucyd cloud. Ordinary-optician lens fitting strengthens long-term owner control and survivability. No public device SDK, firmware replacement or low-level sensor interface is established by the current evidence set.
