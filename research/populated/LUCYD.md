# Lucyd Lyte — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/HIGH_THROUGHPUT_BATCH_02.md`, Lucyd/Innovative Eyewear primary material and cited SEC filings.

## Lineage
This pass covers Lucyd Lyte 1.0 and Lyte 2.0 as successive audio-eyewear generations. Both are prescription-oriented Bluetooth peripherals rather than camera/HUD platforms.

## Lyte 1.0 — GLS-0030
January 2021 first commercial Lyte generation. Existing research documents Bluetooth open-ear audio, microphone, phone voice-assistant use, prescription compatibility and broad lens customization. Later SEC disclosure that Lyte 2.0 improved battery life ~50% places Gen 1 playback around eight hours.

Report-card anchor: H5.5 W9.0 VAI N/A S5.5 O3.0 OC6.5 CI9.0 Hack3.5 HUD N/A; Value not yet graded.

## Lyte 2.0 — GLS-0031
Launched February 2023. Manufacturer/SEC evidence documents four speakers, dual noise-cancelling microphones, Bluetooth 5.2, 12-hour playback/call time, thinner fashion-oriented frames and extensive prescription/lens options.

Report-card anchor: H6.5 W9.0 VAI N/A S6.5 O3.0 OC6.5 CI9.0 Hack3.5 HUD N/A; Value not yet graded.

## Ownership interpretation
Standard Bluetooth interoperability gives practical owner choice across phone assistants and audio services, and core calls/audio do not require a Lucyd cloud. The evidence set does not establish public device SDKs, firmware replacement, low-level sensor access or a wearer-facing display/camera.

## Evidence gaps
Current value, exact Bluetooth profiles/codecs, app/account requirements, firmware/update mechanism, battery aging/replacement, independent optical serviceability and later-generation electronics need dedicated evidence.
