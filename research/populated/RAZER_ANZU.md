## Razer Anzu — GLS-0018
2021 legacy audio-glasses product. Razer documents open-ear audio, dual 16 mm drivers, two omnidirectional microphones, touch controls, Bluetooth 5.2, SBC/AAC, approximately five hours battery life, 43–48 g mass, blue-light-filtering lenses, polarized sunglass replacements and a low-latency gaming mode through the Razer Audio app. There is no camera or display.

Report-card anchor: H6.5 W8.0 VAI N/A S5.5 O3.0 OC4.0 CI8.5 Hack3.5 HUD N/A; Value not yet graded.

## Ownership interpretation
Standard Bluetooth audio, microphones and controls do not inherently require a Razer cloud, producing strong core cloud independence. The device is not documented as a programmable platform: no meaningful public SDK, firmware source or low-level hardware-development path is established.

## N/A rule
Visual AI and Display/HUD are N/A because no outward-facing camera or wearer display is part of the architecture.

## Evidence gaps
Current secondary-market value, battery aging/replacement, Bluetooth profile/codec behavior across hosts, app/firmware availability, independent lens/prescription service and physical repairability.
