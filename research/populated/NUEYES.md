# NuEyes — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/LINEAGE_NUEYES.md` (2026-08-12) and its cited NuEyes primary sources.

## Lineage
NuEyes resolves into a self-contained low-vision branch culminating in e2+ and a lighter tethered Pro branch: Pro 3, Pro 3e, Pro 4. Unnamed predecessors remain lineage context, not catalog rows.

## e2+
Standalone assistive headset. Existing lineage research documents Snapdragon 835, 64 GB storage, 4K display, 101° FOV, 16 MP autofocus camera, 3DoF tracking, OCR/text-to-speech, up to 18× magnification and 268 g. No phone is required. This supports strong hardware and cloud-independence evidence, but modern general-purpose visual-AI claims should remain narrower than OCR/assistive vision.

## Pro 3
Documented 2020 family with 4K displays, ~51° FOV and ~88 g. Flagship configuration included dual 6DoF cameras, 20 MP RGB camera and an SDK covering SLAM, object/speech/gesture recognition and plane detection. Pro 3-C used USB-C DisplayPort and a 5 MP camera; thermal version targeted enterprise use. Treat configurations separately only if the catalog later establishes materially distinct hardware rows.

## Pro 3e
Display-centric tethered branch: 68 g, birdbath optics, 1920×1080 @ 75 Hz, 2000 nits, 3DoF IMU, stereo audio and USB-C DisplayPort. Core display use is host-driven and therefore highly cloud-independent; AI is N/A unless supplied by the host.

## Pro 4
Current tethered successor in the research packet: 102 g, binocular 1080p OLED, 43° FOV, 12 MP camera, IMU/light/distance sensors and USB-C host connection. Standard, federal/DoD and low-vision bundles are configurations, not automatic hardware generations.

## Evidence gaps
Prescription/optical serviceability, repairability, battery aging, exact regional variants, current developer-access status and long-term service survival still require field-level evidence. Preserve unknowns rather than inheriting claims across generations.
