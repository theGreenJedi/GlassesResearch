# HTC VIVE Eagle — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/HIGH_THROUGHPUT_BATCH_01.md` and HTC primary specification/newsroom sources cited there.

## Hardware
Completed research documents Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1, 12 MP ultrawide camera, 4 GB RAM, 32 GB storage, four-microphone beamforming array, stereo open-ear speakers, Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.3, IP54, 235 mAh battery and encrypted local storage. Weight is roughly 49–52 g with lens depending on configuration.

## AI and software
VIVE AI supports camera-driven translation, recall, note and assistant workflows, with access to Gemini/OpenAI-backed capabilities. This is genuine visual-AI use, but the experience remains vendor-defined rather than an unrestricted development platform.

## Ownership interpretation
Local encrypted storage and multiple AI-provider options improve owner control relative to single-provider closed products. They do not establish replaceable system software, unrestricted model endpoints or a public hardware SDK. Capture/storage remain more local than many AI functions, which are service-connected and smartphone-dependent.

## Evidence gaps
Exact offline feature survival, provider-selection limits, regional/account requirements, prescription serviceability, repairability, battery aging, protocol exposure, firmware policy and current price/value need field-level population.
