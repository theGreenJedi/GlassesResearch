# Optinvent ORA — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/LINEAGE_OPTINVENT_ORA.md` (2026-08-12) and cited Optinvent primary sources.

## Lineage
ORA-1 → ORA-2 are the smart-glasses lineage. ORA-X is adjacent over-ear AR-display hardware, ORA-EYE is software/service packaging, and ORA-H is a prototype smart helmet.

## ORA-1
Purchasable developer smart glasses established through Kickstarter delivery/company-history evidence. Native Android application execution and Wikitude SDK positioning support meaningful software openness and owner control. Surviving first-party hardware detail is incomplete, so Hardware remains ungraded rather than inherited from ORA-2.

Current anchors: W3.5, VAI N/A, S7.0, O6.5, OC7.5, CI9.0, Hack6.5, HUD7.0, V5.5; Hardware not yet graded.

## ORA-2
Commercial professional smart glasses. Existing research documents 699 EUR ex-VAT pricing, Android AOSP 4.4.2, dual-core compute/GPU, 5 MP autofocus camera, microphone/audio, 9-axis sensing, GPS, Wi-Fi, BLE, trackpad, 1200 mAh battery, ~5 hours display-on time, ~90 g mass and Flip-Vu transparent display. SDK/manual were included.

Report-card anchor: H7.5 W4.5 VAI N/A S7.5 O6.5 OC8.0 CI9.0 Hack7.0 HUD8.0 V6.5.

## Adjacent members
ORA-X remains lineage-relevant but outside the glasses count because Optinvent positions it as over-ear AR headphones. ORA-EYE is a solution package around ORA-2, not a hardware generation. ORA-H remains prototype-only.

## Evidence gaps
Recover ORA-1 manuals, historical shop pages, pledge tiers and SDK documentation; document modern repairability, battery/parts survivability and long-term software availability for both admitted glasses generations.
