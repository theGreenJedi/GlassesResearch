# Sony SmartEyeglass Developer Edition — populated research record

Source basis: `docs/report-cards/HIGH_THROUGHPUT_BATCH_06.md` and Sony's SED-E1 launch/specification material.

## SED-E1 — GLS-0113
Sony sold SmartEyeglass SED-E1 specifically as a Developer Edition and accompanied it with an SDK. Existing research documents a binocular transparent waveguide, 419×138 green monochrome display, 20° diagonal FOV, accelerometer, gyroscope, compass, light sensor, microphones, 3 MP camera, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi.

The eyewear weighs approximately 77 g plus a cabled controller, with roughly 150 minutes non-camera battery life. It is therefore best understood as an early developer AR platform rather than ordinary all-day eyewear.

## Ownership interpretation
A public SDK provides meaningful application-layer openness and owner experimentation. The platform does not approach open-firmware/open-hardware benchmarks, and surviving long-term platform/service support needs archival verification. Core local sensing/display architecture is not inherently dependent on a Sony cloud.

## Evidence gaps
Developer SDK archive availability, exact host/controller architecture, firmware access, repairability, battery replacement, prescription/optical service, post-discontinuation application survival and current historical-market value.
