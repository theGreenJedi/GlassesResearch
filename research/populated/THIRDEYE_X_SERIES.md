## Lineage
X1 is a documented earlier member, X2 is the best-documented commercial generation, and Alpha1 is a later real hardware generation whose acquisition/status details remain incomplete. MIDAS belongs on the adjacent protective-equipment/head-worn AR shelf.

## X1
Preserve as an earlier X-series member. Current surviving first-party evidence is insufficient for a complete generation-specific product sheet, acquisition record or report card. Do not inherit X2 specifications.

## X2 MR Glasses
Commercial standalone enterprise MR platform. Existing research documents $2,450 direct purchase, 1280×720 waveguide displays, Snapdragon XR1, 4 GB RAM, 64 GB flash, GPS, multiple depth/camera sensors, prescription-frame availability, USB-C offline updating plus OTA, built-in thermal sensing, SLAM SDK support and Android customization.

Report-card anchor: H8.0 W4.0 VAI6.5 S8.0 O6.5 OC7.0 CI7.5 Hack6.5 HUD8.0 V5.5.

Cloud dependence is mixed: local standalone AR and offline updating are real, while MDM, remote-assistance and cloud-backed workflows add optional/operational service reliance.

## Alpha1
ThirdEye's compliance material identifies Alpha1 as a real product generation, but a clean price/purchase route and complete generation-specific specification set are not yet in the evidence package. Preserve without copying X2 claims.
