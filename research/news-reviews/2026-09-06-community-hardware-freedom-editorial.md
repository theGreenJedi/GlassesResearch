## Supported synthesis

- Panny777 documents and publishes an independent MYVU / Star Air XGA010C client that speaks the glasses' Bluetooth protocol directly and reports hardware verification against one pair.
- The Rokid AI Glasses Style community repository publishes evidence-labeled consumer, developer, and research documentation and explicitly distinguishes demonstrated capabilities from replacement-companion, authorization/session, and firmware capabilities not yet delivered.
- CyanBridge publishes an alternative companion/interoperability workspace with a supported HeyCyan path and explicitly experimental/partial paths for other ecosystems.
- CyanBridge explicitly acknowledges Panny777's Meizu MYVU Client and says its hardware-verified BLE, ECDH, RFCOMM relay, heartbeat, and display transport work is used by CyanBridge's native MYVU integration.

## Editorial boundary

The feature may argue that these projects illustrate an emerging community pattern around practical owner control. It must not claim that all three repositories are one organization, that all capabilities have been independently reproduced by GlassesResearch, that vendor security boundaries are illegitimate, or that any experimental command/firmware path is safe.

This feature is deliberately **not** inserted into `data/verified-changes.json`. Editorial prominence is separate from chronological Verified-news state, and the homepage must continue to expose the actual newest Verified items in their canonical order.
