# Sensing Capability Framework

Research should separate sensing hardware from the ways it can actually be used.

For each model, record documented imaging, audio, motion, proximity, ambient-light, touch, eye/gaze, depth/spatial, location, and other sensing capabilities.

For every sensing capability distinguish physical presence, use by manufacturer software, availability through an official developer interface, community-demonstrated availability, and direct GlassesResearch verification. Preserve engineering details such as resolution or sampling characteristics only when evidence supports them.

Manufacturer use of a sensor does not automatically mean owner access. Lack of a public developer interface also does not prove access is impossible. Unknown remains unknown.

These findings support Hardware, Visual AI, Openness, Owner Control, and Hackability.
