## What makes this site different

GlassesResearch is not a review blog and does not rank products for sponsorship or affiliate sales. We collect sources, preserve useful technical material, test what we can, and separate what is known from what remains unknown or disputed.

When a claim is supported, we show the source. When evidence conflicts, we say so. When we do not know, we keep researching rather than filling the gap with speculation.

## Current phase: Research / Content / Populate

The framework phase is complete. The standing operating model is:

> **Research → Verify → Populate → Preserve → Improve**

The priority now is knowledge: reduce Unknown and Not Verified entries, find stronger sources, populate model and comparison data, preserve fragile resources, improve historical coverage, and correct mistakes.

A useful discovery should not remain isolated. Once verified, it should improve every relevant model page, comparison, timeline entry, FAQ, developer resource, glossary entry, evidence record, or archive page.

## Evidence standard

Public-facing status is intentionally simple:

- **Verified** — enough evidence exists to support the claim, with sources.
- **Unknown** — we have not yet found adequate evidence.
- **Disputed** — credible evidence conflicts and the claim remains under review.

Unknown is not intended as permanent filler. It belongs on the research agenda until evidence establishes the answer or the reason it cannot currently be established is documented.

The source itself tells you whether the evidence came from hands-on testing, a manufacturer, an FCC filing, a research paper, a repository, or community work.

Read the full [Research Standards](docs/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md).
