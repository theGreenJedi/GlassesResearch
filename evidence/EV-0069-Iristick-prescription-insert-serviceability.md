# EV-0069 — Iristick prescription-insert serviceability

Last verified: 2026-08-15  
Applies to: Iristick G3 (GLS-0119), with lineage context for G2/G2 PRO and H1  
Evidence class: vendor-primary

## Finding

Iristick provides an ordinary-optician route for the G3 through a removable magnetic prescription insert.

The current G3 product page and FAQ state that:

- the magnetic insert is a separate optional accessory;
- the wearer takes that insert to an optician to have corrective lenses fitted;
- the completed insert attaches magnetically to the smart-glasses frame;
- each wearer can keep a personalized insert while the electronic device remains shareable.

Iristick's current technical-specification page also lists a prescription insert among G3 accessories, although the product FAQ describes it as separately available. Treat package inclusion as region/order dependent until confirmed at purchase.

Sources:

- [Iristick G3 product page](https://iristick.com/tools/Iristick.G3/)
- [Iristick smart-glasses specifications](https://docs.iristick.com/smart-glasses/specifications/)
- [Iristick FAQ](https://iristick.com/support/faqs/)

## Classification

**Ordinary-optician service through an owner-removable magnetic insert.**

This is stronger than a generic “prescription compatible” claim because the manufacturer explicitly directs the owner to an optician and describes removal/reinstallation. It does not establish independent service of the safety-glasses lens, HUD optics, cameras, or electronics.

## Lineage limits

- Current Iristick documentation also describes prescription inserts for G2/G2 PRO, but the magnetic attachment and included/separate accessory details must remain model-specific.
- H1 is documented for use in combination with the wearer's existing prescription glasses; that is compatibility with personal eyewear, not direct glazing of H1.
- No interchangeability between G2 and G3 inserts is established.

## Unknowns

The current sources do not publish:

- sphere, cylinder, prism, addition, or progressive limits;
- lens material, index, base curve, minimum thickness, or bevel requirements;
- insert price, replacement-part stock, or universal regional availability;
- safety-certification implications of the corrective lenses;
- an optician instruction sheet or exact fitting restrictions;
- repair paths for the electronic frame, HUD, cameras, cable, or connectors.

Those fields remain unknown rather than inferred.
