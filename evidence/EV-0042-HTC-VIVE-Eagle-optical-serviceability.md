# EV-0042 — HTC VIVE Eagle optical and prescription-lens serviceability

Verified: 2026-08-14
Source class: vendor product/support primary
Confidence: confirmed for the documented prescription range and professional lens-replacement path; independent optician availability and replacement cost remain unverified
Scope: GLS-0025 — HTC VIVE Eagle

## Finding

VIVE Eagle uses a **specialist-serviceable lens path**, not a sealed or manufacturer-only optical path. HTC explicitly permits a qualified eye-care professional to remove and replace the lenses and directs owners to certified eyewear retailers. HTC documents a supported prescription range of **-8D to +4D**.

The retail Sun and AdaptiveSun configurations are sold as non-prescription lenses in the cited Singapore product page. Prescription use therefore requires replacement lenses rather than a prescription configuration of those stock lens packages.

## Interpretation

This is materially better than eyewear whose optics are bonded, display-calibrated or available only as proprietary inserts. It does not yet establish ordinary walk-in service everywhere: HTC's wording relies on qualified/certified optical channels, and no public lens geometry, edging template, replacement part number or global price was located in this pass.

The evidence supports raising confidence in VIVE Eagle wearability and ownership durability without treating the entire device as repairable. Lens serviceability is only one service layer; battery replacement, temple electronics, hinges, buttons, camera, charging hardware and spare-part availability remain separate questions.

## Remaining verification

- Ask representative independent opticians in supported markets whether they will edge lenses for the frame.
- Record lens blank/material limits, cylinder/prism restrictions and whether high-index lenses are required near -8D.
- Locate any official edging template, demo lens markings or frame/lens part numbers.
- Record regional price, turnaround and warranty effects.
- Verify whether replacement lenses can retain equivalent UV, tint, photochromic and coating performance.
