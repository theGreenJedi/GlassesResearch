# EV-0088 — nubia Neovision Glass acquisition and primary specifications

Checked: 2026-08-31

## Evidence lane

Primary manufacturer / commercial evidence.

## Identity and acquisition

ZTE/nubia exposes a first-party product-store page for **nubia Neovision Glass**. The page shows a **$529.00** total, states that the product ships in three days, provides shipping-policy and warranty/return language, and exposes a Buy now flow gated by an invitation-code field. This is direct manufacturer-controlled commercial evidence and crosses GlassesResearch's purchaser-history threshold.

Primary sources:
- https://global.ztedevices.com/products/nubia-neovision-ar-glasses
- https://www.zte.com.cn/global/about/news/zte-launches-nubias-first-ar-eyewear-nubia-neovision-glass-at-mwc-2023.html

## Primary technical claims

The first-party store page advertises, among other fields:
- a 120-inch virtual-screen presentation;
- 0–500° myopia adjustment;
- 79 g weight;
- replaceable colored glow lenses;
- integrated audio;
- broad device compatibility.

These are manufacturer claims. They establish identity, acquisition and a bounded set of claimed specifications, but do not establish measured visual quality, openness, owner control, cloud independence, repairability, or long-duration comfort.

## Catalog consequence

Neovision Glass is fundamentally eyewear rather than a headset. It belongs in the canonical smart-glasses purchaser-history ledger as a tethered personal-display/XR-glasses product. Admission must preserve Unknown for unsupported Report Card fields.
