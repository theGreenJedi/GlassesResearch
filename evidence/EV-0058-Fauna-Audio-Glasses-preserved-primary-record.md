## Findings

Fauna's 2020 guide identifies the product as Bluetooth 5.0 audio glasses offered with sun or blue-light lenses. It documents music, calls and phone-assistant interaction; acetate frames; optician-adjustable temples; prescription-lens retrofitting; and a dedicated charging case. The product is a screen-free Bluetooth peripheral, not a camera or display platform.

USound identifies Fauna as a former subsidiary proof-of-concept product and documents its micro two-way speaker system: MEMS tweeter plus electrodynamic driver in a dipole arrangement. USound also states that the project has fulfilled its purpose, supporting legacy/discontinued status without implying the hardware has ceased functioning.

No primary source located in this pass documents a public SDK, firmware source, general application runtime or vendor-cloud requirement for core music and calls. Those absences are not converted into undocumented negative technical claims; they bound the Openness and Hackability judgments conservatively.
