# EV-0050 — Mainstream optical-serviceability wave seven

- **Captured:** 2026-08-14
- **Scope:** Meta/Ray-Ban/Oakley, Even Realities, Brilliant Labs, Rokid, and RayNeo
- **Evidence class:** first-party product, FAQ, support, and hardware documentation
- **Purpose:** distinguish ordinary-optician, authorized-partner, owner-installable carrier, and vendor-only paths without letting prescription availability imply electronic repairability.

## Findings

| Product / family | First-party evidence | Supported optical path | Classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oakley Meta HSTN | Oakley sells model-specific replacement lenses and states that, depending on region, an existing pair may receive prescription lenses at the owner's optometrist or preferred optical provider. | Replacement lenses plus a region-dependent independent optical-provider path. | **Ordinary optical provider supported where Oakley documents the regional pathway.** |
| Ray-Ban Meta | Ray-Ban sells prescription configurations and supplies instructions for ordering prescription lenses; warranty repair/replacement remains a Ray-Ban claim path. The reviewed FAQ does not establish that every independent shop may reglaze every Meta frame. | Brand/store prescription and replacement workflow; independent-shop generalization not proven. | **Vendor/authorized optical path documented; ordinary-independent path model/region dependent.** |
| Even G2 | Even documents a wide supported single-vision range, replacement lenses, and progressive/UltraFit fitting through certified optician partners. | Replacement without replacing the electronic frame, but the integrated digital lens keeps the supported service path specialist-controlled. | **Specialist/authorized optical service.** |
| Brilliant Frame | Brilliant publishes an Rx clip design and prescription-lens mechanical drawing. | Owner/fabricator-accessible interface documentation; actual ordinary-shop willingness and fabrication workflow not established. | **Documented open optical interface; practical service channel unverified.** |
| Brilliant Halo | Brilliant directs prescription and sunglass lens orders through SmartBuyGlasses and documents display-optic adjustment around +2 to -6 D. | Named partner path. | **Partner optical service; ordinary-independent path unverified.** |
| Rokid Glasses | Rokid's FAQ says the magnetic prescription frame may be taken to a local optical store for lenses made by a professional optician, with documented range/curvature limits. | Ordinary optician using the official magnetic carrier. | **Ordinary optical shop supported with official carrier.** |
| RayNeo X3 Pro | RayNeo's product page says its global lens partners provide customized nearsighted lenses. Current first-party guidance describes a magnetic insert/partner-lab route. | Partner-supplied magnetic prescription insert. | **Partner optical service; ordinary-independent fabrication not established.** |

## Boundary rules

1. Prescription availability is not proof of lens replacement after purchase.
2. A removable or magnetic carrier is not proof that an ordinary shop will fabricate the required lens geometry.
3. A documented optometrist/preferred-provider route can be called ordinary optical service only for the product and regions for which the manufacturer says so.
4. Lens replacement does not establish battery, hinge, camera, display, temple, charging-case, or board repairability.
5. Published mechanical drawings improve preservation potential but are not proof of a currently operating retail service channel.
