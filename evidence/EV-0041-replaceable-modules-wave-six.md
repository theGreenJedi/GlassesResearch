# EV-0041 — replaceable modules wave six

Verified: 2026-08-13

## RealWear Navigator 500 / 520 / Z1
Primary sources: RealWear Navigator 520 product/specification pages, battery replacement support page, current spare battery listing, and model-identification support page.

- Navigator 500, 520 and Z1 use swappable external battery architecture.
- Navigator 520 supports hot swapping: the device enters a low-power state and the battery can be replaced without shutting down when done within the documented window.
- RealWear currently sells a dedicated Navigator 520 spare battery pack and single-battery charger.
- The product page specifies a 2600 mAh / 3.8 V hot-swappable lithium-polymer pack.
- HMT-1Z1 is explicitly identified as having an internal non-swappable battery, so battery-repair claims must not be transferred backward across the lineage.

Interpretation: Navigator 500/520/Z1 provide strong field-replaceable battery evidence and current replacement-part availability. This is materially better long-term power serviceability than sealed consumer eyewear. HMT-1/HMT-1Z1 require separate treatment.

## Solos modular temples
Primary sources: Solos current user manuals, FAQ and AirGo A5 Temple Kit product page.

- Solos documents detachable frame fronts and temples as a core physical architecture.
- AirGo V guidance explicitly instructs users to detach the left temple to stop the camera module drawing standby power, confirming that temple removal is an owner-supported operation.
- Solos currently sells an AirGo A5 Temple Kit that attaches to compatible AirGo 3 frame fronts and upgrades the smart-electronics temples without replacing the optical front.
- Historical manuals include an AirGo Battery Temple Kit for AirGo 1.
- Current exchange-policy language can restrict exchange of individual temples/accessories, which is different from technical modularity or retail spare-part availability.

Interpretation: Solos has genuine owner-removable modular electronics and a current upgrade/replacement temple product for at least part of the lineage. Do not generalize battery-temple replaceability to every AirGo generation without model-specific evidence.

## Repairability implications
A replaceable module should be recorded with four separate questions:
1. physically owner-removable;
2. replacement part currently sold;
3. replacement preserves calibration/sealing/function without vendor repair;
4. replacement remains available after model end-of-sale.

Current evidence can strongly establish the first two while the last two remain model/lifecycle dependent.
