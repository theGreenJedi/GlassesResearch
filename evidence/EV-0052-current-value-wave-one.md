## Calibration rationale

- **Oakley Meta HSTN:** strong mainstream camera/audio/AI polish and wearability at a sub-$500 price, offset by no display, mandatory ecosystem dependencies and sealed batteries.
- **Even G2:** higher price than audio/camera glasses but includes a discreet binocular HUD, strong wearability, a documented plugin surface and replaceable prescription lenses. Missing camera/speaker and uncertain electronics repairability cap the score.
- **Rokid Glasses:** at the checked sale price, delivers integrated HUD, sensing, audio, local storage/compute, developer access and a strong optical-service route. Activation/cloud dependence and unknown battery repair prevent an exceptional score.
- **RayNeo X3 Pro:** among the most capable integrated consumer AR glasses in this wave, but the $1,299 price is nearly twice Rokid's sale price. Creator Mode and local residue preserve ownership value, while cloud-defined AI and unknown physical service limit it.

These are catalog-wide scores, not within-category rankings. A later price change, required subscription, included-accessory change, or verified repair path requires recalculation.
