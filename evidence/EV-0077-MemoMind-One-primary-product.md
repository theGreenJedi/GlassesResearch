## What the current primary material establishes

MemoMind's current product material presents **MemoMind One** as a camera-free AI smart-glasses product with:

- a dual-eye Micro-LED display;
- a claimed display brightness of 2,000 nits;
- stereo open-ear speakers using Harman AudioEFX processing;
- triple directional microphones;
- a claimed weight of 46.6 g;
- a claimed 16+ hours of mixed use per charge;
- AI assistant, recording/summarization, translation, teleprompter, notification and navigation functions;
- optional prescription ZEISS XRRX lenses;
- current pricing shown from **$399**, with the official product path linking to Kickstarter.

The paid Kickstarter/crowdfunding route is sufficient for **GLS-0160** under the canonical ledger's existing acquisition rule. It establishes an acquisition path; it does not establish broad fulfillment or retail delivery.

MemoMind's corporate history says the smart-glasses project grew from XGIMI optical research and that MemoMind / Qinglai Innovation was founded in 2025. XGIMI's January 2026 announcement independently confirms that it launched MemoMind as a new AI-hardware brand and introduced a dual-eye-display flagship in the initial product family. The early XGIMI release called that model **Memo One**; MemoMind's current product pages use **MemoMind One**.
