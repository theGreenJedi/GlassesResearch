# EV-0084 — Smart glasses and environmental context in health research

- **Evidence state:** institution-primary commentary summary
- **Last verified:** 2026-08-24
**Scope:** digital phenotyping and environmental health context

## Source

- Duke University School of Medicine: https://medschool.duke.edu/news/smart-glasses-provide-critical-information-about-impact-daily-environments-health-according

## Supported use

Duke researchers argue that camera-equipped glasses paired with machine-learning analysis could add surrounding social and environmental context that watches and body sensors often miss. The article gives health-behavior examples and points to an underlying 2020 commentary in *npj Digital Medicine*.

## Claim boundary

This is a university news summary of a commentary, not a clinical validation of a particular device or intervention. It is a lead to the underlying paper and a useful framing source for sensing, consent, privacy, and digital-phenotyping investigations.
