# EV-0049 — optical serviceability wave six

**Verified:** 2026-08-14  
**Evidence class:** Current first-party vendor product, FAQ, support and accessory pages

## Solos AirGo families

Solos explicitly states that owners may take supported smart glasses to **any optical eyewear shop** for prescription lenses because the lenses are swappable. Solos also sells vendor-produced prescription lenses with multiple indices and finishes.

**Classification:** ordinary independent optical service supported, plus optional vendor lens service.

This corrects the previous provisional vendor/specialist-only classification. Exact compatibility and removable-front architecture remain generation/frame-specific; the statement should not be used to claim that every discontinued Solos frame still has parts available.

Primary sources:

- [Solos FAQ](https://solosglasses.com/pages/frequently-asked-questions)
- [Solos warranty and lens-replacement terms](https://solosglasses.com/pages/warranty-terms-conditions)
- [Solos AirGo 3 prescription example](https://solosglasses.com/products/airgo3-xeon-collection-xeon-5s)

## Huawei audio-eyewear families

Huawei's official material establishes two useful service levels:

- HUAWEI × GENTLE MONSTER Eyewear II optical frames ship with decorative lenses using universal mounting techniques; Huawei instructs owners to replace them with prescribed optical lenses and notes that Huawei does not provide optometry.
- HUAWEI Eyewear 2 regional sales documentation says buyers needing prescription lenses should contact an optician.
- Huawei also recommends an eyewear store for professional frame adjustment and cautions against heating/bending electronics-bearing temples or ultrasonic cleaning.

**Classification:** ordinary optician prescription fitting supported for documented optical-frame variants, with electronics-specific handling restrictions.

This classification must not be copied to sunglass variants whose lens geometry or replacement policy differs.

Primary sources:

- [HUAWEI × GENTLE MONSTER Eyewear II product guidance](https://consumer.huawei.com/es/gentle-monster-eyewear2/)
- [HUAWEI Eyewear 2 regional purchase FAQ](https://consumer.huawei.com/ae-en/audio/huawei-eyewear-2/buy/)
- [HUAWEI Eyewear 2 product guidance](https://consumer.huawei.com/en/audio/huawei-eyewear-2/)

## Vuzix Z100 and Blade 2 confirmation

Vuzix sells made-to-prescription, owner-installable kits for both Z100 and Blade 2. Z100 is described as a do-it-yourself lens-change kit. Blade 2 uses a removable nose bridge and screw-fixed prescription frame assembly; its current kit is limited to single-vision prescriptions.

**Classification:** owner-installable specialist prescription insert/frame assembly.

This confirms the existing GlassesResearch classification and adds the current price basis of **$149.99 USD** for each listed kit as of the verification date. Price is volatile and is not a device-value grade.

Primary sources:

- [Z100 prescription insert](https://www.vuzix.com/products/z100-smart-glasses-prescription-inserts)
- [Blade 2 prescription insert](https://www.vuzix.com/products/vuzix-blade-2-smart-glasses-prescription-inserts)
- [Vuzix installation guides](https://www.vuzix.com/pages/prescription-lens-install-guides)

## Ruler effect

These findings reinforce three distinct service states:

1. **Ordinary independent optical service:** Solos and documented Huawei optical variants.
2. **Owner-installable specialist insert:** Vuzix Z100 and Blade 2.
3. **Vendor/certified-only service:** reserved for products whose integrated optics require the manufacturer's designated channel.

A product can be optically serviceable while its battery and electronics remain sealed. Optical evidence must not inflate overall repairability.
