# EV-0081 — AR smart-glasses use and acceptance review

- **Evidence state:** peer-reviewed secondary research
- **Last verified:** 2026-08-24
**Scope:** augmented-reality smart-glasses acceptance and use

## Source

- Koutromanos et al., “Augmented reality smart glasses use and acceptance”: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2949678023000223

## Supported use

The 2023 paper reviews 21 empirical studies published from 2015 onward concerning acceptance of augmented-reality smart glasses across application fields. It is useful for identifying recurring adoption variables and gaps in the empirical literature.

## Claim boundary

This is a review of heterogeneous studies, not a GlassesResearch hands-on test and not evidence for the specifications, comfort, safety, or acceptance of any current consumer model. Model-level claims must trace to the underlying study and device context before promotion.
