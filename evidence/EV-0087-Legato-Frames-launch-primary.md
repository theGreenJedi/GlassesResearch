# EV-0087 — Legato Frames launch

Date checked: 2026-10-01
Evidence lane: manufacturer-primary
Status: attributed primary evidence; not GlassesResearch bench verification

## Established event

Legato announced on 2026-10-01 that Legato Frames are available for purchase in the United States, starting at $999.

## Manufacturer claims preserved

Legato says processing runs on the glasses, with no cameras or recording. The company lists prescription-lens support, Bluetooth streaming, weight as low as 34 g with lenses, and 10–12 hours of battery life.
