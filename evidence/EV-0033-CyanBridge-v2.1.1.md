## What the release establishes

CyanBridge v2.1.1 is a signed Android release of the independent HeyCyan companion stack. The release notes document:

- improved HeyCyan media synchronization and Wi-Fi Direct transfer reliability;
- fixes for remote OpenAI-compatible model endpoints, including Ollama and other servers reached over Tailscale;
- validated TuneBuds compatibility;
- expanded diagnostics for local-agent, model-backend, device-session, and transfer behavior;
- assistant setup checks and clearer firmware-subscription guidance;
- signed APK and AAB artifacts.

## Why it matters

This is stronger current evidence that the HeyCyan-compatible ecosystem can operate with owner-selected or independently hosted AI backends rather than being limited to one vendor assistant. It also shows continued maintenance of independent media-transfer and device-integration tooling.

The release does not prove that AI inference occurs on the glasses themselves, nor does it establish compatibility with every HeyCyan-branded or suspected rebrand device. Compatibility remains model-specific until tested.
