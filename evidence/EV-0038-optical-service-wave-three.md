# EV-0038 — Rokid and Vuzix optical-service evidence

Verified: 2026-08-13
Source class: vendor primary
Confidence: confirmed for exact cited products

## Rokid Glasses

Primary sources:
- https://global.rokid.com/pages/lens-service
- https://global.rokid.com/ja/pages/faqs
- https://global.rokid.com/products/rokid-glasses-prescription-frame

Rokid Glasses use an official magnetic prescription frame. Rokid's FAQ explicitly states that owners may take that frame to a local optical store and have prescription lenses made by a professional optician. Rokid gives a supported customization range of approximately +6.00D to -16.00D and supports myopia and astigmatism; its lens-service material also documents multiple refractive-index options.

Rokid recommends its official partner service for fit assurance, but local optical-shop fitting is explicitly supported. This is direct evidence of **ordinary optical service with a product-specific carrier/frame constraint** rather than vendor-only prescription service.

## Rokid AI Glasses Style

Rokid documents direct prescription-lens support for the non-display Style product and a broad lens-service program. Separate FAQ material recommends local in-network optical shops when owners want to use vision insurance. This is another strong independent-optical-service signal, but it should remain model-specific and not be copied automatically to every Rokid product.

## Vuzix Z100

Primary sources:
- https://www.vuzix.com/pages/prescription-lenses
- https://www.vuzix.com/products/z100-smart-glasses-prescription-inserts
- https://www.vuzix.com/pages/prescription-lens-install-guides

Vuzix sells prescription inserts for Z100 as a do-it-yourself kit. The owner can order prescription lenses, receive the manufactured insert separately from the glasses, and install the prescription assembly using Vuzix's published instructions.

This is strong **owner-replaceable optical service** evidence. It is not the same as an ordinary optician cutting directly into the electronics-bearing frame, but replacement does not require returning the device to Vuzix for factory installation.

## Vuzix Blade 2

Vuzix also lists Blade 2 prescription lenses and publishes an owner installation guide. The prescription assembly mounts by removing the nose-bridge screw and installing the new prescription frame assembly.

This again supports **owner-replaceable specialist insert service** rather than vendor-sealed optics.

## Comparison implication

Optical-service states should distinguish at least:
- ordinary optical shop directly supported;
- ordinary optical shop supported with an official carrier/frame;
- owner-installable prescription insert;
- authorized/certified optical partner;
- vendor lab only;
- unknown/not supported.

Rokid Glasses and Vuzix Z100/Blade 2 now have direct primary evidence allowing them to move out of generic `prescription compatible` status into more precise owner-service categories.
