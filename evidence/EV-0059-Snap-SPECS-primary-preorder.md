# EV-0059 — Snap SPECS primary preorder record

**Evidence state:** vendor-primary; announced/preorder, not independently shipped or tested  
**Model:** GLS-0146 — Snap SPECS  
**Checked:** 2026-08-14

## Primary source

- [Snap Newsroom — Introducing SPECS Augmented Reality Glasses](https://newsroom.snap.com/introducing-specs-augmented-reality-glasses)

## Verified boundary

Snap introduced SPECS on June 16, 2026 and opened a preorder route at $2,195 with a $200 refundable deposit. The company targets fall 2026 shipment in the United States, United Kingdom and France. This establishes a canonical preorder model; it does not establish customer fulfillment or independent performance.

Snap documents 47 mm and 52 mm sizes weighing 132 g and 136 g, removable prescription inserts, electrochromic lenses, a 51° full-color LCoS/waveguide display, dual Snapdragon processors, 7 ms motion-to-photon latency, and up to four hours of mixed use. The included charging case is claimed to provide four additional charges.

The platform claims hundreds of Lenses, Lens Studio development, agentic development integrations, a spatial benchmark, migration tooling and a Native Development Kit. Snap also says recording activates an LED, sensitive access requires permission, on-device processing is prioritized, and users control storage, synchronization, sharing and deletion. These are primary architectural claims, not GlassesResearch hands-on verification.
