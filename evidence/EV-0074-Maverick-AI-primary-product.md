## Supported claims

Current Everysight material identifies Maverick AI and Maverick AI Pro as distinct full-color AR + AI smart-glasses products that remain in development. The current published specifications include a 47 g weight, 22-degree field of view, Everysight BEAM projection engine, full-color Sony micro-OLED imager, Bluetooth 5.2, IP55, 8+ hours claimed continuous operation, forward-facing AI camera, microphones, audio speaker, inertial/line-of-sight sensors, touch control, and interchangeable lenses. AI Pro adds native GazeIntent eye tracking.

Everysight states that single-vision prescription support is planned at launch for SPH -3.0 to +3.0 and CYL 0 to -1.0; progressive/varifocal lenses are not available at launch. Clear, tinted, transition, and prescription lens options are described as swappable purchase/use options.

## Temporal caution

The product is pre-release and Everysight explicitly says details may change before shipping. Current 22-degree FOV supersedes older independent material reporting 28 degrees for purposes of the current specification snapshot. Older values remain historically relevant and should be preserved as temporal evidence rather than silently erased.
