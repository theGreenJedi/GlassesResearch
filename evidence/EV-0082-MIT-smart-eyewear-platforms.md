# EV-0082 — MIT Smart Eyewear Research Platforms

- **Evidence state:** institution-primary research description
- **Last verified:** 2026-08-24
**Platforms:** Project Captivate; AirSpecs

## Source

- MIT Media Lab overview: https://www.media.mit.edu/projects/smart-eyewear/overview/

## Supported claims

MIT describes a research program motivated by the lack of standard smart-glasses platforms that expose physiological data. The team reports working with eyewear manufacturers in Shenzhen to integrate face, head-movement, location, and environmental sensing into forms closer to conventional glasses. The resulting named platforms include Project Captivate and AirSpecs.

## Design relevance and boundary

The source supports the design lesson that bulky research eyewear can alter wearer behavior and undermine naturalistic studies. It does not establish that either platform is a consumer product, open platform, or direct Hawkframe component blueprint. Any engineering transfer requires publication-level component and method review.
