## Verified generation-specific facts

Lucyd launched Lyte XL on October 17, 2023 through Lucyd.co and Amazon. The collection comprised six styles and introduced improved audio/call quality, spring hinges, thinner Bluetooth temples, wider-fit frame dimensions and continued access to Lucyd's broad custom/prescription lens catalog.

Innovative Eyewear's SEC disclosure independently identifies Lyte XL as the October 2023 generation and describes the company's glasses as Bluetooth audio eyewear for music, calls and phone voice-assistant tasks. It also documents ordinary-optician lens fitting across prescription, sunglass, reading and blue-light formats.

## Evidence boundary

The sources do not justify copying Lyte 2.0's four-speaker or 12-hour endurance claims into Lyte XL. The exact XL battery, speaker count, mass, Bluetooth revision and internal chipset therefore remain unpopulated. Lucyd promoted its phone application and ChatGPT access, but neither source establishes open firmware, a public hardware SDK or an owner-replaceable onboard assistant.

## Catalog implication

The evidence supports a conservative Report Card centered on wearability, optical service, standard Bluetooth utility and cloud survival of core audio. Hardware details that remain generation-ambiguous are not promoted into the structured record.
