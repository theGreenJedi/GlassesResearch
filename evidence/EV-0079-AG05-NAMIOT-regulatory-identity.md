# EV-0079 — AG05 regulatory identity and owner-facing architecture

**Checked:** 2026-08-22  
**Subject:** AG05 SMART AI GLASSES / Shenzhen NAMIOT Technology Co., Ltd.  
**Evidence lane:** regulatory primary + manufacturer-filed user documentation + separately attributed team/community lead

## Durable regulatory anchor

FCC equipment authorization `2BWND-AG05` identifies:

- product/equipment: **SMART AI GLASSES**;
- product code: **AG05**;
- applicant: **Shenzhen NAMIOT Technology Co., Ltd.**;
- application purpose: **Original Equipment**;
- application date: **2026-07-21**.

## What the filed manual establishes

The filed manual documents a physical architecture including:

- camera;
- removable battery;
- left and right speakers;
- multiple microphones;
- touch/button controls;
- magnetic charging connection;
- privacy and status indicators.

It also documents owner-facing functions:

- local photo capture;
- video recording;
- audio recording;
- app-mediated AI image recognition;
- app-mediated AI voice assistant;
- Wi-Fi media transfer;
- Bluetooth/device name `AG05`;
- companion application named **OneGlance**.

The manual states that the battery can be removed and replaced directly when depleted. That is strong evidence for a user-swappable battery architecture; it is not by itself evidence for open battery specifications, third-party battery availability or long-term repair support.

## Community/team lead kept separate

A 2026-08-22 r/SmartGlasses post presents AG05 as a team-built product with a swappable dual-battery design and says the project keeps R&D, product design and electronic integration in-house while using specialized contract manufacturers for manufacturing/assembly. The poster also says AG05 is not yet officially listed on public ecommerce platforms.

Source:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/SmartGlasses/comments/1vv2ppn/we_got_tired_of_smart_glasses_dying_midday_so_our/

Those statements are useful provenance leads, but this evidence packet does **not** silently equate the Reddit poster/team identity with the FCC applicant unless a durable corporate/project source makes that relationship explicit.

## Conclusions supported now

High confidence:
- AG05 is a real regulatory identity associated with Shenzhen NAMIOT Technology Co., Ltd.;
- the FCC filing treats NAMIOT as the original-equipment applicant;
- AG05 has a removable-battery design in the filed manual;
- the OneGlance app is part of the filed owner workflow;
- FCC internal/external-photo exhibits exist and are suitable for later hardware-provenance study.

Not established by this packet:
- public retail availability;
- exact commercial brand used at sale;
- who operates OneGlance;
- whether NAMIOT itself owns the factory/assembly line;
- exact SoC, ISP, camera sensor or memory configuration;
- SDK/API availability;
- firmware/bootloader access;
- independent battery sourcing;
- relationship to AG03 or other NAMIOT product codes beyond the shared applicant name.

## Research disposition

Keep AG05 as an **in-scope pre-release/OEM lead**, not a canonical purchaser-history row, until a genuine acquisition route is verified. Investigate NAMIOT's other FCC filings and the OneGlance software/operator identity as a potential product-line and supply-chain graph.
