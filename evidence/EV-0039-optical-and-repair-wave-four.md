# EV-0039 — optical and repair evidence wave four

Verified: 2026-08-13

## Brilliant Labs Frame
Primary source: Brilliant Frame hardware documentation.

- Frame supports optional personalized prescriptions.
- Brilliant publishes a mechanical prescription/Rx clip design and a dedicated prescription-lens mechanical drawing.
- The hardware documentation publishes schematics, mechanical assets, firmware customization guidance, SWD debug access, and source references.
- The same documentation warns owners not to remove the built-in lithium battery because of short/overheat/fire risk.

Interpretation: Frame has unusually strong owner-access and preservation documentation, including a documented prescription interface, but that does not prove ordinary-optician lens service or safe owner battery replacement. Repairability must remain component-specific.

## Brilliant Labs Halo
Primary source: Brilliant Halo product page.

- Brilliant sells Halo with prescription and sunglass lens options through partner SmartBuyGlasses.
- Halo's display optic is adjustable approximately +2 to -6 diopters.
- Brilliant describes Halo hardware and software as open source.

Interpretation: Halo has a documented partner prescription path and optical diopter adjustment. Ordinary independent-optician service is not established by this source.

## Chamelo
Primary sources: Chamelo current prescription collection/product pages and FAQ.

- Chamelo now sells prescription-ready Aura Rx / Aura prescription products.
- Current Aura prescription product material describes premium high-index prescription lenses.
- A currently indexed FAQ still says prescription lenses are not yet offered, conflicting with current product pages.

Interpretation: treat the current product pages as evidence that prescription Aura products exist, while preserving the FAQ conflict as a documentation-staleness warning. Do not generalize prescription support to Dusk Classic or Music Shield Gen 2 without model-specific evidence. Independent optical-shop service is not established.

## Ampere Dusk Rx
Primary source: Ampere Dusk Rx product page.

- Dusk Rx is a distinct prescription-ready branch of Dusk.
- Ampere states support for more than 90% of prescriptions, approximately +/-7 SPH and +/-2 CYL, using premium high-index MR lens material.
- The product page is currently marked sold out and contains an old Spring 2025 pre-order estimate, so current availability/lifecycle status requires separate verification.

Interpretation: prescription support is established for Dusk Rx, not automatically for ordinary Dusk. Independent local-optician service and replacement-lens process remain unknown.

## Taxonomy implications
This wave reinforces separate optical-service states:
1. published owner/developer optical interface;
2. vendor/partner prescription path;
3. ordinary local-optician service;
4. owner-installable prescription carrier/insert;
5. prescription product exists but service path unknown;
6. unknown/not established.

Prescription availability alone does not establish independent serviceability or repairability.
