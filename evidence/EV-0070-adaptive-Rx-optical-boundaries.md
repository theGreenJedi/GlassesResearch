# EV-0070 — Ampere Dusk Rx and Chamelo Aura Rx optical boundaries

Last verified: 2026-08-15  
Applies to: Ampere Dusk / Dusk Rx branch (GLS-0037) and Chamelo Aura / Aura Rx configuration (GLS-0139)  
Evidence class: vendor-primary, with lifecycle interpretation

## Ampere Dusk Rx

Ampere's current Dusk Rx product page establishes a distinct vendor-supplied prescription version of the electronic-tint Dusk platform. It states:

- support for more than 90% of prescriptions;
- an approximate range of ±7 sphere and ±2 cylinder;
- premium high-index MR lens material.

The page is currently sold out and retains stale preorder-era language. That establishes a vendor prescription product and a useful correction envelope, but not current deliverability, replacement-lens stock, independent reglazing, progressive support, or ordinary-optician service.

Source: [Ampere Dusk Rx product page](https://ampere.shop/products/dusk-rx-premium-smart-glasses-with-electronic-tint-control).

**Classification:** vendor-integrated prescription configuration; independent optical service unverified.

Do not generalize Dusk Rx support to standard Dusk lenses or imply that electrochromic lens assemblies can be replaced by an ordinary optician.

## Chamelo Aura Rx

Chamelo's current prescription collection and Aura Rx product pages sell Aura Rx as a prescription version of its electronically color-changing Aura platform. This establishes a live vendor prescription-ordering path for Aura Rx.

Current primary pages do not establish:

- an exact sphere/cylinder/add/prism range;
- progressive support;
- independent reglazing or ordinary-optician service;
- replacement electronic-lens modules;
- lens interchangeability with non-Rx Aura;
- extension of Aura Rx support to Dusk Classic or Music Shield Gen 2.

Earlier research preserved a conflicting/stale FAQ that said prescription lenses were not offered. Current product and collection pages are stronger evidence of present commercial intent, but fulfillment, regional availability and replacement service remain separate questions.

Sources:

- [Chamelo prescription collection](https://chamelo.com/collections/prescription)
- [Chamelo Aura Rx collection](https://chamelo.com/collections/aura-rx)

**Classification:** vendor-integrated prescription configuration; independent optical service unverified.

## Catalog treatment

Dusk Rx and Aura Rx are treated as prescription configurations/branches of canonical Dusk and Aura records unless evidence shows materially distinct electronics sufficient for separate canonical models. Their optical support belongs in structured research notes without inflating the canonical count.
