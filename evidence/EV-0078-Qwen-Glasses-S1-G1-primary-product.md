## Supported claims

The December 2025 launch establishes two separately named hardware series under the Quark brand: flagship **S1** and lifestyle-focused **G1**. It describes S1 as a dual-display product and G1 as a camera-first product without the display, with G1 claimed at 40 g. Alibaba states the products use Qwen-powered voice/vision AI and Qwen App integration, and claims a swappable dual-battery system with up to 24 hours of use. The S1 launch material claims 0.6-second photo capture, 3K video, AI-enhanced 4K output, and low-light imaging features.

The same launch states that the glasses support the standard **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** as a developer direction. This supports an ecosystem/developer claim only; it does not prove a public device SDK, unrestricted direct hardware control, firmware access, or owner-controlled model execution on the glasses.

The March 2026 MWC announcement preserves the **S1** and **G1** product-series names while unifying the eyewear under the **Qwen Glasses** brand. It states China preorders were live and official sales began 2026-03-08. It also states an international version was planned for rollout within 2026.

## Identity conclusion

Current evidence supports a brand/name transition rather than a new third hardware generation:

- Quark AI Glasses S1 → Qwen Glasses S1
- Quark AI Glasses G1 → Qwen Glasses G1

The former Quark names must remain searchable aliases. Qwen itself is the current brand/platform identity, not another canonical glasses model.

## Unknowns preserved

Primary launch material does not establish:

- bootloader or recovery access;
- downloadable firmware or source;
- unrestricted direct-device APIs;
- whether MCP access is exposed directly to owners/developers on production glasses;
- the exact local / phone / cloud execution boundary for each AI feature;
- useful AI operation without Alibaba/Qwen services or account dependencies;
- international retail availability as of this verification date;
- independently measured weight, battery life, imaging quality, display performance, durability, or thermal behavior.

These remain unknown rather than inferred from platform marketing.
