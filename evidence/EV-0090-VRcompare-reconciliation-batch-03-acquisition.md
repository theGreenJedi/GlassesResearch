Primary identity/support evidence:
- RayNeo's official firmware updater lists **RayNeo Air Plus** among supported models: https://ota.rayneo.com/

Contemporaneous acquisition evidence:
- IT Home reported the **Thunderbird Air Plus** launch on 2023-05-18 with a ¥1 reservation route and a ¥2,799 preorder price, linking to JD retail: https://www.ithome.com/0/693/482.htm

Identity decision: Thunderbird Air Plus and RayNeo Air Plus are treated as the same marketed hardware lineage/name transition unless contrary hardware evidence emerges; do not create duplicate rows for the alias pair.

Admission conclusion: preorder/commercial threshold crossed.

  - **Dream Glass 4K** at NT$13,980, future retail NT$23,580;
  - **Dream Glass 4K Plus** at NT$19,980, future retail NT$29,800.
  Source: https://www.zeczec.com/projects/dreamglass4kAR
- The same project later posted shipping progress and software updates explicitly covering **4K/4K Plus**: https://www.zeczec.com/projects/dreamglass4kAR/updates
- Japan CAMPFIRE separately offered both DreamGlass4K and DreamGlass4K Plus as reward products with stated delivery schedules: https://camp-fire.jp/projects/355376/view

Admission conclusion: both named SKUs independently crossed the preorder/purchaser threshold. They are separately priced and separately named products rather than mere color/storage bundles.

- Rokid's developer forum distinguishes Air Pro from Air and states the UXR SDK supports both, including the hardware distinction that Air Pro has cameras: https://forum.rokid.com/post/detail/365

Commercial evidence:
