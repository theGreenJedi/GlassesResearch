# EV-0087 — ASUS AirVision M1 acquisition and primary specifications

Checked: 2026-08-31

## Evidence lane

Primary manufacturer / commercial evidence.

## Identity and acquisition

ASUS identifies **ASUS AirVision M1** as a named glasses product on its U.S. product and technical-specification pages. The U.S. technical-specification page displays an **ASUS Store Price of $699.00**, establishing a manufacturer-controlled commercial route and crossing GlassesResearch's purchaser-history threshold.

Primary sources:
- https://www.asus.com/us/displays-desktops/glasses/airvision/asus-airvision-m1/
- https://www.asus.com/us/displays-desktops/glasses/airvision/asus-airvision-m1/techspec/

## Primary technical claims

The ASUS technical-specification page states, among other fields:
- dual Sony Micro OLED displays;
- 1920 × 1080 resolution per eye;
- 72 Hz refresh rate;
- 38-degree field of view;
- 58 PPD;
- native 3DoF head tracking;
- USB-C with DisplayPort Alt Mode;
- speakers and microphone array.

These are manufacturer claims. They establish product identity and documented specifications but do not by themselves establish owner control, openness, repairability, measured visual quality, software independence, or real-world wearability.

## Catalog consequence

AirVision M1 is fundamentally eyewear and is not a VR headset. It belongs in the canonical smart-glasses purchaser-history ledger as a tethered personal-display/XR-glasses product. Admission must not infer Report Card scores from manufacturer specifications alone.
