# EV-0080 — Nimbo X1 purchase state and open-platform claims

**Checked:** 2026-08-22  
**Subject:** Nimbo X1  
**Evidence lane:** project-primary commercial/campaign + team AMA + independent hands-on lead

## Project-primary surfaces

Sources:
- https://nimbopearl.com/
- https://nimbopearl.com/campaign/
- https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/nimbopearl/nimbo-x1-worlds-lightest-sic-color-display-ar-glasses

The project currently exposes a direct preorder/campaign surface for **Nimbo X1**. Nimbo describes X1 as a 49 g full-color AR product using a silicon-carbide waveguide and presents a shipping plan for backers. This is sufficient evidence that X1 is more than an announcement-only concept and appears to satisfy the purchaser-history acquisition threshold.

Project-primary claims currently include:
- 49 g product weight;
- SiC color waveguide;
- 30° field of view;
- 32 MP camera;
- 2000-nit brightness claim;
- open SDK;
- preorder/campaign acquisition route.

These remain manufacturer/project claims unless independently measured.

## Team AMA — unusually specific developer claims

Source:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/augmentedreality/comments/1vtu6j9/ama_aug_20_5_pm_pdtnimbo_x1_49g_fullcolor_ar/

The Nimbo team states that X1 is funded on Kickstarter and answers specific technical questions. Claims include:

- AOSP-based operating system;
- support for most AOSP apps;
- no general installation restriction claimed;
- system-level signing access;
- open SDK;
- low-level hardware interfaces;
- raw sensor access;
- direct camera + IMU access for custom applications;
- independent App Center;
- support for standard Bluetooth HID controllers;
- 2 GB RAM + 32 GB storage configuration;
- 640×480 physical display resolution;
- local storage plus Wi-Fi streaming;
- OpenAI-compatible API configuration allowing user-selected compatible providers/keys;
- no current eye-tracking support;
- prescription optics bonded to the waveguide and requiring factory return for replacement.

This is high-value project/team evidence because the answers expose concrete privilege and serviceability boundaries. It is **not yet reproduced owner-side evidence**.

The poster reports hands-on possession of a unit, a measured 49 g weight and observations of the physical/optical product. Treat this as independent community hands-on evidence, not a controlled laboratory measurement.

## Evidence boundary

Supported as current project state:
- Nimbo X1 is a named physical product with public project/commercial surfaces;
- an acquisition route exists through preorder/crowdfunding;
- the project publicly claims an unusually open application/developer architecture.

Still requiring verification before scoring:
- actual SDK availability, license and documentation quality;
- whether system-level signing is delivered to ordinary owners/developers;
- whether camera/IMU/raw sensors are accessible continuously and at claimed rates;
- bootloader and recovery-image access;
- firmware redistribution/replacement rights;
- whether owner-selected AI endpoints work independently of Nimbo-operated services;
- power/battery performance under realistic workloads;
- display brightness/FOV/color performance;
- repairability and prescription-lens replacement in practice.

## Research disposition

**Canonical admission warranted** because the product now exposes a paid preorder/crowdfunding route. Do not assign a high Openness, Owner Control, Hackability or Cloud Independence score merely from the openness claims; the next step is to acquire/preserve the SDK, developer documentation, licenses and shipped-owner privilege boundaries.
