# Smart-Glasses Model Profiles — Open Camera Platforms

These profiles are written downstream of completed evidence-backed Report Cards. The paragraph is the readable synthesis; the Report Card and source package remain the supporting research.

## GLS-0038 — Mentra Live

Mentra Live is one of the strongest examples in the catalog of **camera glasses that behave like an owner-controlled platform instead of a sealed AI accessory**. The 43 g frame combines a wide 119° camera, 1080p video, stereo speakers, three microphones, touch and physical controls, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, while its charging case extends the glasses beyond a simple short-session wearable. The more important distinction is software: MentraOS is MIT-licensed, developers can build their own Android or iOS host applications, and the documented APIs expose the camera, microphone, speakers, touchpad and buttons directly. Those applications can operate offline without Mentra-hosted cloud infrastructure, so visual AI can be routed to the owner’s chosen local or remote stack rather than being inseparable from one vendor assistant. That combination explains its unusually strong Report Card—**9.5/10 for Openness, Owner Control and Cloud Independence, with 9/10 Hackability**—while the lack of a HUD is correctly treated as N/A rather than a failure. Mentra Live is therefore less interesting as a finished assistant than as a lightweight, programmable first-person sensor and audio interface that the buyer is actually allowed to repurpose.
