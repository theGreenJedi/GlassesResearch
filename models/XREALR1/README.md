# ROG XREAL R1 — Research Hub

**Canonical ID:** `GLS-0213`  
**Lineage:** [XREAL / Nreal](../../lineages/XREAL.md)  
**Audit:** [Manufacturer Completeness Wave 02](../../research/investigations/MANUFACTURER_COMPLETENESS_WAVE_02_2026-09-05.md)

ROG XREAL R1 is a 2026 gaming-oriented XR-display glasses generation offered through XREAL's manufacturer preorder channel. The manufacturer positions the glasses around 240 Hz operation and sells them with a ROG Dock; the dock is a system component, not a second eyewear identity.

## Admission boundary

The preorder route crosses the GlassesResearch purchaser-history threshold. Co-branding alone would not; the named R1 eyewear hardware and manufacturer preorder identity do.

## Investigation priorities

- generic USB-C DisplayPort behavior versus ROG-specific 240 Hz workflows;
- supported resolutions/refresh modes by host;
- onboard spatial processing and firmware boundaries;
- IPD size-class implications for fit and optical usability;
- operation without the ROG Dock;
- SDK/developer access;
- owner control, cloud independence and firmware survivability;
- comparison against XREAL 1S/One/One Pro without inherited scores.
