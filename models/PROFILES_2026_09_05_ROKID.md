## GLS-0063 — Rokid AI Glasses / Style / Neo

The display-free Rokid AI-glasses family is preserved under stable ID **GLS-0063** rather than being duplicated as “Style” or “Neo.” Rokid's current security center identifies the hardware as **RV203**, while current global and regional retail pages use **Rokid AI Glasses Style**, **Rokid AI Glasses Neo**, and related naming. The product is a roughly 38.5 g camera/audio architecture with AR1/RT600-class processing, 12 MP Sony IMX681 camera, four microphones, open-ear audio, local storage, Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.3, but no in-lens display. The identity correction is stronger than the current service-survival evidence: companion-account requirements, offline residue, SDK scope, firmware access and Bolon `RV201/RV202` differences remain active research questions. [Model chapter](RokidAIStyle/README.md)

## GLS-0182 — Rokid Air Pro

**Rokid Air Pro** is a distinct 2021 glasses model rather than an Air accessory bundle. Rokid's corporate history names Air and Air Pro separately, and its own developer material supplies the decisive hardware boundary: **Air Pro has cameras; Air does not**, while Air includes myopia adjustment that Air Pro lacks. Rokid's UXR SDK addresses both devices as supported targets. That camera/optical split materially changes privacy, sensing, AR capability and serviceability, so Air Pro cannot inherit the ordinary Air report card. The exact first-party manual, regulatory identifier and generation-specific internals remain archival targets. [Model chapter](RokidAirPro/README.md)

## GLS-0183 — Rokid Max Pro

**Rokid Max Pro** is the eyewear component of the Rokid AR Studio system, paired with Station Pro. First-party AR Studio developer material gives Max Pro its own display and 6DoF/spatial characteristics, and Rokid's security center identifies hardware model **RA202**. AR Studio therefore contains a distinct glasses model plus a compute host; it is not simply ordinary Max sold in a bundle. Max Pro's useful spatial behavior is materially entangled with Station Pro/YodaOS-Master, so glasses-level cloud independence, host independence and owner control require separate testing from both ordinary Max and the complete AR Studio system. [Model chapter](RokidMaxPro/README.md)

## GLS-0184 — Rokid Glass 3

**Rokid Glass 3** is a current enterprise generation with first-party model numbers **RG301/RG303**, its own enterprise manual, and a dedicated developer workflow. Rokid documents YodaOS-Sprite Enterprise, glasses-side application execution, Android Studio/debug connection, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi/P2P messaging, media capture, OTA-related capabilities, and enterprise functions including offline recognition and work-assistance workflows. Those signals make Glass 3 an especially important owner-control and hackability research target, but they do not justify inheriting scores from Glass 2 or consumer Rokid Glasses. Regional seller pages also conflict with the first-party manual on some display details, so the manual remains the stronger evidence layer pending hands-on verification. [Model chapter](RokidGlass3/README.md)

## Adjacent disposition — Rokid X-Craft

**Rokid X-Craft** is not included in the `GLS-` count. Rokid describes it as an industrial explosion-proof AR headband/helmet-mounted system, and current B2B channels preserve separately orderable variants. Its form factor therefore belongs in the Adjacent Wearable-HCI Catalog as **ADJ-0010**, where its 5G, ATEX/industrial, sensing and enterprise-control questions can be studied without misclassifying it as ordinary eyewear.
