# Smart-Glasses Model Profile — Nimbo X1

## GLS-0166 — Nimbo X1

**Nimbo X1** is a full-color AR smart-glasses product with a documented preorder/crowdfunding acquisition route. Project material describes a 49 g silicon-carbide-waveguide design with a camera and AOSP-based software, while the Nimbo team has made unusually expansive developer-access claims including an open SDK, system-level signing, direct camera/IMU access and user-selectable OpenAI-compatible API endpoints. Those are promising project/team claims, not owner-side verification, so this profile does not infer Openness, Owner Control, Hackability or Cloud Independence scores from them.

Nimbo X1 remains distinct from **NIMO Holo-Optical Glasses**; similar naming is not evidence of shared identity or lineage. Canonical admission records purchaser-history status only and leaves unverified technical fields unknown until artifacts or reproduced testing support them.
