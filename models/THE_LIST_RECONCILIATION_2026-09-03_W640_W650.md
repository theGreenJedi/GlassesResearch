# Canonical reconciliation — HeyCyan W640 / W650

Date: 2026-09-03

This packet resolves two previously queued HeyCyan sibling platforms. Both are independently named and commercially offered by Goodway, an OEM/ODM supplier already represented in the W630 lineage research. Shared HeyCyan software and overlapping silicon do not make these devices W610/W630 aliases when their physical platform, power, charging, controls, and documented product identities differ.

## Identity decision

**W640** is not merely a seller label for canonical W630 or W610. Goodway publishes it as a separately named production product with a 35 g chassis, JL7018F + Allwinner V821L2 architecture, Sony 8 MP camera, 290 mAh battery, IP66 protection, USB-C charging, dual physical buttons plus sliding touch control, wear detection, and 3D six-axis + EIS stabilization. W630 shares some silicon and battery characteristics but is separately documented with magnetic charging and a different product configuration. The overlap supports common platform lineage, not identity collapse.

**W650** is more clearly distinct. Goodway publishes it as a separate production-ready base model with a 38 g chassis, 220 mAh battery, 3C fast charging through a magnetic two-pin connector, Bluetooth 5.3, dual microphones, 1080p multimedia capture, and HeyCyan. Goodway states no minimum-order requirement on the W650 product page and separately lists samples as available for camera-glasses base models.

The highest allocated canonical identifier before this packet is `GLS-0168`, therefore W640 and W650 receive `GLS-0169` and `GLS-0170`.

## Evidence boundary

Admission establishes product identity and documented acquisition route only. It does not infer that Goodway is the sole original designer/manufacturer of every W640/W650 sold under other labels, nor that every marketplace listing with those model numbers is genuine.

Shared HeyCyan software, JL7018F/V821-family silicon, AI claims, translation claims, or visual similarity do not establish rebrand equivalence. Retail aliases should be routed into these canonical identities only after model-level evidence supports the match.

No Openness, Owner Control, Cloud Independence, Hackability, camera-quality, battery-life, privacy, or durability score is promoted by this admission. Manufacturer specifications remain primary/commercial evidence pending hands-on verification where appropriate.
