## Evidence boundary

These admissions establish **product identity and acquisition history**, not complete generation-specific technical truth. No report-card scores are inherited from neighboring models. The companion editorial profiles intentionally preserve open questions for optics, firmware, cloud/service behavior, regulatory identifiers, regional SKUs and owner-control properties.
