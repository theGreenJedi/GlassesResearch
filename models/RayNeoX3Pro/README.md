# RayNeo X3 Pro

**GLS-0066** is RayNeo's 2025 binocular full-color AI/AR smart-glasses platform.

RayNeo's current product documentation specifies a binocular MicroLED display using diffractive waveguides, 640×480 resolution, a 30° field of view, approximately 3,500-nit average and 6,000-nit peak brightness, and a 76 ± 1 g frame. The compute platform is Qualcomm Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1 with 4 GB RAM and 32 GB storage.

Imaging uses a dual-camera arrangement: a 12 MP Sony IMX681 RGB camera plus an OV spatial camera used for spatial positioning. RayNeo lists three microphones with narrow beamforming/noise cancellation, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.2, USB 2.0, a 245 mAh battery, accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, ambient-light sensing, and wear detection.

The glasses run RayNeo AIOS and advertise Gemini/Gemini Live integration, real-time translation, navigation, reminders, and spatial applications. RayNeo also advertises a developer-oriented Creator Mode. The manufacturer provides prescription-lens support through lens partners.
