## Identity decision

**MYVU Air / StarV Air (XGA010C)** is a distinct commercially released product. Meizu's first-party specifications identify a 43 g binocular MicroLED display product with dual 1280×480 resolution, 30° FOV, 183 mAh battery, USB-C, Flyme AR and diffractive-waveguide optics. The official manual independently names model **XGA010C** and records the same 183 mAh battery class and regulatory identity.

**StarV Air2** is a materially different named generation rather than a seller alias. Meizu's first-party specifications identify a 44 g product with 640×480 display, 204 mAh battery, BT 5.2, Flyme XR 2.0 and high-refractive-index tempered-glass optics. The product page further identifies a 0.15 cc single-green MicroLED light engine and new scroll-wheel interaction. These differences are sufficient to keep Air2 distinct from XGA010C even before teardown-level lineage work.

The highest existing canonical allocation on current `main` is `GLS-0166`, so these products receive the next stable identifiers `GLS-0167` and `GLS-0168`.
