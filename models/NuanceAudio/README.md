# Nuance Audio family — generation research

**Manufacturer:** EssilorLuxottica / Nuance Audio  
**Evidence lane:** manufacturer-primary product and support material  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-23

## Generation 1 — Nuance Audio

Nuance Audio is hearing-assistance eyewear for mild-to-moderate hearing loss. It integrates directional microphones and open-ear speakers into conventional-looking prescription-ready eyewear. The manufacturer currently preserves the original generation as a distinct product rather than silently replacing it with Plus.

### Documented product surface

- Square and Panthos frame families are documented by the manufacturer.
- Prescription and non-prescription lens configurations are offered.
- Manufacturer support documents a charging case/pad ecosystem and app/remote control.
- Manufacturer support distinguishes the original power behavior from Plus: opening the temples does not by itself complete power-on; the multifunction button is used, and closing the temples powers the glasses down.
- Hearing performance, comfort, battery endurance, and speech-in-noise results remain manufacturer/clinical claims unless independently reproduced.

## Generation 2 — Nuance Audio Plus

Nuance Audio Plus is a separately named second-generation product, not merely a color or lens package. Manufacturer material documents stronger amplification, improved clarity in noise, more natural own-voice perception, increased battery capacity, new physical power and volume controls, and an expanded style/color range.

### Documented changes from Gen 1

- Square, Panthos, and new Upturn frame styles.
- Physical slider power control and temple volume buttons.
- Fold-to-sleep / unfold-to-wake behavior when the power slider remains on.
- Manufacturer states enhanced all-day battery life; support material distinguishes charging time from the original generation.
- Double-tap call answer/end support is documented for compatible iPhones (iOS 16+); manufacturer notes this call feature is tied to MFi/iPhone support.
- Hearing-assistance remains the central function. This is not a camera/display general-purpose AI-glasses platform.

## Lineage disposition

GlassesResearch treats these as two generations in one manufacturer family:

**EssilorLuxottica → Nuance Audio family → Nuance Audio (Gen 1) → Nuance Audio Plus (Gen 2)**

Gen 1 must remain visible in the historical record. Plus does not rewrite or erase the predecessor.

## Report Card implications

Both generations warrant separate Report Card/model treatment. Hardware, Wearability, Software, Owner Control, Cloud Independence, Value, and serviceability claims should be scored only where evidence supports a score. Visual AI is not a relevant capability claim for this camera-free hearing product and should not be inflated through category mismatch.
