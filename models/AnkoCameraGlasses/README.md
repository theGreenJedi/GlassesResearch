# Anko Camera Glasses

**Status:** documented retail product  
**Brand / retailer:** Anko / Kmart Australia  
**First documented retail availability:** 2026  
**Category:** camera/audio smart glasses  
**Software ecosystem:** HeyCyan

## What is established

Kmart Australia sold Anko-branded camera glasses for AUD $89 in 2026. Contemporary reporting documented that the clear-lens, black-rimmed glasses could capture still images and record high-definition video, and that they sold out across Kmart stores and online.

The same reporting states that the glasses run the **HeyCyan** software platform. Through that software the glasses support music playback, calls, an AI assistant, and photo/video transfer.

This places Anko inside the wider low-cost HeyCyan ecosystem already represented elsewhere in GlassesResearch. It does **not**, by itself, prove that the Anko hardware is identical to the W610 or establish the underlying ODM. Hardware equivalence should only be asserted when component, firmware, regulatory, or hands-on evidence supports it.

## Why this model matters

The Anko product demonstrates how smart-glasses platforms can appear under mass-market retail brands rather than only specialist eyewear or technology companies. Tracking these products helps connect retail names to underlying applications, software platforms, OEMs, regulatory records, and compatible community tools.
