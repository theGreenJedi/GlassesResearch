# Editorial Profiles — Manufacturer Completeness Wave 02

These profiles establish model identity, architecture class and investigation priorities. They intentionally do **not** inherit report-card scores from neighboring products.

## GLS-0195 — Vuzix M920

Legacy monocular wearable-display family sold by Vuzix from 2003 through 2009. Preserve it as the pre-Tac-Eye monocular branch; investigate exact subvariants, mounting form, inputs and surviving manuals before scoring.

## GLS-0196 — Vuzix AV230 XL

Consumer binocular Video Eyewear documented as a current Vuzix product by 2009. Its eyeglass-temple display architecture makes it a direct historical predecessor to modern tethered display glasses; recover exact introduction year and host interfaces.

## GLS-0197 — Vuzix AV310 widescreen

Distinct widescreen binocular Video Eyewear identity in Vuzix's pre-Wrap consumer line. Preserve model-specific resolution/host evidence separately from AV230 XL and AV920.

## GLS-0198 — Vuzix AV920

Higher-resolution consumer binocular display eyewear sold in the iWear-branded era. It also served as the basis of a low-vision bundle, but that bundle does not create a separate canonical electronics identity.

## GLS-0199 — Vuzix VR920

Tracked binocular VR/video eyewear from Vuzix's 2007-era consumer branch, with USB/video host dependence and developer software support. Do not transfer later Wrap tracking or camera behavior onto this record.

## GLS-0200 — Vuzix Wrap 230

Entry Wrap-generation video eyewear introduced in the 2009 sunglass-styled family. Research priority: exact retail window, connection kit and whether any region-specific suffixes represent hardware changes.

## GLS-0201 — Vuzix Wrap 310XL

Widescreen Wrap-generation consumer display eyewear. Treat as a separate named hardware tier rather than a cosmetic option because Vuzix differentiates native resolution and display package.

## GLS-0202 — Vuzix Wrap 920

VGA Wrap-generation video eyewear sold as a consumer mobile display. Keep host compatibility and optical behavior generation-specific.

## GLS-0203 — Vuzix Wrap 920VR

VR-focused Wrap 920 variant with integrated head tracking and PC host dependence. The tracking package is a material functional distinction from the plain Wrap 920.

## GLS-0204 — Vuzix Wrap 920AR

Camera-equipped tracked AR member of the Wrap 920 family. Stereo cameras and AR-specific host behavior make it materially different from both Wrap 920 and 920VR.

## GLS-0205 — Vuzix Wrap 1200

Higher-resolution consumer Wrap eyewear sold by 2011/2012. Investigate exact analog/component/VGA accessory behavior and surviving prescription/focus options before grading.

## GLS-0206 — Vuzix Wrap 1200VR

Tracked VR member of the 1200 generation. Preserve it independently from the plain 1200 and later HDMI DX generation.

## GLS-0207 — Vuzix STAR 1200

See-through AR Video Eyewear for developer/research use, with transparent displays and sensor/camera functions. This is an early direct ancestor of Vuzix's later waveguide smart-glasses work.

## GLS-0208 — Vuzix Wrap 1200DX

HDMI-era refresh of Wrap 1200 consumer display eyewear. The digital-input/HDCP generation boundary is sufficiently material to preserve as a named marketed generation.

## GLS-0209 — Vuzix Wrap 1200DX VR

Tracked VR version of the HDMI-era 1200DX platform. Research should separate integrated tracking behavior from host-side software requirements.

## GLS-0210 — Vuzix Wrap 1200DX-AR

HDMI/USB AR eyewear with stereo cameras and tracking. Vuzix sold DX AR models directly even when broader retail channels did not carry them.

## GLS-0211 — Vuzix STAR 1200DX

Second-generation see-through STAR AR Video Eyewear with updated camera/sensor package. Preserve developer-oriented acquisition and software evidence independently from STAR 1200.

## GLS-0212 — Vuzix M400-C

Host-driven enterprise monocular using the M400 physical/camera/display form while moving compute to a USB-C host. That compute-boundary change is central to Owner Control, Cloud Independence and Hackability and prevents score inheritance from M400.

## GLS-0213 — ROG XREAL R1

2026 preorder gaming/XR glasses co-branded by ROG and XREAL, marketed around 240 Hz operation. The included ROG Dock is a system accessory; the glasses are the canonical identity. Investigate generic DisplayPort behavior separately from ROG-specific high-refresh workflows.

## GLS-0214 — Solos Smart Glasses — first HUD generation

Original 2016 Solos/Kopin sport HUD eyewear, offered for preorder and later shipped. Phone/ANT+/Bluetooth-hosted performance data and the pupil-scale see-through display distinguish it sharply from later display-free AirGo glasses.

## GLS-0215 — Solos Smart Glasses — second HUD generation

2018 redesign explicitly described by Kopin as a new generation, expanding the original cyclist HUD into running, richer audio and group communication. Preserve optical and software differences from the first HUD generation.

## GLS-0216 — Solos AirGo 1

First display-free AirGo generation, documented by the 2020 crowdfunding program and 2021 manual. Interchangeable frames/temples and phone-hosted assistant/fitness functions establish the architectural pivot that precedes canonical AirGo 2.

## GLS-0217 — Solos AirGo A5

Current fifth-generation Solos Audio AI Smartglasses. Manufacturer retail evidence establishes the named generation, while AI behavior, local/cloud boundaries and compatibility with earlier AirGo software remain model-specific investigation questions.
