# Solos AirGo A5 — Research Hub

**Canonical ID:** `GLS-0217`  
**Lineage:** [Solos](../../lineages/SOLOS.md)  
**Audit:** [Manufacturer Completeness Wave 02](../../research/investigations/MANUFACTURER_COMPLETENESS_WAVE_02_2026-09-05.md)

Solos currently identifies AirGo A5 as its **5th Generation of Solos Audio AI Smartglasses** and exposes a manufacturer purchase/backorder path. That generation language and acquisition route make A5 a canonical eyewear identity rather than another frame style.

## Investigation priorities

- identify the exact electronics platform and regulatory model number;
- compare Bluetooth/audio/microphone hardware against AirGo V2 and earlier AirGo generations;
- map companion application, account and AI-provider dependencies;
- determine local functionality when network/cloud services are unavailable;
- examine SDK/API compatibility and whether older AirGo developer interfaces survive;
- verify battery, controls, prescription/lens handling and frame interchangeability hands-on where possible;
- score owner control and cloud independence independently rather than inheriting family reputation.
