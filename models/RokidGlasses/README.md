# Rokid Glasses — GLS-0064

Display-equipped AI smart glasses from Rokid, launched globally in 2025.

## Hardware

Rokid's current primary documentation specifies:

- approximately 49 g weight;
- dual-eye monochrome MicroLED waveguide display;
- 480 × 640 resolution per eye;
- 30° field of view;
- up to 1500 nits brightness;
- Qualcomm Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1 plus NXP RT600;
- 2 GB RAM and 32 GB storage;
- 210 mAh battery;
- Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.3;
- 12 MP Sony IMX681 camera;
- four-microphone directional array;
- dual open-ear speakers;
- IPX4 splash resistance.

Rokid documents prescription-lens support through an official prescription-frame/lens service.

## Software and functions

The Hi Rokid companion app manages setup, media, settings, AI services and updates. Primary documentation describes real-time translation, captions, teleprompter functions, navigation prompts, camera capture and multimodal AI workflows.

Rokid also publishes an official terminal/glasses SDK covering device-side capabilities including media capture, voice, recognition, messaging, device state, Bluetooth and P2P.
