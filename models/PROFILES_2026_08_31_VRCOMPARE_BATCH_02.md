# Smart-Glasses Model Profiles — VRcompare reconciliation batch 02

These profiles summarize four purchaser-history admissions proposed by the 2026-08-31 VRcompare reconciliation. They deliberately preserve Unknown where only manufacturer or commercial-channel claims exist.

## GLS-0169 — Rokid Max Pro (RA202)

**Rokid Max Pro** is the glasses component of Rokid AR Studio and is materially distinct from Rokid Max and Max 2. Rokid's own security register identifies model RA202, while its AR Studio material documents a 6DoF host-driven AR architecture. A first-party European B2B storefront establishes a commercial AR Studio route. Admission therefore records a real sold product identity, not a transfer of scores from neighboring Rokid display glasses. ## GLS-0170 — HUAWEI Vision Glass

**HUAWEI Vision Glass** is a host-connected dual-display viewing-glasses product, separate from Huawei's audio-eyewear line. Huawei maintains the product/specification page; contemporaneous December 2022 reporting records the first Vmall retail sale after the historical storefront became difficult to retrieve. The admission establishes purchaser history and model identity only; measured optics, comfort, owner control and service survival remain evidence-dependent. [Evidence](/evidence/EV-0089-VRcompare-reconciliation-batch-02-primary-acquisition/) · [reconciliation](/models/THE_LIST_RECONCILIATION_2026-08-31_VRCOMPARE_BATCH_02/)

## GLS-0171 — Lenovo Glasses T1

**Lenovo Glasses T1**, support identity **AR-6561Y**, is a host-driven wearable display. Lenovo's own material directs buyers to regional Lenovo representatives and maintains product warranty/manual support. Lenovo also identifies **Lenovo Yoga Glasses** as the China-market name of T1; that name is treated as an alias rather than a second canonical model absent evidence of materially different hardware. ## GLS-0172 — DigiLens ARGO

**DigiLens ARGO** is standalone enterprise AR smartglasses. DigiLens' own product and legal materials establish the product identity and explicitly contemplate contracts covering sale of an ARGO headset, while a current XR integrator publishes a concrete order path and price. These facts establish commercial existence; they do not by themselves establish display quality, wearability, repairability, openness, cloud independence or value.
