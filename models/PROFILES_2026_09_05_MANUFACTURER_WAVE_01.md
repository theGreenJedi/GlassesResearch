# Smart-Glasses Model Profiles — Manufacturer Completeness Wave 01

These profiles accompany the 2026-09-05 whole-manufacturer audit. Admission proves a distinct purchasable/obtainable product identity; it does not authorize specification or score inheritance from neighboring generations.

## GLS-0185 — RayNeo iO

**RayNeo iO** crossed from release-tracker status into the canonical catalog when RayNeo's first-party store opened active checkout after the September 4, 2026 availability milestone. RayNeo positions iO as unusually light everyday display eyewear rather than an X-series standalone AR computer: current product material describes a roughly 33 g frame, transparent monochrome information display, AI/translation/meeting/teleprompter functions and prescription ordering. The direct purchase route is clear; the next research job is separating local display/control residue from phone, account and cloud-service dependencies, then identifying the exact wireless, firmware, storage and developer boundaries rather than importing them from X2/X3 Pro. [RayNeo lineage](../lineages/RAYNEO_TCL.md) · [first-party store](https://www.rayneo.com/products/rayneo-io-ai-glasses)

## GLS-0186 — RayNeo GT

**RayNeo GT** is a current host-driven personal-display generation with active manufacturer checkout. RayNeo's own GT comparison describes a 46° field of view, approximately 68 g weight, 120 Hz display behavior, quad-speaker audio and native 3DoF through its Zone 360 tracking chip. Those first-party claims establish why GT is not merely a renamed Air-family bundle, but they remain manufacturer claims pending independent optical and interoperability work. Its likely owner-control advantage comes from display-peripheral use with user-selected hosts; firmware, 3DoF processing, DRM/compatibility and accessory dependencies still require generation-specific testing. [RayNeo lineage](../lineages/RAYNEO_TCL.md) · [GT series](https://www.rayneo.com/pages/rayneo-gt-series-ar-glasses)

## GLS-0187 — RayNeo GT Max

**RayNeo GT Max** is sold beside GT as separate eyewear, not simply a larger accessory pack. RayNeo gives Max its own 59° field of view, approximately 76 g weight, larger virtual-display modes and three precision IPD size classes, while retaining the GT family's native 3DoF/display role. That hardware-level distinction plus separate pricing and checkout supports a canonical identity. GlassesResearch should now verify eyebox/IPD behavior, host compatibility, optical edge clarity, 3DoF persistence, firmware ownership and whether the larger-FOV design imposes comfort or prescription compromises. [RayNeo lineage](../lineages/RAYNEO_TCL.md) · [GT series](https://www.rayneo.com/pages/rayneo-gt-series-ar-glasses)

## GLS-0188 — VITURE Pro 2

**VITURE Pro 2** is VITURE's 2026 fifth-anniversary successor in the Pro branch, actively sold by the manufacturer rather than a cosmetic refresh inferred from marketing. First-party material describes UltraClarity 3.0 optics, 50° FOV, claimed 1600-nit brightness, 0 to -5.0D built-in diopter adjustment and an approximately 63 g, slimmer/lighter chassis. Those differences establish the product identity but do not automatically justify carrying forward Pro's report-card scores. NDI should verify optical performance, electrochromic behavior if applicable to the exact SKU, generic DisplayPort fallback, SpaceWalker dependence, firmware behavior and serviceability. [VITURE lineage](../lineages/VITURE.md) · [Pro 2](https://www.viture.com/pro2)

## GLS-0189 — Epson Moverio BT-350

**Moverio BT-350** is a distinct commercial/enterprise smart-glasses generation that had been hidden inside the site's broader Epson coverage. Epson identifies model `V11H837020` and describes a multi-user Moverio OS platform with onboard Intel Atom compute, camera, sensors, wireless connectivity, storage, fleet-management support and an SDK/developer environment. That makes BT-350 closer to the standalone BT-300 branch than the later passive/tethered monitor products, but generation-specific Android/Moverio OS support and owner-control behavior must be researched independently. Its surviving first-party product page makes both identity and commercial role unusually well preserved for discontinued enterprise eyewear. [Epson lineage](../lineages/EPSON_MOVERIO.md) · [first-party product](https://epson.com/For-Work/Wearables/Smart-Glasses/Moverio-BT-350-Smart-Glasses/p/V11H837020)

## GLS-0190 — Epson Moverio BT-30E

**Moverio BT-30E** is a separate 2018 monitor-model smart-glasses product, not an alias for BT-35E. Epson Japan records a November 2018 release date and a dedicated firmware history; Epson technical material jointly supports BT-30E/BT-35E while distinguishing their monitor-model architecture from earlier Android-integrated BT-300/350 and later direct USB-C generations. The canonical admission intentionally avoids borrowing BT-35E's ruggedization, camera/interface or mechanical details. Research should recover original retail pricing, exact optical/mechanical specification, firmware packages and host-compatibility behavior for BT-30E itself. [Epson lineage](../lineages/EPSON_MOVERIO.md) · [Epson support](https://www.epson.jp/support/portal/hoshu/bt-30e.htm)

## GLS-0191 — Lucyd Loud 1.0

**Lucyd Loud 1.0** restores the beginning of Lucyd's commercial smart-eyewear history. In its own 2020 retrospective, Lucyd calls Loud 1.0 its first Bluetooth audio glasses and says approximately 800 units were made and sold, mostly in 2018, with prescription lens options. That is direct manufacturer evidence of a real retail generation, not an announced concept. Surviving evidence is not yet deep enough to grade battery, codec, controls, radio hardware, repairability or service survival, so the profile preserves the acquisition fact without reverse-filling later Loud or Lyte specifications. [Lucyd lineage](../lineages/LUCYD.md) · [manufacturer history](https://lucyd.co/blogs/news/july-2020-corporate-update)

## GLS-0192 — Lucyd Loud — second edition

Lucyd's **second edition of Loud** is preserved using the manufacturer's own wording rather than the invented shorthand “Loud 2.0.” Lucyd says this generation was designed in-house, built in Q1 2019, offered in numerous colors/patterns and remained available on Lucyd.co in July 2020; the smaller Slim frame name was later changed to Youth. The combination of successor-generation language, in-house redesign and continued sale supports a distinct canonical product generation while the multiple frame treatments remain styles within it. Hardware internals and exact model numbers remain archival targets. [Lucyd lineage](../lineages/LUCYD.md) · [manufacturer history](https://lucyd.co/blogs/news/july-2020-corporate-update)

## GLS-0193 — Lucyd Loud 2020

**Lucyd Loud 2020** is the next explicitly named electronics generation in Lucyd's first-party chronology. The company states that availability of a Bluetooth 5.0 chipset led it to develop Loud 2020 in seven styles and launch the generation in January 2020. This is strong generation evidence, but the seven styles are not seven GLS records. The research queue is to recover manuals, battery/audio/control specifications and identify which later Lucyd products reused or replaced this platform before Lyte arrived. [Lucyd lineage](../lineages/LUCYD.md) · [manufacturer history](https://lucyd.co/blogs/news/july-2020-corporate-update)

## GLS-0194 — INMO X AI+Camera Glasses

**INMO X AI+Camera Glasses** enter the canonical ledger from INMO's own product/history record rather than from a marketplace inference. INMO says its X branch focuses on photography, lists “INMO X AI+Camera Glasses” as a November 2024 product, and states that the first blind-order batch for the November 2024 releases exceeded 10,000 units. That supports a marketed/preorder identity. It does **not** prove that this device is identical to the earlier 5G INMO X-series hardware demonstrated in May 2021, so that relationship remains unresolved. Model-specific camera, display, compute, Android/ADB, cloud, privacy and owner-control evidence is required before scoring. [INMO lineage](../lineages/INMO.md) · [manufacturer history](https://www.inmoxr.com/pages/about-us)
