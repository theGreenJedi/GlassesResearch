# AR Compare Completeness Admissions — Editorial Profiles

These profiles accompany the 2026-09-08 canonical admissions. They establish conservative evidence boundaries; none is a GlassesResearch hands-on evaluation.

## GLS-0233 — Almer Arc 2

Almer Arc 2 is enterprise augmented-reality eyewear offered through Almer's industrial remote-assistance program. The manufacturer exposes an order/demo route and subscription procurement, which is sufficient for purchaser-history admission. Treat detailed optical, battery, compute, and software behavior as manufacturer claims until independently reproduced. Source: https://almer.com/almer-home-armazing2/

## GLS-0234 — DigiLens ARGO

DigiLens ARGO is a standalone enterprise AR smart-glasses platform marketed for industrial deployment. Current first-party product material and commercial deployment/procurement evidence establish a real obtainable product rather than a reference design or concept. GlassesResearch has not hands-on validated its optical performance, runtime, tracking, software openness, or owner-control behavior. Source: https://www.digilens.com/argo/

## GLS-0235 — Dreamworld Dream Glass Flow

Dream Glass Flow is a commercial AR/XR display-glasses identity in Dreamworld's DreamGlass family. Dreamworld presents Flow as AR smart glasses and provides a manufacturer order path; that acquisition evidence supports canonical inclusion. Specifications and host compatibility should remain manufacturer-attributed until independently tested. Source: https://www.dreamworldvision.com/pages/root0

## GLS-0236 — Dreamworld Dream Glass Lead Pro

Dream Glass Lead Pro is the externally identified commercial Lead-family AR smart-glasses product. Manufacturer material establishes the family and an order route, but the historical boundary between names such as Lead and Lead Pro is not yet strong enough to justify multiple canonical generations. GlassesResearch therefore admits one conservative Lead Pro identity and leaves any additional split to future lineage evidence. Source: https://www.dreamworldvision.com/pages/root0

## GLS-0237 — INAIR 2 Pro

INAIR 2 Pro is commercially offered XR display eyewear sold by INAIR as part of its current spatial-computing system. The manufacturer/store route establishes the glasses as a purchasable eyewear component rather than merely a host or accessory. Display, tracking, host dependence, software behavior, and owner-control characteristics remain unverified by GlassesResearch hands-on testing. Source: https://inairspace.com/products/inair-2-pro-set
