# Razer Anzu

**GLS-0018** is Razer's 2021 audio-eyewear platform. It combines open-ear audio with interchangeable blue-light-filtering and polarized sunglass lenses.

Razer's current support documentation lists two 16 mm dynamic drivers, two omnidirectional microphones, Bluetooth 5.2 with SBC/AAC support, and approximately five hours of battery life. Weight varies by frame shape and size from roughly 43 to 48 g. The glasses use touch controls and the Razer Audio mobile app, and Razer documented a low-latency gaming mode alongside ordinary Bluetooth audio operation.

The frame does not contain a visual display or camera. Replacement lenses were part of the original product design, and Razer's launch documentation explicitly positioned the lenses as interchangeable. Current support retains manuals, compliance material, pairing guidance, lens-replacement instructions, and technical specifications even though Anzu is now a legacy product.
