# Brilliant Labs Halo

**GLS-0052** is Brilliant Labs' open-source smart-glasses platform announced in 2025.

Brilliant describes Halo as an open hardware-and-software platform with a color micro-OLED display, two ultra-compact bone-conduction speakers, two microphones with audio-activity detection, a 6-axis IMU with tap detection, and an ultra-low-power optical sensor for AI inference. Connectivity is Bluetooth 5.3. The compute platform is an Alif Balletto B1 with a Cortex-M55 CPU and NPU for on-device AI workloads.

The software stack is based on ZephyrOS with a Lua interface, a cross-platform mobile application, and a cloud AI agent. Brilliant advertises roughly 14 hours of battery life and offers prescription and sunglass lens options. The company's own positioning explicitly emphasizes open-source hardware and software, making Halo notable as a developer-oriented platform rather than only a closed consumer assistant.
