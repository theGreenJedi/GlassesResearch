# HUAWEI Eyewear 2

**GLS-0021** is Huawei's 2023 audio-eyewear generation. It is a display-less open-ear audio platform offered in multiple optical and sunglass frame styles.

Huawei's global specifications list Bluetooth 5.3, dual-device Bluetooth support, wear detection, accelerometer/gyroscope/capacitance sensing, and two co-directional dual-diaphragm dynamic audio units. The product is rated IP54. Battery capacity is specified as 110 mAh minimum per temple, with advertised operation of up to 11 hours of audio playback or 9 hours of voice calls. Charging uses a USB-C charging converter with magnetic contacts to the eyewear.

Weight depends on frame configuration. Huawei lists approximately 36.6–43.0 g including lenses across the published Rectangle Semi-Frame, Browline, Square Sunglasses, and Round Eyewear configurations. The product supports touch gestures, voice-assistant activation, and Huawei-specific quick-pair behavior on compatible EMUI/HarmonyOS devices while retaining standard Bluetooth connectivity.
