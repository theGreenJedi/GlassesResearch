## Identity decision

Alibaba launched the hardware in China in December 2025 as **Quark AI Glasses S1** and **Quark AI Glasses G1**. At MWC Barcelona on 2026-03-04, Alibaba presented the same two product series under the unified **Qwen Glasses** brand, retaining the S1 and G1 designations. The March announcement says official China sales began 2026-03-08.

GlassesResearch therefore treats:

- **Quark AI Glasses S1** as the former/launch name of **Qwen Glasses S1 / GLS-0161**;
- **Quark AI Glasses G1** as the former/launch name of **Qwen Glasses G1 / GLS-0162**;
- **Qwen** as the current brand/platform identity, not a third glasses model or new hardware generation.

The two series remain separate canonical hardware records. Alibaba's launch material distinguishes the S1 as the flagship **dual-display** model and the G1 as the **camera-first, no-display** everyday model.

## Evidence boundary

Primary Alibaba material supports a real China retail route, S1/G1 identity, Qwen App integration, camera/audio/AI functions, the S1 display distinction, the G1 40 g claim, and a stated MCP-compatible developer direction. These sources do **not** establish unrestricted device ownership, public firmware access, bootloader/recovery access, cloud independence, or the exact local-versus-cloud execution boundary for each AI function.

Those dimensions remain unknown unless separately evidenced. No Report Card score is inherited between S1 and G1 merely because Alibaba says they share core hardware outside the display distinction.
