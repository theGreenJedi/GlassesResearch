# Solos Historical Smart Glasses — Research Hub

**Canonical IDs:** `GLS-0214`–`GLS-0216`  
**Lineage:** [Solos](../../lineages/SOLOS.md)  
**Audit:** [Manufacturer Completeness Wave 02](../../research/investigations/MANUFACTURER_COMPLETENESS_WAVE_02_2026-09-05.md)

The whole-manufacturer audit recovered the missing bridge between Solos' original sport-HUD eyewear and the existing AirGo records.

## `GLS-0214` — Solos Smart Glasses, first HUD generation

The original Solos/Kopin performance glasses crossed the acquisition threshold through a 2016 preorder route and later completed first-generation shipment. Its see-through HUD makes it architecturally distinct from later screen-free AirGo products.

## `GLS-0215` — Solos Smart Glasses, second HUD generation

Kopin explicitly called the 2018 redesign a new generation, announced $499 availability and later reported shipments. It remains a display/HUD product and must not inherit AirGo audio-glasses assumptions.

## `GLS-0216` — AirGo 1

The first AirGo generation is preserved by the 2020 crowdfunding program and a surviving 2021 Solos manual. It marks the move to display-free Bluetooth smart glasses with interchangeable frame/temple concepts and phone-hosted functions.

## Investigation priorities

- recover exact model numbers and regulatory records for both HUD generations;
- determine what computation occurred on-glasses versus phone/host;
- map ANT+/Bluetooth/audio/display behavior on the HUD generations;
- preserve AirGo 1 firmware/app history;
- establish whether early AirGo frame names are cosmetics or electronics revisions;
- score each generation independently.
