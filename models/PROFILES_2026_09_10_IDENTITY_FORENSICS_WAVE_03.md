## GLS-0241 — RayNeo / Thunderbird Air 1S

**Air 1S** is a distinct 2022 consumer XR-display generation in the Thunderbird/RayNeo personal-display lineage. RayNeo's own launch announcement calls it a new generation, lists CNY 2,499 pricing and states that paid presales opened on JD and Tmall. The same first-party material describes changes from the earlier Air in the Sony MicroOLED generation, audio system, chassis/ergonomics, controls, 2D/3D switching and detachable magnetic cable. RayNeo's current browser-based firmware updater continues to recognize Air 1S as a supported device identity separate from Air Plus, Air 2 and later generations.

This profile establishes identity and acquisition only. Display performance, audio quality, certification claims, host interoperability and firmware behavior remain manufacturer-sourced until independently reproduced.

Sources: [RayNeo Air 1S launch](https://www.rayneo.cn/xinwen/352.html) · [RayNeo firmware updater](https://ota.rayneo.com/)

## GLS-0242 — RayNeo / Thunderbird Air Plus

**Air Plus** is a distinct 2023 host-driven XR-display generation. A surviving RayNeo product page names the glasses, provides a direct purchase route, and documents the Air Plus-specific product surface. RayNeo's current OTA service lists Air Plus separately from Air 1S, Air 2, Air 2s and later products. TCL Electronics' 2023 interim filing independently records that the Group launched the upgraded Air Plus XR smart glasses in the PRC during the first half of 2023.

The surviving manufacturer page describes a 49° field of view, 60/120 Hz modes, Sony MicroOLED display architecture and magnetic prescription-lens attachment. Those are manufacturer claims, not GlassesResearch measurements. RayNeo Pocket is treated as a host/accessory rather than part of the eyewear identity.

Sources: [RayNeo Air Plus product page](https://test.rayneo.cn/airplus/) · [RayNeo firmware updater](https://ota.rayneo.com/) · [TCL Electronics 2023 interim report](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2023/0825/2023082500287.pdf)

## GLS-0243 — MAD Gaze GLOW

**MAD Gaze GLOW** is a legacy tethered AR/MR smart-glasses product that crossed purchaser history through paid crowdfunding. MAD Gaze's 2019 Kickstarter raised funds from hundreds of backers and preserves shipping, warranty, SDK and host-support statements for the GLOW family. A subsequent Japanese CAMPFIRE campaign offered GLOW as its own paid reward tier with a product-specific price and delivery window.

GLOW depends on a host rather than containing its own general-purpose compute platform. Preserved GLOW-family documentation describes cameras, microphone, speakers, sensors and developer access, but the exact behavior and survival of those interfaces today are not independently verified here.

GLOW must not be collapsed into GLOW Plus. Preserved product/support material distinguishes the base model's optical/display configuration and 3D-content capability from the Plus model.

Sources: [MAD Gaze Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/madgaze/mad-gaze-glow-lightweight-and-stylish-mr-glasses-for-you) · [Kickstarter FAQ](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/madgaze/mad-gaze-glow-lightweight-and-stylish-mr-glasses-for-you/faqs) · [CAMPFIRE paid GLOW reward](https://camp-fire.jp/projects/248361/view)

## GLS-0244 — MAD Gaze GLOW Plus

**MAD Gaze GLOW Plus** is a separately marketed legacy tethered AR/MR smart-glasses configuration, not merely a color or accessory bundle for GLOW. The Japanese CAMPFIRE campaign offered GLOW Plus through separately priced paid reward tiers with its own delivery window, while MAD Gaze's Kickstarter FAQ names both GLOW and GLOW Plus and preserves shipping/warranty statements for the family.

Preserved product/support material distinguishes GLOW Plus from GLOW by optical architecture, field of view, brightness/power behavior and 3D-content support. Contemporary documentation commonly describes the Plus as a roughly 53° / 1080p configuration versus the base model's smaller/lower-resolution display. These remain provenance-bound historical specifications rather than current lab measurements.

Sources: [CAMPFIRE paid GLOW Plus rewards](https://camp-fire.jp/projects/248361/view) · [MAD Gaze Kickstarter FAQ](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/madgaze/mad-gaze-glow-lightweight-and-stylish-mr-glasses-for-you/faqs) · [MAD Gaze Japan model-difference note](https://note.com/madgaze_japan/n/n664d9a804036)

## Adjacent — ADJ-0017 RealMax Qian

**RealMax Qian** belongs in the adjacent wearable-HCI catalog rather than the eyewear-only `GLS-` count. RealMax's own surviving material calls Qian a standalone AR headset, and its integrated wide-FOV compute/optical assembly is designed to fit over ordinary prescription glasses. The platform can also accept a magnetic VR cover. That physical/interface role is closer to HoloLens/Magic Leap-class headsets than to eyeglass-frame smart glasses under the site's established taxonomy.

Qian nevertheless has strong purchaser-history evidence: its Kickstarter offered paid Light and Standard reward tiers, and independent owners/reviewers documented delivered units. The campaign FAQ says the Standard package principally adds accessories such as the VR cover, power adapter and Leap Motion mounting hardware, so Light and Standard are preserved as configurations of one Qian headset identity rather than two adjacent IDs.

Sources: [RealMax Qian Kickstarter FAQ](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/realmax100/realmax-qian-the-1008-fov-ar-glasses-for-a-more-immersive/faqs) · [manufacturer product route](https://realmax.myshoplaza.com/products/realmax-qian-immersive-ar-glasses-with-ultra-large-fov) · [independent delivered-unit review](https://skarredghost.com/2021/02/11/realmax-qian-review-wide-fov-ar/)
