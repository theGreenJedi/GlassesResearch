# VITURE Pro 2 — GLS-0188

**Technology lineage:** [VITURE](../../lineages/VITURE.md)  
**Category:** host-driven XR display glasses  
**Catalog state:** current / manufacturer retail  
**Evidence state:** manufacturer-primary; independent/NDI verification pending

VITURE Pro 2 is the company's 2026 fifth-anniversary Pro successor. It is a distinct orderable eyewear identity rather than an accessory bundle or collector finish.

## Manufacturer-documented differences

Current first-party material describes:

- UltraClarity 3.0 optics;
- 50° field of view;
- up to 1600-nit claimed brightness;
- 0 to -5.00D built-in diopter adjustment;
- approximately 63 g weight;
- a chassis described as 16% thinner and 20% lighter than Pro.

These claims establish a generation-specific research target; they are not independent measurements.

## Ownership / interoperability questions

The most important GlassesResearch questions are whether basic display operation remains generic host-controlled behavior and where proprietary value begins: SpaceWalker features, firmware, spatial modes, electrochromic behavior on the exact SKU, accessory integration and recovery/update paths.

## Configuration boundary

VITURE's 2026 Phantom Beast collector edition remains attached to canonical VITURE Beast unless evidence establishes a distinct optics/electronics platform. Packs containing Pro 2 plus neckbands, docks, adapters or console accessories are bundles, not additional glasses models.
