## Identity rationale

Lucyd Armor is not a frame-color or fashion-label variation of the Lyte line. Innovative Eyewear introduced Armor as a separately named smart-safety product line after a multi-year development cycle, with safety certification and a distinct wrap-style/product architecture, and began consumer/retailer shipping in October 2024. Lucyd's current store continues to present Armor as its own purchasable product family.

The Nautica, Eddie Bauer, and Reebok fashion collections are not admitted as separate canonical hardware generations in this packet. Innovative Eyewear describes those collections as smart eyewear powered by its Lucyd technology; absent evidence that a co-brand represents a materially distinct hardware generation, those names remain brand/frame identities rather than automatic GLS inflation.
