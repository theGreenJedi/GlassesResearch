# Smart-Glasses Model Profiles — HeyCyan W640 / W650

## GLS-0169 — W640

**W640** is a Goodway / HeyCyan camera-audio smart-glasses platform documented as a distinct commercial product. Goodway specifies a 35 g frame, JL7018F + Allwinner V821L2 architecture, Sony 8 MP camera, 290 mAh battery, IP66 protection, USB-C charging, dual physical buttons plus sliding touch control, wear detection, Wi-Fi media transfer, Bluetooth calling, and 3D six-axis + EIS stabilization. Its shared silicon and HeyCyan software place it in the same broader OEM ecosystem as W610/W630, but the charging, control and chassis configuration support a separate canonical identity rather than an alias collapse. ## GLS-0170 — W650

**W650** is a separate Goodway / HeyCyan camera-audio smart-glasses platform. Goodway documents a 38 g chassis, 220 mAh battery with 3C fast charging, magnetic two-pin charging, Bluetooth 5.3, dual microphones, Wi-Fi media transfer, 1080p capture and HeyCyan companion software. The manufacturer lists it as a production-ready base model and states no minimum-order requirement on the product page. Those physical and power differences make W650 distinct from W610, W630 and W640 even though the family shares software and some component lineage. Canonical admission does not convert manufacturer claims into hands-on performance or owner-control scores.
