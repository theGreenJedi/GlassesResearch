# Lucyd Loud generations — GLS-0191 through GLS-0193

**Technology lineage:** [Lucyd / Innovative Eyewear](../../lineages/LUCYD.md)  
**Evidence state:** first-party historical acquisition evidence; generation-specific technical enrichment pending

The manufacturer-completeness audit recovered an entire pre-Lyte Lucyd commercial lineage that was absent from the canonical catalog.

## GLS-0191 — Lucyd Loud 1.0

Lucyd calls Loud 1.0 its first Bluetooth audio glasses and states that approximately 800 units were made and sold, mostly in 2018. Prescription lens options were offered through a lens-lab partnership.

Do not backfill later Loud or Lyte battery, chipset, control or audio specifications into this generation.

## GLS-0192 — Lucyd Loud — second edition

Lucyd describes a second Loud edition designed in-house and built in Q1 2019, with multiple colors/patterns. The smaller Slim style was later renamed Youth. The company said this edition remained available on Lucyd.co in July 2020.

“Second edition” is retained as the manufacturer-supported generation name; GlassesResearch does **not** invent “Loud 2.0.”

## GLS-0193 — Lucyd Loud 2020

Lucyd states that availability of a Bluetooth 5.0 chipset led to Loud 2020, developed in seven styles and launched in January 2020. The seven styles remain one electronics generation unless future evidence shows otherwise.

## Remaining lineage question

First-party later records reference a **Lucyd Lyte 2025 collection** launched in December 2024. It remains a candidate rather than a canonical generation until the electronics/platform boundary is established. Nautica, Eddie Bauer and Reebok collections are similarly mapped by underlying hardware rather than counted per fashion frame.
