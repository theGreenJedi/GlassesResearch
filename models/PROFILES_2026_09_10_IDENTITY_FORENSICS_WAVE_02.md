# Identity Forensics Wave 02 — Editorial Profiles

These profiles accompany the 2026-09-10 canonical admissions. They establish identity and evidence boundaries only; none is a GlassesResearch hands-on evaluation.

## GLS-0238 — RealWear Arc 3

RealWear Arc 3 is a current enterprise assisted-reality headset sold directly by RealWear. RealWear identifies the hardware model as **A31G** and distinguishes it from the Navigator line with a see-through full-color micro-OLED display, Snapdragon 662 platform and a different headband-style architecture. The manufacturer currently offers a direct purchase path. Treat optical quality, runtime, camera performance, durability, software behavior and owner-control characteristics as manufacturer claims until independently reproduced. Sources: https://shop.realwear.com/products/realwear-arc-3 and https://support.realwear.com/knowledge/arc3/specifications

## GLS-0239 — Iristick G1

Iristick G1 is the 2021 market identity that replaced the earlier **Iristick Z1** name. Iristick's contemporaneous release states that G1 had formerly been on the market as Z1 and that G1 was available from May 2021. The surviving manufacturer specification describes a 77 g glasses-style safety device with a monocular 428 × 240 display, central and optical-zoom cameras, phone/pocket-unit architecture and prescription-lens compatibility. Preserve **Iristick Z1** as a historical alias rather than a separate canonical model. Manufacturer specifications remain source-attributed rather than independently verified. Sources: https://innovationfund.eu/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/2021-04-27-IRI-PRESS-RELEASE-Iristick.H1-launch-april2021_final.pdf and https://iristick.com/uploads/files/IRI-spec-sheet-Iristick.G1-32021-EU-final.pdf

## GLS-0240 — Iristick G1 PRO

Iristick G1 PRO was marketed alongside G1 in 2021 as the PRO configuration of the same G1 platform. Iristick's comparison specification shows the same physical dimensions, 77 g weight, display, cameras, 6× optical zoom and pocket-unit architecture as base G1; the documented PRO differentiation is multilingual voice commands and advanced barcode scanning. Contemporaneous manufacturer material states that G1 PRO was available from May 2021, and later trade records preserve a shipped complete G1 Pro set. Its separate canonical identity reflects a separately marketed and acquired PRO product under the same ruler already used for G2 / G2 PRO; it does **not** assert a different core electronics generation. Sources: https://iristick.com/uploads/files/IRI-spec-sheet-Iristick.G1-32021-EU-final.pdf and https://innovationfund.eu/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/2021-04-27-IRI-PRESS-RELEASE-Iristick.H1-launch-april2021_final.pdf
