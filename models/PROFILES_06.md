# Additional Smart-Glasses Model Profiles

## GLS-0159 — Lucyd Armor

Lucyd Armor is the safety-eyewear branch of Innovative Eyewear's Lucyd platform rather than another fashion frame in the Lyte family. Introduced commercially in 2024, Armor combines open-ear Bluetooth audio and hands-free phone/assistant access with impact-protective smart safety eyewear, and Lucyd subsequently expanded the line with additional certified workplace styles. Its significance is the change in use case and physical architecture: this is eyewear intended to function as protective equipment while retaining connected-audio features, not simply ordinary smart glasses with a different lens color. The canonical identity therefore belongs to the **Armor product line**, while fashion co-brands and individual Armor styles remain aliases/variants unless evidence establishes a materially different electronics generation.
