# Additional Smart-Glasses Model Profiles

## GLS-0159 — Lucyd Armor

Lucyd Armor is the safety-eyewear branch of Innovative Eyewear's Lucyd platform rather than another fashion frame in the Lyte family. Introduced commercially in 2024, Armor combines open-ear Bluetooth audio and hands-free phone/assistant access with impact-protective smart safety eyewear, and Lucyd subsequently expanded the line with additional certified workplace styles. Its significance is the change in use case and physical architecture: this is eyewear intended to function as protective equipment while retaining connected-audio features, not simply ordinary smart glasses with a different lens color. The canonical identity therefore belongs to the **Armor product line**, while fashion co-brands and individual Armor styles remain aliases/variants unless evidence establishes a materially different electronics generation.

Source: [Lucyd — Lucyd Armor](https://lucyd.co/products/lucyd-armor)

## GLS-0160 — MemoMind One

MemoMind One is MemoMind / Qinglai Innovation's camera-free binocular-display AI-glasses product, developed from the optical-engineering lineage XGIMI publicly associated with the MemoMind launch. Current primary material presents a dual-eye Micro-LED display, open-ear audio, microphones, optional prescription lenses and AI-assisted recording, translation, teleprompter, notification and navigation functions. MemoMind currently lists the product from **$399** and routes acquisition through Kickstarter, which crosses the canonical ledger's documented-acquisition threshold while broad fulfillment remains unproven. The record therefore enters as `preorder/crowdfunding`; performance, SDK access, firmware ownership, local/cloud AI boundaries and service survival remain unknown until separately evidenced.

Sources: [MemoMind One](https://www.memo-mind.com/pages/memomind-one) · [XGIMI CES 2026 MemoMind announcement](https://us.xgimi.com/blogs/news/xgimi-ces-2026-with-memomind-ai-glasses)
