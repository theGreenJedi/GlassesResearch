# Sender G600Pro / JTLlink family profile

## GLS-0250 — Sender G600Pro

**Status:** current marketplace acquisition; canonical purchaser-history entry.  
**Evidence state:** regulatory + commercial; no GlassesResearch hands-on evaluation.  
**Lineage:** [Huizhou JTLlink G600Pro hardware family](../lineages/JTLLINK_G600PRO.md).

Sender G600Pro is a display-free camera/audio AI-glasses product currently offered through an OEM/ODM marketplace channel. Its canonical admission is based on two separate evidence layers: a commercial Sender-branded acquisition route and a 2026 FCC test package for model G600Pro under Huizhou JTLlink Technology Co., Ltd.

The regulatory record is unusually useful for identity work. FCC report EED32S80475301, FCC ID `2BEKU-G600PRO`, identifies Huizhou JTLlink as applicant, manufacturer, and factory. It names G600Pro together with G600S, E06S, E06, S100, S200, S300, S500, and HS600, and states that the family shares electrical design, PCB, and layout while model names and appearance may vary for marketing. G600Pro was the tested model.

GlassesResearch therefore records the sibling names as **confirmed hardware-lineage members**, not eight additional canonical products. A separate GLS row would require evidence that a sibling name represents a materially named commercial product or generation with its own acquisition identity rather than a marketing, appearance, or rebrand variant.

Marketplace claims about camera resolution, battery capacity, AI assistant behavior, translation, charging, waterproofing, and related features remain supplier-reported until corroborated or independently reproduced. The relationship between the Sender commercial brand/seller and JTLlink's regulatory/manufacturing role is also intentionally left unresolved beyond what the sources establish.

**Research priorities:** identify the companion app and account/cloud boundary; resolve retail identities for the FCC sibling names; compare sibling frame/mechanical differences; map firmware/update infrastructure; and determine which seller specifications are stable platform properties rather than listing-level configuration claims.
