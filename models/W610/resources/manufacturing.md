## Current conclusion

The strongest public evidence supports a broad OEM/ODM ecosystem around a shared W610 reference platform. It does **not** yet establish a single definitive manufacturer. Device labels, app model lists, firmware endpoints, regulatory identifiers, and PCB markings are the best next evidence sources.
