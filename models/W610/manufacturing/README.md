# W610 Manufacturing Intelligence

This section maps OEM, ODM, solution-house, supplier, brand, app-operator, and reseller roles without collapsing them into unsupported claims.

The W610 is the first detailed case study inside the ecosystem-wide investigation [Who Actually Makes These Glasses? Mapping the Shenzhen Smart-Glasses Platform Ecosystem](../../../docs/SHENZHEN_SMART_GLASSES_SUPPLY_CHAIN.md).

## Current leads

- [ORG-0004 — Shenzhen Qingcheng Wireless Technology](../../../glossary/organizations/ORG-0004-qingcheng-wireless.md) is now a strong upstream solution/design-house lead: Allwinner ecosystem-event coverage reports a V821 complete-glasses solution jointly developed by Qingcheng Wireless and Allwinner, and 2026 coverage shows Qingcheng Wireless demonstrating a V881 camera/display glasses solution. Direct W610 design/manufacture remains unproven.
- [ORG-0002 — Goodway Techs](../../../glossary/organizations/ORG-0002-goodway-techs.md) publishes a detailed W610 product page and is a strong commercial/OEM lead; exact design and factory authority remain unresolved.
- [ORG-0003 — Dongguan Zhiyang Electronic Technology](../../../glossary/organizations/ORG-0003-dongguan-zhiyang.md) markets a W610-labelled OEM/ODM product; exact upstream role remains unresolved.
- [ORG-0001 — Shenzhen Qingcheng Future Technology](../../../glossary/organizations/ORG-0001-hecyan-qingcheng-future.md) remains the existing HeyCyan app-operator record. It is **not currently treated as an alias of Qingcheng Wireless**.
- Multiple retail names repeat the same chipset, camera, battery, and app fingerprint, supporting a shared platform, solution-house, or rebranding ecosystem.

## Working conclusion

The W610 should currently be treated as a multi-brand OEM/reference-platform family. The new Allwinner/Qingcheng Wireless evidence materially narrows the upstream search, but exact W610 PCB ownership, firmware authority, mold ownership, SMT/final-assembly responsibility, and revision lineage remain unresolved.

The research model now distinguishes at least five potentially separate roles:

1. silicon/reference platform;
2. smart-glasses solution/design house;
3. ODM/factory and tooling owner;
4. app/cloud operator; and
5. brand/reseller/commercial supplier.

A company can occupy more than one role, but GlassesResearch does not infer that from a shared product name or similar corporate name.

## Next evidence

1. Compare packaging, manuals, QR destinations, product images, dimensions, button layouts, PCB/FPC markings, and camera modules across brands.
2. Preserve supplier names, legal entities, addresses, MOQ, certifications, and archived marketplace pages.
3. Search FCC, Bluetooth SIG, patents, trade-show records, corporate registries, and independent teardowns using every known alias.
4. Resolve the relationship, if any, between Qingcheng Wireless and Qingcheng Future / HeyCyan.
5. Ask upstream candidates who owns the schematic, PCB layout, firmware signing, molds, camera placement, SMT, and final assembly.
6. Confirm relationships with device-level or primary engineering evidence before promoting claims.
