# W610 Manufacturing Intelligence Map

The commercial name on a listing may identify a reseller rather than the designer or factory. Track each role separately.

## Entity roles

- Platform or reference-design owner
- Industrial designer
- OEM manufacturer
- ODM developer
- Contract factory
- Component supplier
- Brand owner
- Importer or distributor
- Retail seller
- App or cloud-service operator
- Certification applicant

## Investigation sources

- Packaging, manuals, labels, QR codes, and app privacy policies
- FCC, Bluetooth SIG, CE, RoHS, and other certification records
- Alibaba, Made-in-China, Global Sources, and archived product pages
- Trade-show exhibitor catalogs
- Import/export and shipping databases where lawfully accessible
- Domain registration, corporate registries, and support contacts
- PCB silkscreen, firmware strings, APK metadata, and cloud endpoints

Never collapse multiple roles into a single unsupported claim. A seller, brand, factory, and software operator may all be different companies.
