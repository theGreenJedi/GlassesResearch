## Evidence needed

- Package and manual model markings
- BLE advertisements and device-information values
- App identifiers and supported-device lists
- Firmware names and update endpoints
- PCB photographs and component markings
- Regulatory filings and certification records
- OEM or ODM product sheets

Do not infer common ancestry from matching frames alone. Reused industrial design can conceal unrelated electronics, while different shells can contain the same reference platform.
