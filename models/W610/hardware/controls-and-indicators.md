# W610 Controls and Indicators

## Specimen

Project-owned W610, observed August 2026.

## Right-temple controls

Two physical buttons are present on the right temple.

| Control | Confirmed behavior | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Rear button | Power on/off through a press-and-hold action | High |
| Forward button | Function not yet conclusively mapped | Open |

## Startup behavior

1. Press and hold the rear button.
2. An audible tone is heard.
3. The LED near the hinge flashes briefly.
4. The device becomes discoverable as `HeyCyan Glasses`.

## Indicator location

The visible status LED is near the right hinge. Exact color and blink-code mapping have not yet been systematically recorded.

## Open tests

- Short press, long press, and double press for each button
- Behavior while disconnected, Bluetooth-connected, recording, charging, and low on battery
- LED color and cadence for startup, shutdown, pairing, capture, charging, full charge, and faults
- Whether button mappings change when the HeyCyan companion app is connected

## Safety note

Button testing should begin with non-destructive observation. Avoid undocumented long-press combinations until recovery behavior is understood.
