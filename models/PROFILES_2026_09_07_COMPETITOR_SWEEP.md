# Competitor Completeness Sweep — Model Profiles

**Research date:** 2026-09-07  
**Canonical packet:** [Competitor Completeness Sweep](THE_LIST_RECONCILIATION_2026-09-07_COMPETITOR_SWEEP.md)

These profiles preserve the strongest current evidence while keeping preorder, regional, manufacturer-claim and unverified owner-control boundaries explicit.

## GLS-0219 — XREAL xbx a01+

XREAL xbx a01+ is a current host-driven XR display sold directly by XREAL. The manufacturer store lists the named glasses at $299 with a normal purchase path. It is not merely an accessory or a retail bundle, and XREAL presents it separately from One, 1S, One Pro and ROG XREAL R1. Host compatibility, spatial behavior, firmware and any model-specific chipset claims should remain evidence-bound rather than inherited from adjacent XREAL generations.

Sources: [XREAL xbx a01+ store](https://us.shop.xreal.com/products/xreal-xbx-a01) · [XREAL product catalog](https://us.shop.xreal.com/collections/xreal-product-catalog)

## GLS-0220 — XREAL AURA

XREAL AURA crossed the purchaser-history threshold when XREAL began accepting paid, refundable AURA deposits that are applied to the final purchase price. XREAL states that the deposit reserves launch priority and that the product launches in regional waves beginning in fall 2026. The deposit is not itself physical merchandise, so this row remains explicitly `preorder/reservation`; final retail price, production-unit specifications, fulfillment and post-launch software boundaries remain unresolved.

Source: [XREAL AURA Priority Deposit](https://us.shop.xreal.com/products/xreal-aura-priority-deposit)

## GLS-0221 — Everysight Maverick AI

Maverick AI is Everysight's full-color AR + AI everyday-eyewear branch. It is now purchaser-history eligible because a funded Kickstarter campaign accepted paid backers and Everysight maintains fulfillment/support material for those orders. Current manufacturer material describes a 47 g frame, BEAM projection engine, full-color display, AI camera, audio, phone/wearable connectivity and prescription options, but Everysight still calls the product in development and has moved initial production timing. Production hardware therefore remains pre-shipment evidence.

Sources: [Everysight Maverick AI](https://www.everysight.com/pages/maverick-ai) · [Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/everysight/maverick-full-color-ai-ar-glasses) · [Everysight preorder support](https://support.everysight.com/hc/en-us/categories/27996757089820-Kickstarter-VIP-Pre-orders)

## GLS-0222 — Everysight Maverick AI Pro

Maverick AI Pro is a distinct paid-campaign configuration rather than a frame color. Everysight documents the Pro model as adding native eye tracking through GazeIntent while retaining the broader Maverick AI platform. Its purchaser-history status comes from the same Kickstarter/backer fulfillment program. Eye-tracking behavior, runtime certificate policy, AI behavior and final production specifications remain unverified on delivered production hardware.

Sources: [Everysight Maverick AI / AI Pro](https://www.everysight.com/pages/maverick-ai) · [Everysight preorder support](https://support.everysight.com/hc/en-us/categories/27996757089820-Kickstarter-VIP-Pre-orders)

## GLS-0223 — ENGO 1

ENGO 1 is the first commercial ENGO sports-HUD generation in the ActiveLook ecosystem. ActiveLook preserves it as a distinct supported product identity and its own store preserves a commercial ENGO 1 sample-unit record. It belongs to the [ActiveLook lineage](../lineages/ACTIVELOOK.md), whose BLE/open-API architecture is documented separately from product-specific hardware and lifecycle claims.

Sources: [ActiveLook consumer lineage](https://www.activelook.net/pages/for-consumers) · [ENGO 1 preserved store record](https://shop.activelook.net/products/engo-1-sample-unit)

## GLS-0224 — ENGO 2

ENGO 2 is a distinct second-generation sports HUD. ActiveLook announced a paid first-run preorder in 2022, and current ENGO storefront material continues to expose ENGO2. ActiveLook describes the family as BLE-connected Light AR eyewear using a shared open API, but model-specific battery, optical and compatibility claims should not be inherited automatically across generations.

Sources: [ActiveLook ENGO 2 announcement](https://www.activelook.net/news-blog/introducing-engo-2) · [ENGO current collection](https://us.engoeyewear.com/collections/)

## GLS-0225 — ENGO3

ENGO3 is the current ENGO sports-HUD generation, sold directly by ENGO. The current product page exposes a normal add-to-cart route and describes compatibility with Garmin and Apple Watch ecosystems plus customizable in-view sport metrics. Photochromic, color and sport-specific lens configurations remain variants of this electronics generation unless future evidence proves otherwise.

Sources: [ENGO3](https://us.engoeyewear.com/products/engo-3) · [ActiveLook technology](https://www.activelook.net/pages/the-tech)

## GLS-0226 — Julbo EVAD-1

Julbo EVAD-1 is an early commercial ActiveLook-powered sport-HUD product. ActiveLook announced in 2021 that EVAD-1 was available for sale and orderable from Julbo. The product is therefore preserved as a legacy purchaser-history identity rather than being reduced to an ActiveLook platform footnote.

Source: [ActiveLook — EVAD-1 available for sale](https://www.activelook.net/news-blog/evad-1-by-julbo-the-first-activelook-glasses-are-now-available-for-sale)

## GLS-0227 — Cosmo Vision

Cosmo Vision is an ActiveLook-powered navigation/performance HUD from Cosmo Connected. The manufacturer preserves a €349 product page that is now sold out, while ActiveLook lists Cosmo Vision among its compatible eyewear. That is enough to preserve the historical commercial identity; exact first-sale timing and current service longevity remain research questions.

Sources: [Cosmo Vision](https://cosmoconnected.com/en/products/cosmo-vision) · [ActiveLook consumer ecosystem](https://www.activelook.net/pages/for-consumers)

## GLS-0228 — ActiveLook Enterprise

ActiveLook Enterprise is MicroOLED/ActiveLook's enterprise-oriented Light AR eyewear. The current ActiveLook store lists the named unit at €349 with Add to cart and positions it as a development/deployment platform. ActiveLook documents a shared open BLE API across certified glasses, making this row particularly relevant to Openness and Owner Control research, but the platform's published API does not by itself establish unrestricted firmware or bootloader control.

Sources: [ActiveLook Enterprise store](https://shop.activelook.net/products/activelook-enterprise) · [ActiveLook developer architecture](https://www.activelook.net/news-blog/developing-with-activelook-getting-started)

## GLS-0229 — Huawei AI Glasses

Huawei AI Glasses are a current China-market camera/audio AI-glasses generation distinct from Huawei Eyewear 2 and Huawei Vision Glass. Huawei's own product page provides an immediate purchase path starting at ¥2499 and documents a 12 MP camera, HarmonyOS-linked AI behavior, audio, IP54 and model-specific fit/weight variants. Regional software, account/cloud dependencies, owner-selectable AI, firmware access and international availability remain separate evidence questions.

Sources: [Huawei AI Glasses](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/audio/ai-glasses/) · [Huawei specifications](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/audio/ai-glasses/specs/)

## GLS-0230 — ThinkAR AiLENS V1

AiLENS V1 is ThinkAR's camera-free AI/HUD eyewear. ThinkAR documents a green MicroLED 640×480 display, BLE 5.2, dual microphones/speakers, translation, AI assistant, notifications, calls and teleprompter functions, approximately 38 g weight, and smartphone-app dependence. The support site documents a one-year warranty and dedicated app. The current evidence establishes a named commercial product but not unrestricted developer, firmware or cloud-independent operation.

Sources: [ThinkAR AiLENS V1](https://www.thinkar.com/en/pages/ailens) · [ThinkAR support](https://www.thinkar.com/en/pages/support)

## GLS-0231 — Baidu / Xiaodu AI Glasses Pro

Xiaodu AI Glasses Pro is a Baidu/Xiaodu camera/audio AI-glasses product sold in China. Xiaodu's own store exposes a named product route, and Baidu-owned Haokan records the November 2025 commercial launch at ¥2299. This admission is intentionally conservative: it establishes identity and acquisition but does not promote unverified technical, AI, battery, privacy or cloud-independence claims from secondary summaries.

Sources: [Xiaodu store](https://dumall.baidu.com/product/902971303493639) · [Baidu Haokan sale record](https://haokan.baidu.com/v?pd=wisenatural&vid=3511145600541079620)

## GLS-0232 — n+ / Volkswagen Smart Glasses

Volkswagen Smart Glasses are a Volkswagen-licensed HUD product developed/manufactured through n+'s Smart eBike program. n+ publishes a €499 accessory/order path and documents navigation and eBike blind-spot alerts in the wearer's field of view. The product remains preorder/preproduction where regional certification is still pending. The Volkswagen license and eBike integration do not imply Volkswagen manufactures the electronics directly.

Sources: [Volkswagen Smart Glasses](https://smart-bike.net/en-at/pages/smartglasses) · [n+ / Volkswagen press information](https://smart-bike.net/pages/press) · [accessory order surface](https://smart-bike.net/collections/accessories)
