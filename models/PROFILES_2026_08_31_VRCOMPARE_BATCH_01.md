## GLS-0167 — ASUS AirVision M1

**ASUS AirVision M1** is a tethered personal-display glasses product with a direct manufacturer commercial route. ASUS's U.S. technical-specification page lists a $699 ASUS Store price and claims dual Sony Micro OLED displays, 1920 × 1080 resolution per eye, 72 Hz refresh, 38-degree field of view, native 3DoF head tracking, USB-C DisplayPort Alt Mode, speakers and a microphone array. Those fields remain manufacturer claims until independently reproduced. Canonical admission records the documented acquisition route and does not infer Openness, Owner Control, Hackability, Cloud Independence, repairability, comfort or measured display quality. ## GLS-0168 — nubia Neovision Glass

Those claims remain in the primary-source lane and are not converted into independent performance findings.
