# Nuance Audio editorial profiles

These profiles preserve the public editorial path for the two canonical Nuance Audio generations while keeping claims bounded to admitted manufacturer-primary evidence and the existing GlassesResearch family research.

## GLS-0248 — Nuance Audio

EssilorLuxottica / Nuance Audio's first commercial hearing-glasses generation is purpose-built hearing-assistance eyewear for mild-to-moderate hearing loss. Manufacturer-primary material documents directional microphones, open-ear speakers, prescription-ready optical configurations, app/remote control, and a charging ecosystem. GlassesResearch treats it as a distinct generation rather than collapsing it into its successor.

**Evidence boundary:** hearing performance, comfort, battery endurance, and speech-in-noise performance remain manufacturer or clinical claims unless independently reproduced.

**Generation:** predecessor to GLS-0249, Nuance Audio Plus.

**Research:** [Nuance Audio family](NuanceAudio/README.md) · [Report Card](../docs/report-cards/PROFILE_NUANCE_AUDIO.md)

## GLS-0249 — Nuance Audio Plus

Nuance Audio Plus is the separately named second generation in the Nuance Audio family. Manufacturer-primary material documents stronger amplification, improved speech clarity in noise, more natural own-voice perception, increased battery capacity, physical power and volume controls, expanded frame options, and compatible-iPhone call handling.

**Evidence boundary:** independent teardown, battery measurement, comfort, and acoustic-performance verification remain pending. The product is camera-free hearing-assistance eyewear, not a general-purpose visual-AI platform.

**Generation:** successor to GLS-0248, Nuance Audio.

**Research:** [Nuance Audio family](NuanceAudio/README.md) · [Report Card](../docs/report-cards/PROFILE_NUANCE_AUDIO_PLUS.md)
