## Registry rules

- Prefer a manufacturer, project, regulatory, repository, or archived primary source.
- Keep seller pages labeled `commercial`; repeated listings may share one upstream claim.
- Record aliases and rebrands rather than counting them as independent proof.
- Do not copy specifications into this registry until the source and model revision are explicit.
- A dedicated chapter is earned by substantive content, not by market prominence.
- Discontinued models remain in scope because software, protocols, design history, and community work may still matter.
