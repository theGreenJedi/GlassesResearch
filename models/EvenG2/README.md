# Even Realities G2 — GLS-0048

Discreet binocular-display smart glasses from Even Realities.

## Hardware

Even Realities' published specifications describe:

- 36 g frame weight;
- binocular 640 × 350 green display;
- MicroLED waveguide optics;
- 27.5° field of view and 60 Hz refresh;
- 192 mAh battery;
- up to two days of regular use under the vendor's usage assumptions;
- IP65 protection;
- four microphones;
- Bluetooth Low Energy connectivity;
- no camera and no speakers.

Prescription options are offered by Even Realities.

## Software and connectivity

The G2 uses the Even companion application. Even's support documentation states that current functions depend on Bluetooth and internet connectivity rather than operating as a fully offline product.

Even also documents cloud-assisted translation and its own AI/LLM services. This makes G2 a useful contrast to camera/audio AI glasses: information is presented visually without an outward-facing camera or speakers.
