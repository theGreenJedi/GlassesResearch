# Even G2 by Even Realities — GLS-0048

The **Even G2** is a discreet binocular-display smart-glasses platform from **Even Realities**. It is designed to look and function like ordinary eyewear while presenting private visual information in the wearer's field of view, without using an outward-facing camera.

## What Even G2 is

Even Realities positions G2 as everyday display smart glasses rather than camera/audio capture glasses. Its core interaction model is visual: notifications, translation, navigation, teleprompting, AI cues, and other information are presented through the binocular display while a paired phone and the Even application provide the broader software environment.

That makes G2 a useful contrast to camera-led AI glasses. It emphasizes discretion, display quality, prescription integration, and conversational/contextual assistance without an outward-facing camera.

## Hardware and optics

Even Realities' current published specifications describe:

- 36 g frame weight;
- magnesium-alloy frame and titanium-alloy temples;
- binocular 640 × 350 green MicroLED waveguide display;
- 27.5° field of view;
- 60 Hz refresh rate;
- up to 1200 nits stated brightness;
- 98% stated optical passthrough;
- 192 mAh / 0.744 Wh glasses battery;
- four microphones;
- automatic brightness control;
- Bluetooth Low Energy **5.4**;
- IP65 water/dust resistance;
- no outward-facing camera.

Even Realities states up to two days of regular use under its own usage assumptions. Independent normalized battery testing remains necessary before treating that as comparable performance across brands.

## Prescription and optical serviceability

Even offers G2 with prescription lenses and markets the display/prescription lens stack as an integrated optical system rather than a separate corrective insert behind the display. Current product material advertises a broad prescription range and multiple frame/lens configurations.

For GlassesResearch, the remaining question is not simply whether prescriptions are available, but **how serviceable the eyewear remains over time**: whether lenses can be replaced independently, what happens when a prescription changes, which optical partners can service the frame, and whether the display layer constrains ordinary optician workflows.

## Software, AI, and connectivity

The G2 uses the Even companion application. Even's support documentation states that current functions depend on Bluetooth and internet connectivity rather than operating as a fully offline product.

Even also documents its own AI/LLM services and contextual features. This means the glasses' discreet hardware design does not automatically imply cloud independence. GlassesResearch treats the following as separate questions:

- what works with only the glasses and phone;
- what requires an internet connection;
- what depends specifically on Even-operated services;
- whether any functions can be redirected to owner-selected/local services;
- whether data can be exported or preserved independently of the vendor stack.

## Why the G2 is distinctive

The strongest current differentiators are:

- ordinary-eyewear appearance;
- camera-free design;
- binocular display rather than audio-first interaction;
- prescription integration;
- very low frame weight for display glasses.

Those strengths make G2 especially relevant to buyers who prioritize discretion and private visual information. They do not answer the separate questions of hackability, offline operation, repairability, or long-term cloud independence.

## What remains unknown

Important evidence gaps include:

- independently measured display visibility and battery life;
- complete offline/function-by-function behavior;
- SDK/API access and third-party development limits;
- owner-selected AI/model support;
- repairability and component replacement;
- long-term prescription re-lensing/service options;
- firmware and update control.
