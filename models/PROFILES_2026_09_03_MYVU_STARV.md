# Smart-Glasses Model Profiles — MYVU / StarV Air lineage

## GLS-0167 — MYVU Air / StarV Air (XGA010C)

**MYVU Air / StarV Air (XGA010C)** is a lightweight binocular display smart-glasses product from the Meizu / DreamSmart ecosystem. First-party specifications document a 43 g chassis, MicroLED display, dual 1280×480 resolution, 30° field of view, single-layer resin diffractive waveguides, 183 mAh battery, USB-C, dual microphones, dual speakers and Flyme AR. The official manual independently identifies model **XGA010C**.

Public community reverse engineering by GitHub user **Panny777** provides unusually detailed evidence of third-party interoperability: an independent client and unofficial Android SDK reportedly reproduce pairing/session behavior, teleprompter output, notifications, brightness and volume control, settings, weather, trackpad interaction, navigation, microphone ingestion, and pluggable speech/LLM behavior. GlassesResearch classifies that material as **community-attributed technical evidence**, not as a GlassesResearch lab result. Official openness remains distinct from community-discovered hackability, and independent replication is pending acquisition of a GlassesResearch-controlled specimen.

The wider community-development context now also includes **CyanBridge / Alternative HeyCyan App and SDK** by FerSaiyan. CyanBridge is primarily a HeyCyan-compatible Android companion, but its public documentation explicitly treats Panny777's hardware-verified MYVU work as an upstream interoperability reference for native MYVU support. This relationship is useful evidence of knowledge transfer across otherwise separate smart-glasses projects; it does **not** by itself prove identical protocols, firmware, or hardware between XGA010C and HeyCyan/W610-class devices.

A dedicated technical-network note records the relationship between the XGA010C model, Panny777's client/SDK/protocol work, CyanBridge's downstream integration, and unresolved firmware-modification leads. Claims of firmware replacement, arbitrary code execution, root access, or a complete custom-firmware chain remain **unproven** until a directly inspectable source and reproducible hardware result are available.

## GLS-0168 — StarV Air2

**StarV Air2** is the successor lightweight binocular HUD generation. Meizu documents a 44 g frame, 640×480 single-green MicroLED display, 30° diagonal FOV, high-refractive-index tempered-glass optics, 204 mAh battery, BT 5.2, USB-C and Flyme XR 2.0. The Air2 product page further documents a 0.15 cc light engine and new scroll-wheel interaction. Those hardware, optical and software differences are sufficient to treat Air2 as a distinct marketed generation rather than an alias for XGA010C. Marketplace claims such as “4K” are not imported into this profile, and protocol compatibility with XGA010C remains unknown pending direct evidence.
