# INMO X AI+Camera Glasses — GLS-0194

**Technology lineage:** [INMO](../../lineages/INMO.md)  
**Category:** camera / AI smart glasses  
**Catalog state:** legacy / region-limited; 2024 blind-order/preorder evidence  
**Evidence state:** manufacturer-primary identity/acquisition evidence; model-specific technical verification pending

INMO's own company-history page lists **INMO X AI+Camera Glasses** as a November 2024 product and states that the November 2024 release batch—Air3, GO2 and INMO X-series AI photo glasses—received more than 10,000 blind orders in its first batch. That crosses GlassesResearch's preorder/acquisition threshold.

## Important identity boundary

INMO also says an **INMO X series** 5G all-in-one AR device debuted in May 2021. The current evidence does not prove whether the 2021 X hardware became the 2024 X AI+Camera Glasses, whether the name was reused, or whether there were multiple intervening X generations.

`GLS-0194` therefore refers specifically to the **2024 marketed/orderable INMO X AI+Camera Glasses identity**. The 2021 device remains lineage context pending model-specific archival evidence.

## Research queue

1. recover the November 2024 product/order page and exact regional SKU/model number;
2. identify camera sensor, compute, memory/storage, microphones/audio and battery;
3. determine whether X includes an in-lens display and what optical architecture it uses;
4. map Android/app/account/cloud requirements and local capture/storage behavior;
5. test ADB, USB, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth services, firmware/update paths and recovery;
6. resolve the 2021-versus-2024 X relationship.
