# The List reconciliation — Sender G600Pro / JTLlink family

**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**Decision:** admit one new canonical model; preserve the regulatory sibling names as a shared hardware lineage without inflating the canonical count.

## Admission

**GLS-0250 — Sender G600Pro**

G600Pro has a current commercial acquisition route and a matching regulatory identity. The commercial listing establishes a purchasable Sender-branded G600Pro; FCC test report EED32S80475301 establishes Huizhou JTLlink Technology Co., Ltd. as applicant/manufacturer/factory for FCC ID `2BEKU-G600PRO`.

The filing also names G600S, E06S, E06, S100, S200, S300, S500 and HS600 and states that the models share electrical design, PCB, and layout while model name and appearance may vary for marketing. Those sibling names are therefore recorded in the dedicated lineage chapter, **not** admitted as eight additional canonical models.

## Count treatment

Before this admission, the canonical ledger contains **248 active rows**. Stable GLS numbering already runs through `GLS-0249` because retired identifiers remain retired rather than being reused. G600Pro therefore receives **GLS-0250**, and the canonical row count becomes **249**.

No scored Report Card is created by this admission, so the Report Card count is unchanged.

## Provenance boundary

- FCC/regulatory evidence: strong evidence for manufacturer identity and the shared electrical/PCB/layout family relationship.
- Marketplace evidence: sufficient for the G600Pro acquisition threshold and Sender commercial identity; specifications and AI-feature claims remain seller-reported.
- Independent hands-on evidence: none yet.
- Sibling commercial identities: unresolved; no extra canonical rows.
