# Solos AirGo family — GLS-0026 through GLS-0029

Solos has produced several generations of audio and camera smart glasses. The current developer program explicitly covers AirGo V 1/2 camera models and AirGo 3/A5 audio models through one SDK.

## Developer access

Solos publishes a commercial SDK program for iOS and Android developers. Its developer documentation describes:

- BLE for control;
- Wi-Fi data transport on V2;
- microphone and audio input/output;
- motion and e-compass sensors;
- touch gestures;
- voice-activation detection;
- camera/photo access on AirGo V1;
- video streaming and recording on V2;
- webhooks and RTMP endpoints;
- iOS/Android wrappers and n8n integration examples.

This is unusually concrete developer information for a consumer smart-glasses family and makes AirGo useful for interoperability and assistant-integration research.

## Models in The List

- GLS-0026 — AirGo 2
- GLS-0027 — AirGo 3
- GLS-0028 — AirGo Vision
- GLS-0029 — AirGo V2
