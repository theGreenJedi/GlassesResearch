## Why these belong in The List

GlassesResearch already includes tethered display eyewear from modern manufacturers. Vuzix's surviving filings describe the AV/Wrap/STAR products as video eyewear mounted on eyeglass-style frames/temples and document real consumer, direct or developer acquisition routes. Age alone is not a form-factor exclusion.

## What this hub does not claim

Admission proves purchaser-history identity, not complete technical truth. The following remain model-specific evidence queues:

- exact introduction month and regional SKUs for the oldest AV products;
- display panel, optics, FOV and eye-box measurements by revision;
- analog/VGA/HDMI/USB host requirements;
- tracking and camera behavior on VR/AR variants;
- firmware/software survivability on modern hosts;
- prescription/focus adjustment options;
- teardown/component relationships between adjacent generations.

No report-card score transfers automatically across the range.
