# GlassesResearch styled QR

Companion notes for the production QR asset. The encoded target is `https://glassesresearch.org/` and the QR is generated at error-correction level H so a small centered GlassesResearch mark can be used while retaining practical scan reliability.

Before commercial printing, test the final printed proof with multiple phones at the intended physical size and lighting conditions. Do not crop the quiet zone.
