# Model presentation imagery

GlassesResearch treats presentation images as a separate layer from evidentiary photographs. Every canonical model should eventually have a recognizable primary image on its model/report-card surface, while preserving provenance and a documented basis for publication.

## Public editorial-use policy

GlassesResearch may use manufacturer-published product imagery when reasonably necessary to identify, illustrate, compare, document, or discuss the product under independent research and editorial coverage. The image remains the property of its rights holder and is not offered for independent reuse or redistribution.

This is an editorial-use policy, not a claim that publicly viewable images are in the public domain and not a blanket declaration that every manufacturer image is fair use. Each asset is reviewed and recorded individually. GlassesResearch prefers, in order: original GlassesResearch photography; explicitly licensed or manufacturer press/media assets; manufacturer-published product imagery used for a documented editorial purpose; and appropriately licensed/public-domain third-party imagery.

For manufacturer imagery used editorially, GlassesResearch should use only what is reasonably necessary to identify or discuss the product, preserve the source and rights-holder attribution, avoid removing watermarks, avoid implying manufacturer endorsement, and avoid using third-party photography without a separate permission or license basis. A rights-holder concern should be investigated promptly and the asset can be withdrawn while the issue is reviewed.

The legal backdrop is 17 U.S.C. §107's case-specific fair-use analysis. Research, criticism, comment, and news reporting are among the purposes named in the statute, but no single factor—including noncommercial publication—automatically establishes fair use. The site therefore records the publication rationale rather than treating public availability as permission.

## Required record

Each model visual record is keyed by its stable `GLS-####` identifier and records:

- primary image path
- source URL or original-photography evidence folder
- creator / rights holder when known
- rights basis for publication
- retrieval or capture date
- alt text
- asset state: `missing`, `candidate`, `cleared`, or `published`
- optional transparent front/three-quarter asset for visualization
- optional calibrated dimensions / 3D asset for future try-on

A remote image URL is not sufficient. Public presentation imagery must be preserved locally and have a documented publication basis before GlassesResearch republishes it. Acceptable bases include original photography, explicit license/permission, manufacturer press/media permission, public-domain/open license, or a documented manufacturer-imagery editorial-use rationale under this policy.

## Separation from evidence

A presentation image answers **what does this model look like?** It does not automatically prove a specification. Evidence photographs continue to live in the Visual Evidence Archive with their original provenance.

## Virtual try-on

Try-on assets are model-specific presentation assets. Camera frames remain in the user's browser. GlassesResearch does not require an account, upload camera frames, or treat the visualization as evidence of physical or prescription fit.
