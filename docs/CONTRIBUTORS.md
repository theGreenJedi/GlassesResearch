# Hall of Contributors

GlassesResearch is built from verifiable contributions: research, corrections, preservation, testing, documentation, code, and community discoveries.

## Recognition policy

Contributors may be credited publicly when they choose to be. Credit should describe the contribution rather than inflate status or imply endorsement.

Examples include:

- locating or preserving a manual, SDK, firmware release, or regulatory filing;
- providing reproducible hands-on testing;
- documenting BLE, API, protocol, recovery, or repair behavior;
- correcting a factual claim with stronger evidence;
- improving a model record, comparison field, timeline milestone, glossary entry, or source trail;
- contributing code, validation, accessibility, or site reliability improvements.

## Privacy by default

No contributor is required to publish a legal name, employer, location, or other personal details. A GitHub handle, chosen display name, project name, or anonymous attribution is sufficient.

## Contribution ledger

Substantive contributions should be traceable to the pull request, issue, research challenge, commit, or source record that introduced them. The public Hall of Contributors can remain concise while the repository history provides the detailed audit trail.

## Current contributors

The contributor list will be populated from accepted, attributable contributions. Existing repository history remains the canonical source until individual public credits are added.
