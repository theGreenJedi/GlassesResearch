# Community Research

Community Research tracks public work that helps owners understand, interoperate with, preserve, repair, or independently operate smart-glasses hardware and software.

This is a first-class research lane, not an automatic verification shortcut. Community findings remain attributed to their authors and projects. A repository claim does not become a GlassesResearch laboratory finding unless GlassesResearch independently reproduces it. Evidence status, limitations, hardware scope, and provenance should remain visible.

## Inaugural feature

**[When owners take their glasses back](../news/articles/2026-09-06-when-owners-take-their-glasses-back.md)** — Three community repositories show how protocol reverse engineering, evidence-disciplined device research, and alternative companion software can increase practical owner control.

## Projects in the inaugural feature

- [Panny777 / Meizu-Myvu-Client](https://github.com/Panny777/Meizu-Myvu-Client) — Meizu MYVU / Star Air XGA010C replacement-client and protocol work.
- [aimindseye / rokid-ai-glasses](https://github.com/aimindseye/rokid-ai-glasses) — evidence-labeled research into the non-display Rokid AI Glasses Style.
- [FerSaiyan / Alternative-HeyCyan-App-and-SDK](https://github.com/FerSaiyan/Alternative-HeyCyan-App-and-SDK) — CyanBridge alternative companion and multi-device interoperability research.

Future entries should preserve the same distinction between **what a project reports**, **what its public evidence demonstrates**, and **what GlassesResearch has independently reproduced**.
