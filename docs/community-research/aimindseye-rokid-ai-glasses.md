## What the project does

This repository is an independent, community-maintained research wiki for the display-free Rokid AI Glasses Style. It separates consumer guidance, developer material, and a research library, and labels evidence and limitations rather than treating every observation as a settled capability.

The project reports a tested production device running Android 12 / API 32 with privileged Rokid services, documents the role of the Hi Rokid companion, records qualified USB ADB behavior, and publishes bounded protocol, Binder, Wi-Fi, and independent-client investigations. It also explicitly records important negative boundaries: a complete replacement companion, independent authorization/session reproduction, custom firmware, and a proven persistent phone-free data path have not yet been established.

## Why it matters

The project's contribution is not merely that it finds hidden interfaces. It demonstrates a reusable research discipline for community hardware work: define scope, preserve provenance, publish sanitized evidence, distinguish observation from inference, and state what remains unproven.

That makes it useful beyond a single device. Other researchers can begin from a documented boundary instead of rediscovering the same architecture from zero.
