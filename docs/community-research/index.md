# Community Research

Community Research tracks public work that helps owners understand, interoperate with, preserve, repair, or independently operate smart-glasses hardware and software.

Community findings remain attributed to their authors and projects. Inclusion here is not an automatic verification shortcut, and a repository claim does not become a GlassesResearch laboratory finding unless GlassesResearch independently reproduces it.

## Founding projects

| Project | Primary scope | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|
| [Panny777 / Meizu-Myvu-Client](panny777-meizu-myvu-client.md) | Meizu MYVU / Star Air XGA010C | Hardware-verified community replacement client and protocol reverse engineering that turns undocumented behavior into owner-usable interoperability. |
| [aimindseye / rokid-ai-glasses](aimindseye-rokid-ai-glasses.md) | Rokid AI Glasses Style / RV203 | Evidence-disciplined device research, architecture mapping, qualified protocol work, and explicit documentation of what remains unproven. |
| [CyanBridge / Alternative HeyCyan App and SDK](cyanbridge.md) | HeyCyan-compatible glasses and experimental multi-vendor interoperability | Alternative companion software that converts device research into reusable owner-facing software while preserving device-specific validation boundaries. |

These projects are included as standing Community Research resources independently of any GlassesResearch editorial coverage. Their profiles should remain useful even after individual articles rotate out of prominence.

## Evidence rule

A project's own claims remain attributed community evidence unless GlassesResearch separately reproduces them. Experimental, partial, inferred, or unverified capabilities must remain labeled as such. Community inclusion does not automatically change a model's Verified status, Report Card scores, or canonical specifications.
