# Smart-Glasses Historical Archive

A sourced chronology behind the [interactive Industry Timeline](INDUSTRY_TIMELINE.md).

## 2013 — Google Glass Explorer ecosystem

Google Glass helped establish the modern smart-glasses developer ecosystem. On April 10, 2013, Google Ventures, Andreessen Horowitz and Kleiner Perkins announced the **Glass Collective**, a funding initiative for entrepreneurs building applications around Glass. This is useful historical evidence that Glass was already being treated as an application platform rather than merely a hardware experiment.

**Source:** [Google — Introducing the Glass Collective](https://blog.google/alphabet/introducing-glass-collective/)  
**Related model:** GLS-0043 Google Glass Explorer Edition

## 2016 — Snap enters eyewear hardware

On September 24, 2016, Snapchat renamed itself **Snap Inc.** while announcing that it was building products beyond the Snapchat application, explicitly naming **Spectacles**. That launch began a hardware line that later progressed from camera glasses to standalone see-through AR developer hardware.

**Source:** [Snap — Snap Inc.](https://newsroom.snap.com/snap-inc)  
**Related model:** GLS-0040 Spectacles (1st Gen)

## 2019 — Glass becomes an Android enterprise platform

Google announced **Glass Enterprise Edition 2** on May 20, 2019. The device moved to Qualcomm Snapdragon XR1, added USB-C, improved camera and battery performance, and used Android to simplify enterprise software integration and management.

**Source:** [Google — Glass Enterprise Edition 2](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/glass-enterprise-edition-2/)  
**Related model:** GLS-0045 Glass Enterprise Edition 2

## 2021 — Ray-Ban Stories normalizes camera glasses

Meta and EssilorLuxottica launched **Ray-Ban Stories** on September 9, 2021. Cameras, microphones and open-ear audio were placed into conventional Ray-Ban frames, creating the product lineage that later became Ray-Ban Meta.

**Source:** [Meta — Introducing Ray-Ban Stories](https://about.fb.com/news/2021/09/introducing-ray-ban-stories-smart-glasses/)  
**Related model:** GLS-0001 Ray-Ban Stories

## 2023 — Ray-Ban Meta shifts the category toward AI

Meta announced the next-generation **Ray-Ban Meta** glasses on September 27, 2023 with improved cameras/audio, livestreaming and Meta AI integration. This generation became an important reference point for camera/audio AI glasses without an in-lens display.

**Source:** [Meta — New Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/09/new-ray-ban-meta-smart-glasses/)  
**Related model:** GLS-0002 Ray-Ban Meta (Gen 1)

## 2024 — Even G1 demonstrates a different path

Even Realities launched **Even G1** in June 2024. Unlike camera-first AI glasses, G1 used a discreet binocular text display and omitted cameras and speakers, focusing on navigation, teleprompter, translation and glanceable information. Even later announced open-source support during the same year.

**Source:** [Even Realities — 2024 highlights](https://www.evenrealities.com/de-US/blog/a-year-of-innovation-even-realities-2024)  
**Related model:** GLS-0047 Even G1

## 2024 — Snap Spectacles become a standalone AR developer platform

Snap introduced fifth-generation **Spectacles** and **Snap OS** on September 17, 2024. This generation offered standalone see-through AR and was distributed through a developer subscription rather than ordinary consumer retail.

**Source:** [Snap — SPS 2024 Spectacles and Snap OS](https://newsroom.snap.com/sps-2024-spectacles-snapos)  
**Related model:** GLS-0068 Spectacles (5th Gen)

## 2024 — XREAL moves spatial processing onto display glasses

The **XREAL One** generation placed XREAL's X1 spatial-computing chip in the glasses, allowing spatial display behavior without requiring the same companion-compute architecture as earlier generations.

**Sources:** [XREAL history](https://www.xreal.com/us/about) · [XREAL One documentation](https://tutorials.xreal.com/docs/glasses/one-series/spec/)  
**Related model:** GLS-0074 XREAL One

## 2024 — Android XR creates a common Google platform for glasses

Google announced **Android XR** on December 12, 2024 with Samsung and Qualcomm. The platform explicitly targets both headsets and glasses and gives the emerging industry a shared Android/OpenXR development layer rather than requiring every manufacturer to create a fully independent software stack.

**Source:** [Google — Android XR](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/android-xr/)

## 2025 — Rokid combines everyday AI glasses with a display

Rokid launched **Rokid Glasses** globally in September 2025. The product combines a 49 g frame, dual-eye MicroLED waveguide display, Snapdragon AR1, 12 MP camera, microphones, open-ear audio and real-time translation—bridging the camera/audio and display-glasses categories.

**Source:** [Rokid launch announcement](https://global.rokid.com/blogs/news/rokid-launches-worlds-lightest-full-function-ai-ar-smart-glasses)  
**Related model:** GLS-0064 Rokid Glasses

## 2025 — Snap OS 2.0 adds browser and utility computing

Snap introduced **Snap OS 2.0** on September 15, 2025 with a redesigned browser, home-screen widgets and broader utility features, showing the Spectacles platform moving beyond isolated AR lenses toward more general-purpose wearable computing.

**Source:** [Snap — Introducing Snap OS 2.0](https://newsroom.snap.com/introducing-snap-os-2.0)  
**Related model:** GLS-0068 Spectacles (5th Gen)

## 2025 — Meta adds a display and neural wrist input to Ray-Ban

On September 17, 2025, Meta introduced **Meta Ray-Ban Display** and the **Meta Neural Band**, an EMG wristband used for subtle hand input. The glasses brought an in-lens full-color display into Meta's consumer eyewear line while Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 continued the camera/audio branch.

**Source:** [Meta — Meta Ray-Ban Display](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/meta-ray-ban-display-ai-glasses-emg-wristband/)  
**Related models:** GLS-0003 Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2; GLS-0004 Meta Ray-Ban Display
