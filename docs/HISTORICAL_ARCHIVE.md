# Historical Archive

The [Industry Timeline](INDUSTRY_TIMELINE.md) is the visual index of major smart-glasses milestones. This archive is the durable supporting layer behind it.

## What the archive contains

Archive records may cover product launches, discontinuations, company changes, major firmware or platform shifts, SDK and API releases, standards developments, open-source milestones, regulatory events, important research results, and other events that materially changed the smart-glasses ecosystem.

Each durable event record should contain:

- a stable event identifier;
- date or date range;
- concise statement of what happened;
- why the event mattered;
- affected models, companies, technologies, or projects;
- source links;
- event state when relevant: occurred, announced, delayed, cancelled, or provisional;
- related timeline and model links.

## Timeline relationship

The timeline should remain readable even as the archive grows. High-significance events can be promoted to the main visual graph; lower-significance events may remain in the detailed chronology without cluttering the primary view.

Announced future events must remain visually distinct from occurred events. When an anticipated event reaches its expected date, the research process should determine what actually happened rather than silently converting the announcement into history.

## Citation and reuse

Stable event IDs and permalinks are intended to make individual milestones citable by researchers, journalists, developers, and other sites. The event page—not an uncited visual marker—should carry the evidence needed to evaluate the claim.
