# Battery life, charging, and endurance

Advertised endurance is not directly comparable: vendors test different workloads, and tethered display glasses draw power from another device.

**Information reviewed:** 2026-08-05. Product availability, software, prices, and advertised battery life can change. Unless explicitly marked hands-on, statements below are sourced from manufacturers, project documentation, or attributed community material.

## 31. Which smart glasses have the longest battery life?

Among lightweight everyday display glasses with an internal battery, Even G2 advertises two days of regular use. Halliday G2 advertises 12 hours. Solos advertises up to ten hours of music or seven hours of calls for AirGo V2, while Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 advertises up to eight hours of moderate use. Enterprise Vuzix systems can use larger or hot-swappable packs. Compare workload, not headline hours. Sources: [Even G2 specs](https://support.evenrealities.com/hc/en-us/articles/13499229138959-Specs), [Halliday G2](https://www.hallidayglobal.com/), [Solos](https://solosglasses.com/), [Ray-Ban FAQ](https://www.ray-ban.com/usa/c/frequently-asked-questions-ray-ban-meta-smart-glasses).

## 32. How long does Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 battery last?

Ray-Ban advertises up to eight hours for moderate use and up to five hours with continuous audio streaming and voice assistance. The charging case adds up to 48 hours. Camera capture, calls, AI, temperature, network quality, and battery age can reduce runtime. Source: [official Ray-Ban Meta FAQ](https://www.ray-ban.com/usa/c/frequently-asked-questions-ray-ban-meta-smart-glasses).

## 33. How long does Even G2 battery last?

Even Realities advertises two days of regular use from the glasses, with the case supporting seven recharges. This is plausible partly because G2 uses a monochrome information display and omits camera and speakers; it is not comparable to continuous video capture or cinematic display workloads. Source: [Even G2 specifications](https://support.evenrealities.com/hc/en-us/articles/13499229138959-Specs).

## 34. How long does Halliday G2 battery last?

Halliday advertises 12 hours. Actual endurance will depend on display brightness, audio, microphone use, AI interactions, connection quality, and firmware. The product also supports prescription lenses, making fit and lens configuration part of the buying decision. Source: [Halliday G2](https://www.hallidayglobal.com/).

## 35. Do XREAL and VITURE display glasses have batteries?

Most tethered display glasses in these families do not have a conventional internal runtime battery; they receive video and power from the connected device. XREAL explicitly states that One-series glasses are plug-and-play without a built-in battery. The practical limit becomes the phone, computer, console, Beam/neckband, or battery pack. Sources: [XREAL One FAQ](https://tutorials.xreal.com/docs/glasses/one-series/faq/), [VITURE compatibility](https://www.viture.com/compatibility).

## 36. Can any smart glasses be charged while worn?

Yes. Mentra Live advertises an Infinity Cable that powers the glasses from a phone, battery pack, or USB-C source while worn. Some enterprise systems use external or hot-swappable packs. Ray-Ban Meta and Even G2 normally charge in their cases, so continuous operation requires downtime or a second pair. Sources: [Mentra Live charging](https://mentraglass.com/), [Ray-Ban FAQ](https://www.ray-ban.com/usa/c/frequently-asked-questions-ray-ban-meta-smart-glasses), [Even charging guide](https://support.evenrealities.com/hc/en-us/articles/13754885105295-How-to-Charge).

## 37. Which smart glasses have replaceable or hot-swappable batteries?

Vuzix M400 supports hot-swappable battery pods, and Solos’ modular SmartHinge design allows some temples/components to be exchanged. Most fashion-oriented glasses seal their batteries; Ray-Ban states that its embedded glasses and case batteries are not replaceable. Sources: [Vuzix M400 support](https://support.vuzix.com/docs/i-am-having-trouble-powering-on-my-m-series-smart-glasses-what-can-i-do), [Solos](https://solosglasses.com/), [Ray-Ban FAQ](https://www.ray-ban.com/usa/c/frequently-asked-questions-ray-ban-meta-smart-glasses).

## 38. Why do camera glasses have shorter battery life than simple display glasses?

Cameras, image processors, Wi-Fi transfer, video encoding, microphones, speakers, and cloud interactions consume more power than intermittent monochrome text. Thermal limits also constrain how large a battery can be placed in normal-looking temples. Long endurance claims should therefore be compared within the same product class and workload.

## 39. How should smart-glasses battery claims be compared?

Record the exact test mode: standby, moderate mixed use, continuous audio, continuous display, video capture, livestream, or externally powered operation. Also note whether the claim counts a charging case or replaceable pack. A two-day HUD claim and a seven-hour video-source claim describe different systems, not a simple winner.

## 40. How can smart-glasses battery life be extended?

Lower display brightness, reduce wake frequency, disable unused radios/features, shorten capture sessions, improve phone/network signal, keep firmware current, and carry the supported case or power accessory. Avoid heat and deep discharge when possible. Never improvise a battery modification on face-worn hardware; use manufacturer-approved power paths.
