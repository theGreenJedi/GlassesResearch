# Ask Your Own Question

Didn't find your question in the existing 100 Questions? Ask it.

> **No question is too basic or too advanced. If you couldn't easily find the answer, someone else probably can't either.**

GlassesResearch uses community questions as research prompts. Questions are not treated as answered merely because someone has an opinion or a forum post exists. They enter a visible research queue and move forward only as evidence improves.

## What you can submit

- a smart-glasses or AI-glasses question
- a correction to an existing answer
- a newly discovered model
- a manual, datasheet, SDK, APK, firmware reference, or recovery guide
- BLE captures or protocol notes from an owned or authorized device
- photos, teardown evidence, or repair observations
- research papers, regulatory filings, or manufacturer documentation
- a claim that needs verification

## How to submit

Open a GitHub issue in this repository and begin the title with `Question:`.

Include as much of the following as you know:

```text
Question:
Model / platform (if relevant):
Why you are asking:
What you have already found:
Source links:
Hands-on evidence (if any):
Country / region (if relevant):
```

Do not include private credentials, personal data, unlawfully distributed files, or captures from devices or accounts you are not authorized to inspect.

## What happens next

Submitted questions are added to the [Community Questions](COMMUNITY_QUESTIONS.md) queue and given a status:

- **Received** — captured and awaiting triage.
- **Under Investigation** — research or testing is active.
- **Answered** — a supported answer exists and is linked.
- **Added to Knowledge Base** — the result has been promoted into a model chapter, guide, FAQ, glossary entry, or other canonical research page.
- **Unable to Verify** — evidence remains insufficient or contradictory.
- **Removed — Off Topic** — the question does not materially concern smart glasses, AI glasses, wearable display systems, their software, hardware, ownership, privacy, accessibility, development, repair, history, or closely related research.

A broadly useful answered question may eventually become part of the main FAQ collection.

## Moderation

GlassesResearch is a focused research hub, not a general discussion board. Questions that are clearly off topic, spam, promotional, abusive, duplicative without adding new information, or otherwise unrelated to the project's research scope may be removed from the public Community Questions queue.

Removal from the public queue is editorial housekeeping, not a judgment about the person asking. When practical, an associated GitHub issue may be closed with a short reason. The public research queue does not need to preserve material that does not belong in the research corpus.
