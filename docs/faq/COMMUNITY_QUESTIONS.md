# Community Questions

This is the public research queue for questions submitted by readers, contributors, device owners, developers, and researchers.

Questions stay visible as they move from intake to investigation and, when evidence is strong enough, into the permanent knowledge base. Questions that are clearly outside the scope of GlassesResearch may be removed from the public queue.

## Status key

- **Received** — captured and awaiting triage.
- **Under Investigation** — research or testing is active.
- **Answered** — a supported answer exists and is linked.
- **Added to Knowledge Base** — promoted into a canonical research page.
- **Unable to Verify** — current evidence is insufficient or contradictory.
- **Removed — Off Topic** — removed from the public queue because it does not materially relate to smart glasses, AI glasses, wearable display systems, or closely related research.

## Moderation rule

The maintainers may remove a question from this public queue when it is clearly off topic, spam, promotional, abusive, or a duplicate that adds no new research value. Off-topic questions do not need to remain in the published archive.

If the question originated as a GitHub issue, the issue may be closed with a concise explanation. Questions should not be removed merely because they are difficult, unpopular, critical of a manufacturer, or currently unanswered.

## Promotion rule

A question may be promoted into the main FAQ when it is broadly useful and has a sufficiently supported answer. Model-specific answers should normally land in that model's chapter first, with the Community Questions entry linking to the canonical destination.

The queue is not a popularity contest. Interesting unanswered questions remain visible rather than disappearing simply because they are difficult.
