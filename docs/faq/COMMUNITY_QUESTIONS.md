# Community Questions

This is the public research queue for questions submitted by readers, contributors, device owners, developers, and researchers.

Questions stay visible as they move from intake to investigation and, when evidence is strong enough, into the permanent knowledge base.

## Status key

- **Received** — captured and awaiting triage.
- **Under Investigation** — research or testing is active.
- **Answered** — a supported answer exists and is linked.
- **Added to Knowledge Base** — promoted into a canonical research page.
- **Unable to Verify** — current evidence is insufficient or contradictory.

## Promotion rule

A question may be promoted into the main FAQ when it is broadly useful and has a sufficiently supported answer. Model-specific answers should normally land in that model's chapter first, with the Community Questions entry linking to the canonical destination.

The queue is not a popularity contest. Interesting unanswered questions remain visible rather than disappearing simply because they are difficult.
