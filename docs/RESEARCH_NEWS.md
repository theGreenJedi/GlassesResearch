### August 11, 2026 — Courts in England and Wales prohibit Meta smart glasses

His Majesty's Courts and Tribunals Service confirmed that Meta smart glasses are prohibited in court and tribunal buildings because of restrictions on unauthorized recording. This is a device-specific institutional restriction, not a general public-space ban.

Source: [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/aug/11/meta-glasses-banned-from-courts-in-england-and-wales)

### August 10, 2026 — Meta releases Muse Glimmer for local agent workflows

Meta AI Research released Muse Glimmer, an open-weight local-agent model relevant to owner-controlled smart-glasses architectures. The significance for GlassesResearch is architectural: perception and agent logic can increasingly live on wearer-controlled nearby hardware rather than exclusively inside a vendor cloud.

Continue: [Local AI Agents and Smart Glasses](../hacking/LOCAL_AI_AGENTS.md)
