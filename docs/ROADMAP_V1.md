# GlassesResearch Roadmap v1.0

## Framework status

**Roadmap Version 1.0: framework complete**

GlassesResearch now has the major public structures needed to support ongoing research without continuing to expand the site for its own sake.

Completed v1 capabilities include:

- searchable model catalog and model pages;
- evidence-backed comparison framework;
- industry timeline and historical archive structure;
- research and news center;
- developer and open-development resources;
- research library and technical-artifact preservation records;
- practical FAQ and buyer guidance;
- whole-site search and simplified navigation;
- guided visitor entry paths;
- public research standards and correction/challenge process;
- contributor recognition framework;
- privacy-conscious site design and analytics direction.

## Current phase: Evidence Acquisition

The default question for future work is no longer “what section should we add?” It is:

> What useful uncertainty can we reduce?

Future work should primarily add, verify, preserve, correct, or better connect real smart-glasses information. New infrastructure is justified only when the research itself exposes a genuine need.

## Standing principles

**Information first. Infrastructure second.**

**Discover → Verify → Preserve → Organize → Cross-reference.**

**Every unknown should either become verified or have a documented reason why verification is currently impossible.**

## What v1.0 does not mean

Framework complete does not mean research complete. The model catalog, evidence library, comparison data, timeline, developer resources, preservation archive, and FAQ are living collections. Their value should increase continuously as verified information replaces unknowns and concrete resources replace placeholders.
