# Evidence-derived profiles — Magic Leap

## Magic Leap One / Magic Leap 1

Magic Leap's first-generation platform belongs in the wearable-HCI museum as a real commercial head-worn AR system, but its long-term ownership story is now as important as its original technical ambition. Authorized purchasing ended in 2022, and Magic Leap shut down first-generation support, OS updates and cloud services at the end of 2024. Because the surviving first-party public archive no longer exposes enough of the original specification/developer package for a clean fixed-ruler reconstruction, most scores remain explicitly ungraded rather than being filled from memory. What can be graded is the ownership lesson: a sophisticated wearable can still lose meaningful capability when its vendor ecosystem disappears.

## Magic Leap 2

Magic Leap 2 is a much better fit for the repository's owner-control questions. It remains a proprietary enterprise headset, but its AOSP-based OS, public SDKs, OpenXR/Unity/Unreal/native workflows, sample code and supported OS flashing give developers a substantial surface to build and manage their own spatial applications. Dedicated CV/ML compute, 16 GB RAM, 256 GB NVMe, eye/hand tracking, spatial mapping and an advanced AR display put the hardware near the top of the adjacent wearable catalog. Its weakness is not capability but form factor and lifecycle: it is still a dedicated headset, and Magic Leap has already announced the 2026 sales wind-down.
