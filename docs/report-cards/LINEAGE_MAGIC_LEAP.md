# Lineage Research — Magic Leap

**Research date:** 2026-08-12

## Lineage finding

Magic Leap belongs on the adjacent head-worn AR shelf rather than in the smart-glasses count. The commercial lineage resolves into a **first-generation Magic Leap One / Magic Leap 1 family** and **Magic Leap 2**. Creator Edition and later first-generation enterprise packaging are treated as configurations of the first-generation platform unless hardware evidence establishes otherwise.

### Magic Leap One / Magic Leap 1

Magic Leap's current legal and support archive explicitly identifies Magic Leap One: Creator Edition and Magic Leap 1 as the first-generation augmented-reality ecosystem. Authorized purchases ended December 31, 2022. Support, OS updates and cloud services ended December 31, 2024. Magic Leap states that core functionality, including device and apps, has reached end-of-life and that the cloud services are no longer available.

Because the surviving first-party public archive no longer exposes a sufficiently complete first-generation specification/developer package in this research pass, hardware/display/software scores are deliberately left ungraded rather than reconstructed from secondary memory. The cloud-independence result, however, is evidence-backed and poor in long-term ownership terms because vendor cloud shutdown materially affected the ecosystem.

### Magic Leap 2

Magic Leap 2 is a distinct second-generation enterprise AR system consisting of headset, compute pack and controller. Current manufacturer material documents 16 GB LPDDR5 RAM, 256 GB NVMe storage, dedicated CV/ML compute blocks, Wi-Fi 6, up to 3.5 hours battery life, 6DoF controller, hand tracking, eye tracking, voice, on-device spatial mapping and Dynamic Dimming. Magic Leap states the OS is based on AOSP and provides SDKs, developer tools, OpenXR, Unity/Unreal/native workflows, code examples and the ability to flash device OS versions.

Magic Leap announced that direct sales of Magic Leap 2 end March 31, 2026, while authorized resellers may continue delivery through December 31, 2026. This is therefore a commercial but winding-down enterprise platform, not an unreleased device.

### Common-ruler rationale

- **Magic Leap 1:** long-term owner control/cloud independence are penalized because Magic Leap explicitly shut down first-generation cloud services and describes device/apps as end-of-life. Other dimensions remain ungraded until primary archival specifications and developer materials are recovered.
- **Magic Leap 2 Hardware 9.0:** high-end standalone/compute-pack spatial hardware with dedicated CV/ML acceleration, 16 GB RAM, 256 GB NVMe and rich tracking.
- **Wearability 3.0:** lighter/better balanced than many full headsets but still a dedicated head-worn AR system rather than ordinary eyewear.
- **Visual AI 8.0:** substantial native spatial/computer-vision capabilities and dedicated ML/CVIP compute, though not primarily a consumer conversational visual-AI product.
- **Software 9.0:** mature enterprise AR OS, SDK, OpenXR, Unity, Unreal and native-development workflows.
- **Openness 7.5 / Hackability 8.0:** proprietary platform, but AOSP foundation, public developer tooling, sample code and supported OS flashing create a serious development surface. License restrictions and closed hardware keep it below open-device benchmarks.
- **Owner Control 8.0:** owners/developers can build, install and manage custom applications and enterprise deployments; the platform is not merely a vendor assistant.
- **Cloud Independence 8.5:** Magic Leap 2 is a standalone spatial computer and local apps/spatial mapping can operate on-device, while some services/licensing/management can still involve Magic Leap infrastructure.
- **Display/HUD 9.5:** a purpose-built immersive AR display system at the top end of the adjacent wearable catalog.
- **Value:** left ungraded pending a clean primary-source acquisition price in the current evidence package.
