# Report Card Research Batch 03

This batch applies the catalog-wide common ruler to four closely related Meta/EssilorLuxottica models. Shared architecture does not imply shared scores: camera generation, battery endurance, durability and sport-specific design are graded separately, while the common Meta AI dependency is reflected consistently in Openness, Owner Control, Cloud Independence and Hackability.

## GLS-0002 — Ray-Ban Meta (Gen 1)

**Canonical check:** no correction required. The catalog correctly separates the 2023 Ray-Ban Meta generation from Ray-Ban Stories and the later Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 hardware.

**Evidence basis:** Meta documents the 2023 generation as a redesigned successor to Stories with improved audio, a 12 MP ultra-wide camera, livestreaming, Meta AI, open-ear speakers and conventional Ray-Ban frame options. Later updates added Meta AI with Vision, allowing the assistant to answer questions about what the wearer is seeing.

| Wearability | 9.0 | A | Ray-Ban styling, multiple frame/lens options and everyday-eyewear proportions are major strengths. |
| Visual AI | 8.5 | A- | The 12 MP first-person camera plus Meta AI with Vision makes scene understanding a core use case. |
| Software | 8.0 | B+ | Mature capture, calling, audio, translation and Meta AI features, but almost entirely through Meta-controlled software. |
| Display / HUD | N/A | N/A | No visual display is part of this product architecture. |

## Batch result

Four additional canonical models now have explicit evidence-backed Report Cards under the shared catalog ruler. The strongest pattern is architectural: Meta's family scores high in wearability, camera hardware, visual AI and consumer software while consistently scoring low in openness, owner control, cloud independence and hackability. The common ruler makes that tradeoff visible instead of allowing polished features to inflate ownership-related scores.
