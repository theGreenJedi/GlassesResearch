# CY03 MAX — GLS-0247

CY03 MAX is a low-cost camera/audio smart-glasses model circulating through an OEM/ODM marketplace channel. GlassesResearch has not acquired or independently tested a specimen. This profile therefore records commercial identity and the current evidence boundary rather than treating supplier specifications as verified performance.

## Research snapshot

The captured supplier listing markets camera capture, voice translation, an AI/“ChatGPT Assistant,” Bluetooth music and calling, touch control, a 290 mAh battery, IP65 protection and USB-C charging. These remain supplier claims. In particular, “ChatGPT Assistant” does not establish an official OpenAI integration, model provider, cloud path, or local inference capability.

## What remains unresolved

The upstream designer/manufacturer, chipset and camera identity, companion application, account requirements, local storage behavior, Bluetooth/Wi-Fi traffic, cloud endpoints, offline behavior, owner control and rebadged retail identities remain investigation targets.

The evidence-limited Report Card preserves those unknowns rather than converting marketplace copy into GlassesResearch verification.
