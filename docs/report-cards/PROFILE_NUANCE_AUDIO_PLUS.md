# Nuance Audio Plus — GLS-0249

Nuance Audio Plus is the second named Nuance hearing-glasses generation. Manufacturer material documents stronger amplification, improved speech clarity in noise, more natural own-voice perception, increased battery capacity, physical power/volume controls, and expanded frame/color options.

## Evidence-limited Report Card

**Hardware:** manufacturer-primary evidence establishes a revised control/battery implementation. Independent teardown and battery measurement remain pending.

**Wearability:** Square, Panthos and Upturn styles are documented. Comfort and acoustic performance remain manufacturer/clinical claims until independently reproduced.

**Visual AI:** not claimed; this is a camera-free hearing product.

**Software:** app and remote-control paths are documented. Compatible iPhones can use the documented double-tap call feature; the manufacturer identifies the feature as MFi/iPhone-specific.

**Openness / Owner Control / Cloud Independence / Hackability:** currently evidence-limited. No strong score should be inferred without artifact-level investigation.

**Value:** commercial price and prescription options should be refreshed from manufacturer/optical channels when scored.
