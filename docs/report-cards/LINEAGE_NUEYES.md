# Lineage Research — NuEyes

**Research date:** 2026-08-12

## Lineage finding

NuEyes has two overlapping assistive-smart-glasses branches: an early self-contained low-vision headset line that culminates in e2+, and a lighter tethered Pro line that evolves through Pro 3, Pro 3e and Pro 4. NuEyes' own history says the earliest devices were bulky and that two subsequent generations became smaller before the Pro 3 pivot; those unnamed/poorly archived predecessors remain lineage context rather than invented catalog rows.

### e2+

NuEyes currently documents e2+ as a standalone assistive headset with Snapdragon 835, 64 GB storage, 4K display, 101° FOV, 16 MP autofocus camera, 3DoF tracking, OCR/text-to-speech, up to 18x magnification and 268 g weight. It has a direct buy route and does not require a phone.

### Pro 3

NuEyes announced Pro 3 in December 2020 as a three-version AR smart-glasses family with 4K displays, roughly 51° FOV and 88 g weight. The flagship controller version included dual 6DoF cameras, a 20 MP RGB camera and an SDK covering SLAM, object/speech/gesture recognition and plane detection; Pro 3-C used USB-C DisplayPort and a 5 MP camera; a thermal variant targeted enterprise use. NuEyes explicitly stated sale beginning 2020-12-18 with shipments planned for late January.

### Pro 3e

Pro 3e was announced in April 2021 as a lighter display-centric branch and is still documented by NuEyes: 68 g, birdbath optics, 1920×1080 at 75 Hz, 2000 nits, 3DoF IMU, stereo audio and USB-C DisplayPort. It is a plug-and-play display peripheral rather than a standalone computer.

### Pro 4

Pro 4 is the current successor: 102 g, binocular 1080p OLED, 43° FOV, 12 MP camera, IMU/light/distance sensors and USB-C connection to a phone/tablet/computer. NuEyes offers standard, federal/DoD and low-vision bundles.

### Common-ruler notes

- **e2+** scores as a capable but bulky self-contained assistive computer; OCR is real visual processing, but not a modern general visual-AI assistant.
- **Pro 3** receives the strongest openness/hackability marks because NuEyes explicitly documented an SDK with SLAM and recognition capabilities.
- **Pro 3e** earns maximum cloud independence for core display operation because it is fundamentally a USB-C DisplayPort peripheral; cloud dependence belongs to whatever host software the owner chooses.
- **Pro 4** remains host-powered/tethered and therefore owner-friendly at the hardware layer, while its low-vision software bundle is more vendor-specific.
