# Evidence-derived profiles — OrCam MyEye

## OrCam MyEye 2 Pro

MyEye 2 Pro is one of the clearest examples of visual AI without a visual display. A tiny module attached to ordinary eyeglasses uses its camera and local recognition stack to read text, identify faces and products, recognize money and colors, and speak the result to the wearer. The architecture gives it exceptional wearability and historically strong offline operation, but almost no meaningful owner programmability: the AI is useful, mature and tightly controlled by OrCam.

## OrCam MyEye 3 Pro

MyEye 3 Pro keeps the same eyeglass-mounted assistive concept while layering in Smart Magnifier functions and a more conversational AI assistant. That expands what the wearer can ask of captured visual information, but it also nudges the product away from the earlier generation's unusually pure offline model because some new assistant functions can rely on outside data. It remains a highly capable accessibility device rather than a developer platform, and its four-thousand-dollar-plus price makes value much harder to defend outside its specialized use case.
