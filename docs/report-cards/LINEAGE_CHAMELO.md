# Lineage Research — Chamelo Smart Eyewear

**Research date:** 2026-08-12

## Lineage finding

Chamelo is best treated as a smart-lens eyewear maker with multiple product families rather than one linear camera/AI lineage. The recurring technical branches are Eclipse/Dusk tint-adjustable eyewear, Music Shield tint + audio sport eyewear, and Aura/Prismatic color-changing eyewear. NBA and colorway editions are styling/licensing variants, not separate hardware generations unless future evidence shows changed electronics.

### Dusk / Eclipse branch

Dusk Classic is a conventional-looking electrochromic/tint-adjustable sunglass with optional built-in audio and app-enabled tint control. It is a true retail smart-eyewear product but not a display or visual-AI device.

### Music Shield branch

Music Shield combines fast electronic tint control with open-ear audio in a sport shield form. Music Shield Gen 2 is an explicit second generation with revised fit, durability, controls and more directional audio. Current manufacturer pages document 51 g weight, 62 hours tint-adjustment battery life, 6 hours audio battery life, IPX4, 0.1-second tint transitions and direct retail purchase around $260-$299 depending on configuration.

### Aura / Prismatic branch

Aura uses electronically controlled Prismatic color-changing lenses rather than a HUD. Current manufacturer pages document four instant color states, IPX4 construction, roughly 35 g weight, 47-hour lens-control battery life and prescription-ready configurations. Audio and non-audio SKUs are configurations within the Aura family, not automatically separate generations.

### Common-ruler rationale

- These are electronically adaptive eyewear products, not visual-display or camera-AI systems, so **Visual AI** and **Display/HUD** are N/A.
- **Wearability** is unusually strong because the electronics remain inside conventional sunglass or sport-eyewear forms.
- **Owner Control** and **Cloud Independence** are strong because the core function—lens state and, where present, Bluetooth audio—does not inherently require a vendor cloud; Music Shield G2 explicitly separates tint control from Bluetooth pairing.
- **Openness/Hackability** remain low because no meaningful public SDK, firmware source or hardware-development interface was found.
- **Value** is comparatively strong because current retail pricing is hundreds rather than thousands of dollars.
