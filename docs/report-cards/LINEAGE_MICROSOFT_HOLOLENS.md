# Lineage Research — Microsoft HoloLens

**Research date:** 2026-08-12

## Lineage finding

HoloLens is an adjacent head-worn mixed-reality lineage rather than smart glasses under the repository's form-factor taxonomy. The hardware generations are **HoloLens (1st gen)** and **HoloLens 2**. Development and Industrial editions of HoloLens 2 are procurement/support configurations on the same core hardware rather than separate generations.

### HoloLens (1st gen)

Microsoft documents the first HoloLens as a fully untethered holographic computer with dual see-through waveguide displays, four environment-understanding cameras, depth sensing, a 2 MP photo/video camera, IMU, microphones, spatial audio, Intel 32-bit compute, a custom HPU, 2 GB RAM, 64 GB flash, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth and 2–3 hours active battery life. It weighs 579 g and runs Windows 10 Holographic. Microsoft ended monthly servicing after December 2024, but the device continues to function.

### HoloLens 2

HoloLens 2 is the second generation and substantially upgrades sensing and interaction: Snapdragon 850 plus second-generation HPU, 4 GB RAM, 64 GB UFS, 2k 3:2 light engines, four visible-light tracking cameras, two IR eye-tracking cameras, 1 MP ToF depth sensing, 8 MP still / 1080p30 camera, fully articulated hand tracking, eye tracking, iris authentication, spatial mapping, Wi-Fi 5, Bluetooth 5 and USB-C. It weighs 566 g and remains a full headset, not eyewear. Microsoft states HoloLens 2 is no longer manufactured and is out of stock in all geographies.

### Common-ruler rationale

- **Hardware:** both generations were unusually complete standalone spatial computers for their periods; HoloLens 2 adds eye/hand tracking, stronger compute and richer depth sensing.
- **Wearability:** despite improved balance, 566–579 g headsets are far from everyday glasses and score accordingly.
- **Visual AI:** native spatial mapping, tracking and environment understanding are substantial computer-vision capabilities, though not the same as modern general-purpose conversational visual AI.
- **Software:** Windows Holographic plus a mature mixed-reality application/development stack is among the stronger software platforms in the wearable catalog.
- **Openness/Hackability:** these are proprietary devices, but Microsoft supplied public development tooling, Windows/Unity/MRTK workflows and substantial developer access, placing them well above appliance-style glasses but below open-hardware benchmarks.
- **Owner Control:** standalone app execution and custom enterprise applications provide substantial control relative to cloud-assistant-bound consumer eyewear.
- **Cloud Independence:** core spatial computing and local apps can operate without Azure, though account/setup and many enterprise services can use Microsoft cloud. HoloLens 2 requires account/network setup on first use, lowering the score slightly.
- **Display/HUD:** both generations are true stereoscopic spatial-display systems; HoloLens 2 approaches the top of the catalog for integrated head-worn AR display capability.
- **Value:** left ungraded in this pass because a clean contemporaneous primary-source acquisition price was not recovered in the evidence package.
