# Xiaomi Mijia Glasses Camera — Report Card

## GLS-0023 — Mijia Glasses Camera (2022, MJSV01FC)

**Canonical check:** identity blocker resolved. A Beijing municipal-government product list names Beijing Fengchao Century Technology Co., Ltd., “Glasses Camera,” and model `MJSV01FC`. That primary record converges with Xiaomi-hosted product coverage, a Bluetooth-certification index, contemporaneous reporting and peer-reviewed use records. This is the 2022 camera/display branch, not any Mijia audio-glasses generation.

**Evidence basis:** Source-bounded evidence establishes two cameras, a near-eye Sony Micro OLED/free-form optical display, first-person capture, a companion workflow and the `MJSV01FC` identity. Period reporting and commercial records describe approximately 100 g weight, 50 MP wide and 8 MP telephoto cameras, 32 GB storage, Snapdragon compute, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, 1,020 mAh battery and IPX4. Those exact specifications remain secondary until a manufacturer manual or direct regulatory exhibit is preserved.

## Evidence boundary

The Beijing government list is primary evidence for the product/model/company mapping, not a complete technical specification. Xiaomi-hosted trial coverage is stronger than an unaffiliated seller but is not treated as a formal datasheet. Exact hardware numbers remain explicitly secondary/commercial. Unknown software-survival, owner-access and offline behavior are scored conservatively rather than converted into negative facts.
