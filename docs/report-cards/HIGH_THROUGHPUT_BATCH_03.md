## Batch conclusions

This lineage packet exposes an important catalog-wide pattern. Host-driven XR glasses often score lower than camera/AI glasses in Visual AI because that capability simply is not their purpose, but they score exceptionally well in **Cloud Independence and practical Owner Control**: the wearer chooses the phone, PC, console or other compute source, and the display keeps working without a manufacturer's AI cloud. XREAL Air 2 Ultra is the major exception on sensing because its spatial sensors and developer APIs move it toward true AR. XREAL One Pro and VITURE Luma Pro currently sit near the top of the consumer Display/HUD scale, while Brilliant-class devices remain far ahead in openness/hackability.
