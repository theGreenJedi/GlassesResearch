# Lucyd Lyte lineage — evidence and Report Cards

This packet grades Lyte XL only from evidence that names the XL generation. Lyte 2.0 hardware claims are not inherited.

## GLS-0033 — Lucyd Lyte XL

**Evidence lane:** company-primary and SEC disclosure. No GlassesResearch hands-on claims are made.

Lyte XL is screen-free Bluetooth audio eyewear built around ordinary-looking, optician-serviceable frames. Its October 2023 changes focused on comfort and fit—spring hinges, thinner temples, wider dimensions—plus improved call and audio quality. Phone voice assistants and Lucyd's application add connected functions, but the core product remains a Bluetooth peripheral rather than an onboard AI computer.

That simple architecture is simultaneously a limitation and a survival advantage. There is no display, camera or meaningful public firmware/SDK surface, yet music and calls are not inherently trapped behind one vendor cloud. Owners can also use ordinary optical channels rather than treating the frame as sealed electronics.
