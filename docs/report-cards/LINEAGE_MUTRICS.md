# Lineage Research — Mutrics Smart Audio Eyewear

**Research date:** 2026-08-12

## Lineage finding

The surviving public record supports a Mutrics consumer smart-audio-glasses family centered on the M1 / commercially released Mutrics audio sunglasses generation. Manufacturer-origin archival material is incomplete, so this pass deliberately avoids multiplying style names or later marketing variants into separate hardware generations without evidence of materially different electronics.

A 2019 Japanese distributor release documents nationwide retail launch of Mutrics smart sunglasses on 2019-12-28 at a suggested price of ¥12,545 before tax. Red Dot's design archive independently identifies the Mutrics M1 as a smart-glasses product made by Mutrics Innovation Intelligence Co., Ltd. and describes integrated bone-conduction/audio functionality. Together these establish real commercial availability and product identity, but not a sufficiently complete first-party specification archive to justify aggressive generation splitting.

### Common-ruler rationale

- **Hardware 4.5:** useful integrated audio/bone-conduction eyewear for its period, but evidence does not support advanced sensing, camera or compute capability comparable with modern AI glasses.
- **Wearability 8.0:** fundamentally conventional-looking sunglasses rather than an industrial headset.
- **Visual AI N/A:** no evidence of a camera-based visual-AI system.
- **Software 3.5:** companion-device functionality exists, but the surviving evidence does not show a deep application platform.
- **Openness 2.0:** no meaningful public SDK, firmware or open-source evidence was recovered.
- **Owner Control 6.0:** core audio use is ordinary local accessory behavior rather than a vendor-assistant appliance, but firmware/software control is limited.
- **Cloud Independence 9.0:** ordinary Bluetooth/audio operation does not inherently depend on a manufacturer cloud.
- **Hackability 2.5:** standard wireless/accessory surfaces offer some experimentation potential, but there is no strong public development surface.
- **Display/HUD N/A:** this is audio eyewear, not a visual display product.
- **Value 7.5:** the documented 2019 Japanese retail price placed it well below most contemporary smart-glasses systems.
