# Fauna Audio Glasses — evidence and Report Card

## GLS-0034 — Fauna Audio Glasses

**Evidence lane:** manufacturer manual, FCC-linked manual and component-supplier primary record. No GlassesResearch hands-on claims are made.

Fauna treated audio glasses as eyewear first: acetate frames, replaceable lenses, optician fitting and adjustable temples around a Bluetooth 5.0 open-ear system. A two-way MEMS/electrodynamic speaker design handled music, calls and phone assistants, while the charging case kept the glasses independent of a proprietary wired cradle during ordinary use.

The platform is narrow and durable. It has no camera, display or meaningful public developer surface, but basic Bluetooth audio does not require Fauna's storefront or a Fauna-hosted AI service to remain useful. That survival characteristic matters now that USound describes the project as completed.
