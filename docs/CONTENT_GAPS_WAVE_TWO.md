# GlassesResearch — Research Expansion Roadmap

This roadmap captures the next research layers identified after the first five core pillars. It is a working editorial and data agenda, not a collection of placeholder pages. Each section defines a body of evidence that can be populated across device lineages and connected to report cards, comparisons, preservation work, and buyer research.

## Industry synthesis

### State of Smart Glasses
Maintain a concise current overview of the ecosystem: camera/audio AI glasses, HUD glasses, display viewers, standalone spatial systems, enterprise devices, assistive products, open platforms, and adjacent wearable-HCI systems. Track which architectures are growing, converging, or disappearing.

### Chipset and silicon genealogy
Track SoCs, companion processors, display controllers, sensor hubs and important silicon families. Connect devices that share silicon without assuming they share firmware or OEM lineage.

### OEM and manufacturer map
Track provable relationships among consumer brands, ODM/OEM manufacturers, optical suppliers, chipset vendors, display-engine suppliers and software platforms. Evidence strength must be explicit.

## Physical ownership and longevity

### Prescription and lens compatibility
Capture direct glazing versus inserts, prescription limits, progressive support, photochromic options, lens geometry, replacement paths and ordinary optician serviceability.

### Fit and wearability database
Capture weight, frame width, bridge, temple dimensions, nose adjustment, display/IPD constraints, balance and long-duration wear observations.

### Battery normalization
Separate manufacturer claims from repeatable workloads: idle-connected, audio, camera, AI-query, HUD/display and mixed use.

### Failure and aging database
Track hinges, batteries, delamination, charging failures, display defects, heat, microphones, cameras, software abandonment, account failures and other age-related problems with evidence and sample scope.

### Repairability and teardown research
Track screws/glue, battery access, replaceable temples, lens servicing, charging interfaces, internal photographs, regulatory filings, donor-part interchangeability and repair documentation.

## Capability databases

### Connectivity matrix
Track Bluetooth Classic/BLE, Wi-Fi, USB modes, DisplayPort, UVC, audio profiles, proprietary transports, cellular/GNSS and tether requirements.

### Sensor database
Track cameras, IMUs, magnetometers, ambient/proximity sensors, touch, depth sensing, eye tracking, microphones and unusual sensors. Distinguish hardware presence from owner/SDK accessibility.

### AI capability taxonomy
Separate OCR, scene understanding, object identification, translation, navigation, memory, multimodal conversation, contextual awareness, live-video understanding, local inference, cloud inference and third-party model support.

### Offline/local capability testing
Record what boots, pairs, records, displays, transcribes and reasons when internet access is removed. Feed verified results directly into Cloud Independence.

### Account and onboarding dependence
Track mandatory applications, registrations, phone numbers, vendor accounts, region locks, permissions, always-on connectivity and functionality available before login.

### Subscription and ownership cost
Track subscriptions, AI tiers, storage, cellular service, prescription premiums, mandatory accessories, replacement cases and other ongoing costs beyond MSRP.

### Regional/version differences
Track US, EU, China and global variants by exact model number where possible: radios, AI features, applications, firmware, regulatory constraints, bootloader behavior and accessory compatibility.

## Privacy, security and institutions

### Structured privacy indicators
Per device, track recording indicators, shutter sounds, indicator disablement, local/cloud media handling, retention, microphones in standby, permissions and account dependence.

### Security research
Track documented vulnerabilities and relevant CVEs, pairing exposure, insecure transports, firmware signing, storage encryption, cloud/API exposure and lost-device behavior. Do not infer vulnerabilities without evidence.

### Institutional and legal restrictions
Track glasses-specific policies in courts, schools, hospitals, exams, secure workplaces, government facilities, casinos, venues and jurisdictions. Preserve effective dates and primary-source evidence where possible.

## Real-world adoption

### Enterprise deployment dossiers
Document actual deployments in logistics, maintenance, remote assistance, medicine, manufacturing, aviation, defense, training and field service. Prefer customer, quantity, task, outcome and repeat-deployment evidence over marketing announcements.

### Accessibility dossiers
Build evidence-based guides for visual assistance, live captions/hearing support, translation, navigation, cognitive/memory support and occupational accessibility.

## History and preservation

### Discontinued product cemetery
For dead or discontinued products, preserve launch date, launch price, shutdown/discontinuation date, service status, remaining functionality, software availability, failure mode and historical contribution.

### Firsts and milestones
Use the timeline to identify defensible technological and commercial milestones without turning marketing claims into historical fact.

### Patent and research-paper bridge
Connect influential papers, patents and prototypes to later commercial implementations where evidence supports the relationship.

## Open ecosystem

### Open-source software ecosystem map
Map cross-device projects, community bridges, local assistants, BLE libraries, Linux/Android tooling, Home Assistant integrations and reverse-engineering projects. Track maintained versus abandoned status.

## Evidence and buyer tools

### Field-level evidence confidence
Every important specification should eventually carry provenance: manufacturer, regulatory filing, retailer, independent report, community observation or GlassesResearch hands-on verification. Unknown remains unknown.

### Price history and depreciation
Track MSRP, new street price, refurbished price, used price and notable historical lows with date and region. Separate observable listings from completed sales.

### Requirement-driven buyer decision tree
Build a selector around user requirements rather than generic rankings: display, camera, prescription, local operation, developer access, privacy, budget and form factor.

## Synthesis essays

The database should increasingly support research questions larger than any one device: Who controls smart glasses? Which architectures are winning? Which products can survive their vendors? Why do devices fail? Which designs become socially acceptable? What does an owner-controlled wearable stack require? Where is wearable HCI converging?

These synthesis pieces should cite the underlying GlassesResearch evidence rather than becoming unsupported opinion.
