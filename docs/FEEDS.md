## Reader handoff and fallback

The Feedly and Inoreader buttons above are convenience handoffs into those readers. The public RSS URLs remain the canonical source.

If a reader changes its handoff route, asks you to sign in without preserving the feed, or otherwise fails to pre-load it, use **Copy RSS URL** and paste the canonical URL into that reader's **Follow sources**, **Add feed**, or **Add subscription** field. This fallback does not depend on a reader-specific deep link.

!!! note "Seeing XML in a browser is normal"
    A raw RSS link is meant for feed readers. Many browsers display it as an XML document instead of a human-readable page. Use **Open in Feedly**, **Open in Inoreader**, or **Copy RSS URL** when your goal is to subscribe.
