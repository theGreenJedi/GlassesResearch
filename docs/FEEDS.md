## Add either feed to a reader

### Feedly

1. Tap **Copy Verified RSS URL** or **Copy Wire RSS URL** above.
2. Open [Feedly](https://feedly.com/i/discover) and choose **Follow sources**.
3. Paste the copied feed URL into Feedly's source search and follow the result.

We intentionally do not use Feedly's brittle pre-filled discovery link. It can return **Not Found** even when the RSS endpoint itself is healthy.

### Inoreader

1. Copy the feed URL you want above.
2. Open [Inoreader](https://www.inoreader.com/).
3. Use **Add subscription** and paste the URL.

### Other RSS readers

Copy either RSS URL and paste it into your reader's **Add feed**, **Add subscription**, or **Follow URL** field.

!!! note "Seeing XML in a browser is normal"
    A raw RSS link is meant for feed readers. Many browsers display it as an XML document instead of a human-readable page. Use **Copy RSS URL** when your goal is to subscribe.
