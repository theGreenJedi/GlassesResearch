# Hardware

## Purpose

Document the physical construction, controls, interfaces, and observed component layout of W610/W6xx-family smart glasses.

## Confirmed observations

- The tested device advertises itself as **HeyCyan Glasses** during pairing.
- The right temple contains the primary electronics.
- The left temple is comparatively slim.
- Two buttons are present on the right temple; the rear button is used for power.
- A status LED is located near the hinge.
- Power-on produces an audible tone and a brief LED indication.
