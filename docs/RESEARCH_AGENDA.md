# Research / Content / Populate

GlassesResearch has completed its initial framework phase. The standing operating model is now:

> **Research → Verify → Populate → Preserve → Improve**

The goal is to reduce uncertainty, deepen coverage, preserve fragile knowledge, and make verified information easier to find.

## Mission

**Discover, verify, preserve, and freely share the world's smart-glasses knowledge.**

## Permanent workstreams

### Research

Find concrete information: devices, firmware, SDKs, APIs, BLE behavior, manuals, FCC filings, patents, papers, source code, teardowns, community projects, release notes, company events, and historical records.

### Verification

Move claims toward one of three public states:

- **Verified** — supported by sufficient evidence.
- **Unknown** — adequate evidence has not yet been found.
- **Disputed** — credible evidence conflicts.

Unknown is not a destination. It is a research task unless the project documents why the fact cannot currently be established.

### Population

A verified discovery should update every relevant location rather than remain isolated. Depending on the finding, that can include:

- model pages;
- comparison data;
- the industry timeline and historical archive;
- developer resources;
- FAQs and glossary entries;
- research/news pages;
- evidence and preservation records.

### Preservation

When useful information is fragile or likely to disappear, preserve it legally and ethically. Priority material includes manuals, firmware metadata, SDKs, applications, release notes, public repositories, regulatory filings, protocol research, packet captures, and discontinued product documentation.

### Editorial improvement

Continuously make existing knowledge clearer, shorter, better sourced, easier to navigate, and easier to reuse.

### Corrections

Every factual claim remains open to challenge. Corrections and disputes should be reviewed against evidence and resolved transparently.

## Standing editorial rule

> **Every PR must improve knowledge.**

A pull request should add verified information, strengthen sourcing, preserve useful evidence, resolve an unknown, document a dispute, improve cross-linking, or materially improve how existing knowledge is understood.

Infrastructure-only work should be exceptional and justified by a concrete research need.

## Coverage

### Model coverage

Work model-by-model until each significant device has a useful research chapter with specifications, sources, software/developer information where applicable, related timeline events, comparisons, and unresolved questions.

### Ecosystem coverage

Continue expanding across manufacturers and generations rather than concentrating on a single device family. Historical and discontinued products are part of the research record.

## Success measures

The project should judge progress primarily by:

- claims verified;
- unknowns resolved;
- models documented;
- concrete resources discovered;
- primary sources collected;
- artifacts preserved;
- comparisons populated;
- timeline milestones documented;
- questions answered;
- corrections resolved.

PR count and page count are secondary.

## Research lifecycle

1. **Discover** a useful claim, source, artifact, or lead.
2. **Verify** provenance and what the source actually supports.
3. **Populate** all relevant knowledge views.
4. **Preserve** fragile lawful-to-preserve material or its metadata.
5. **Improve** clarity, sourcing, and cross-links as better evidence appears.

This page is a living agenda. Completed investigations leave the agenda and become part of the permanent knowledge base.
