## Comparison criteria

- Frame shape and dimensions
- Button, LED, microphone, speaker, camera, and charging-port placement
- Pairing name and application
- Packaging and manual wording
- Regulatory markings
- Advertised battery capacity and runtime
- Camera and audio specifications
- Firmware or application compatibility
- Seller and return-policy confidence

## Evidence levels

- **Confirmed identical:** direct hardware, firmware, or protocol evidence
- **Strong match:** multiple distinctive physical and software characteristics align
- **Possible relative:** general design similarity but insufficient distinctive evidence
- **Marketing-only claim:** seller language without technical verification

## Current acquisition rule

For experimental hardware purchases, Amazon is preferred because its return process reduces the risk of receiving an incompatible or misrepresented unit.
