# Challenge Our Research

GlassesResearch treats every factual claim as open to review. If you believe we have made an error, omitted important context, relied on a weak source, or have stronger evidence, we want to know.

**Stronger evidence wins.** A challenge is not an accusation and disagreement is not treated as hostility. It is part of maintaining a research record that can improve over time.

[**Submit a research challenge**](https://github.com/theGreenJedi/GlassesResearch/issues/new?template=research-challenge.yml)

## What to submit

A useful challenge identifies:

- the exact page, model, timeline milestone, comparison field, or claim;
- what you believe is incorrect, incomplete, outdated, or misleading;
- why;
- the strongest available supporting evidence;
- primary sources whenever they exist.

Manufacturer, developer, owner, researcher, journalist, and community submissions are all welcome. The submitter's identity or affiliation does not determine the result; the evidence does.

## Public review record

Research challenges are stored as public GitHub issues so the original dispute, evidence, discussion, and resolution remain reviewable instead of disappearing into private correspondence.

[**View open research challenges**](https://github.com/theGreenJedi/GlassesResearch/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+%22%5BResearch+challenge%5D%22)

[**View all research challenges, including resolved cases**](https://github.com/theGreenJedi/GlassesResearch/issues?q=is%3Aissue+%22%5BResearch+challenge%5D%22)

## Review outcomes

A challenge may ultimately be:

- **corrected** — the published claim changes because better evidence was presented;
- **clarified** — the underlying claim remains substantially correct but the wording or context improves;
- **upheld** — the available evidence continues to support the published claim;
- **needs more evidence** — the dispute is credible but cannot yet be resolved;
- **superseded** — later evidence or events make the original dispute obsolete.

Corrections should preserve enough history to understand what changed and why. GlassesResearch should not silently erase a substantive dispute simply because the underlying page was updated.

## Evidence principles

Challenges are reviewed under the same evidence standard as the rest of the project. Primary records, reproducible hands-on evidence, regulatory material, source code, archived documentation, and other direct evidence generally carry more weight than repeated unsourced claims.

Commercial interests do not automatically invalidate evidence, and community consensus does not automatically establish fact. Conflicts of evidence should be documented rather than hidden.

## Scope

This process is for factual research disputes and corrections. General feature requests, questions, and contributions should use the normal contribution and community channels.
