## Current baseline findings

- A W610-family pair of glasses was acquired through Amazon for experimentation.
- The observed Bluetooth pairing name is **HeyCyan Glasses**.
- The right temple houses most of the visible electronics and controls.
- Two right-temple buttons and a hinge-area LED have been observed.
- Initial Bluetooth pairing behavior was inconsistent and merits controlled retesting.
- The project aims to avoid unnecessary vendor lock-in and investigate phone-based, local-first AI processing.

## Research direction

The intended architecture is a wearable interface whose capture and control functions can be documented and, where feasible, connected to software chosen by the user. Local phone processing is preferred, with cloud services optional rather than mandatory.
