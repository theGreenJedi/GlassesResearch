# Open Research

Open Research is where GlassesResearch turns scattered public technical work into durable, provenance-preserving research records.

These are living technical knowledge bases rather than news stories or software tools. They combine upstream reverse engineering, protocol documentation, owner observations and GlassesResearch bench work while keeping each claim's evidence state visible.

> One reverse-engineering project often leads to another—and sometimes to an entire technical ecosystem. We follow those connections because that’s where discoveries ordinary product searches miss tend to surface.

## Current Open Research

### MYVU Open Research

A unified technical research layer for the Meizu MYVU / Star Air family, centered on the XGA010C lineage. It brings together Panny777's hardware-verified Client and SDK work, CyanBridge's downstream integration, community compatibility/failure reports, and the GlassesResearch verification plan without erasing who discovered what.

**Research includes:** hardware and lineage identity, BLE/GATT behavior, ECDH pairing, RFCOMM transport, compatibility, failure modes, upstream attribution, and reproducible conformance testing.

## Evidence discipline

Open Research preserves the distinction between upstream-documented findings, community reports, GlassesResearch verification, inference, and unknown or conflicting evidence. A community report does not become a product fact because it appears here, and downstream reuse of an upstream discovery does not become independent confirmation.

As GlassesResearch reproduces findings on identified hardware and firmware, the record can move those individual claims to **GR-VERIFIED** while retaining their original provenance.

## Why this is separate from Research & News

Research & News answers **what changed?** Open Research answers **what do we know, how do we know it, where does the evidence disagree, and what still needs testing?**

The technical record remains canonical in the repository so model pages, community pages and future site surfaces can point to one maintained source rather than create drifting copies.
