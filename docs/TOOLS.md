# Tools

The GlassesResearch working library: ways to find devices, evaluate evidence, investigate ecosystems, and build beyond vendor limits.

## I want to…

### Choose glasses

Start with what you need, then narrow the catalog by practical requirements instead of marketing categories.

- [**Find glasses that fit my needs**](COMPARISON_ENGINE.md) — filter the full catalog by practical needs, buying routes, verified price ceilings, and Report Card minimum scores; shortlist two-to-four candidates and compare them.
- [**Start by what I need**](START_BY_NEED.md) — begin with use cases instead of brands.
- [**Understand the ownership tradeoffs**](../buyers/BUYER_AND_OPENNESS_GUIDE.md) — privacy, offline use, repairability, prescription options, vendor lock-in, and long-term usefulness.

### Research a device

Go from a model to the evidence behind it: specifications, Report Cards, source-backed research, lineage, companion apps, and cloud dependence.

- [**Browse every canonical smart-glasses model**](../models/THE_LIST.md) — the cross-vendor eyewear index, past and present.
- [**Read the Report Cards**](REPORT_CARD.md) — Core Report Cards for every canonical model, plus deeper Extended Research.
- [**Open the model research chapters**](../models/README.md) — source-backed device and family dossiers.
- [**Check what survives if the vendor or cloud disappears**](SURVIVAL_MATRIX.md) — app, account, AI-service, and cloud-independence research.

### Build, hack, or repair

Find the technical paths into the hardware and software: SDKs, APIs, BLE, firmware, protocols, repair, reverse engineering, and local AI.

- [**Open Development & Hacking**](../hacking/README.md) — the main technical entrance.
- [**Check SDK / API compatibility**](SDK_API_MATRIX.md) — documented access to displays, cameras, audio, sensors, transports, and custom AI integration.
- [**Find open projects and SDKs**](../hacking/OPEN_HACKING_RESOURCE_LEDGER.md) — community projects and development resources.
- [**Find manuals, firmware, and technical files**](../artifacts/README.md) — preserved primary technical materials.

### Understand the industry

Trace how devices, companies, apps, platforms, lineages, and technologies connect—and what came before.

- [**Explore the ecosystem relationship map**](ECOSYSTEM_MAP.md) — evidence-backed connections among models, lineages, apps, platforms, SDKs, protocols, services, and communities.
- [**Trace technology lineages**](../lineages/README.md) — OEM, rebrand, firmware, app, and platform relationships.
- [**Browse the industry timeline**](INDUSTRY_TIMELINE.md) — products, technologies, companies, and milestones over time.
- [**Visit the historical archive and Museum**](HISTORICAL_ARCHIVE.md) — preserved devices and research that should not disappear.

### Reuse or follow the research

Subscribe to new research, cite stable records, or use GlassesResearch data in another project.

- [**Follow Research & News by RSS**](https://glassesresearch.org/feed.xml) — title, date, and summary in your feed reader; each item returns to the canonical article and citations.
- [**Use the open smart-glasses dataset**](../dataset/index.md) — canonical JSON/CSV exports, lineage and relationship data, content-addressed release metadata, and SHA-256 checksums.
- [**Cite GlassesResearch**](CITING_GLASSESRESEARCH.md) — stable GLS/TL identifiers, attribution formats, canonical URLs, and dataset citation guidance.
- [**Use structured data endpoints**](../data/README.md) — lower-level schemas, per-model bundles, evidence resources, and machine-readable endpoints.
