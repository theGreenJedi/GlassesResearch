## Community and preservation routes

- GitHub searches for model names, companion apps, SDKs, firmware, BLE, protocol, teardown, and recovery
- Reddit model/vendor communities, treated as community reports
- Discord, Telegram, QQ, and forum discoveries when publicly accessible and attributable
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) and [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/)
- APK metadata and lawful archival routes
- Retail listings and manual mirrors, labeled commercial/secondary

## Search patterns

Use combinations of:

- `"smart glasses" release notes`
- `"AI glasses" firmware update`
- `"AR glasses" SDK GitHub`
- `<model> manual OR FCC OR Bluetooth`
- `<companion app> APK OR privacy OR permissions`
- `site:arxiv.org "smart glasses"`
- `site:github.com <model> firmware`
- `<manufacturer> discontinued OR recall OR security`
- Chinese-language model numbers, company names, and app identifiers where relevant.
