# INMO Air3 recalled for left-temple overheating

**Verified · September 24, 2026**

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission announced recall **26-797** for INMO Air3 smart glasses after reports that the left temple can overheat during extended use, creating a burn hazard.

CPSC reports approximately **1,643 U.S. units**, with about **120 additional units sold in Canada**. The firm reported **10 overheating incidents**, including reports of burning sensations to the face and left side of the ear.

The official remedy is unusual and technically important: owners are instructed to stop using the affected glasses and contact INMO for a free repair implemented by updating the system to **V3.16**.

## Why this matters

A safety recall resolved through firmware makes pre-/post-update behavior a meaningful research target. GlassesResearch should not assume what V3.16 changes internally. Possible changes to power, clocks, charging, thermal limits or workload behavior require evidence rather than inference.

The recall does not establish that every Air3 overheats, nor does an unaffected unit negate the documented recall population.
