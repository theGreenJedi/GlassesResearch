# Meta announces camera-free Ray-Ban Meta Audio

**Across the Wire · Reported · September 23, 2026**

Meta and EssilorLuxottica announced Ray-Ban Meta Audio, an audio/AI smart-glasses line that omits a world-facing camera.

The omission matters independently of the product's other features. Camera presence is becoming a product-architecture choice rather than an assumed smart-glasses capability: camera-free eyewear can pursue audio, assistant, translation and communications use cases without creating the same recording capability as camera-equipped glasses.

For GlassesResearch, this is a taxonomy signal, not evidence that camera-free products are inherently more private. Microphones, cloud processing, account requirements, telemetry and data retention remain separate questions.

## Evidence boundary

**Provenance:** manufacturer announcement corroborated by same-day independent reporting.

This entry records the announcement. Battery endurance, microphone performance, comfort, assistant quality, privacy behavior and real-world reliability remain unverified unless separately tested or documented.
