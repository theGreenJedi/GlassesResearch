# RayNeo announces iO, GT and GT Max for September 4

**Published:** August 21, 2026  
**Status:** Verified

RayNeo has formally announced three new smart-glasses products: **RayNeo iO**, **RayNeo GT**, and **RayNeo GT Max**. RayNeo's launch material and current product/support pages point to **September 4, 2026** as the next availability milestone.

## What changed

A high-priority collector lead about three new RayNeo products was independently checked against RayNeo-provided launch material and RayNeo's own product and support pages.

RayNeo's August 21 announcement introduces two new product lines:

- **RayNeo iO Smart Glasses**, positioned as lightweight everyday heads-up/AI glasses;
- **RayNeo GT Series**, comprising **RayNeo GT** and **RayNeo GT Max**, positioned around wearable display and spatial-entertainment use.

RayNeo says the new products will be demonstrated at IFA 2026 in Berlin from September 4–8. Current iO and GT product pages also display **Coming September 4** / near-term availability messaging.

## What we verified

RayNeo's current material establishes that:

- iO, GT, and GT Max are distinct named products, not aliases for existing RayNeo models;
- RayNeo iO has a live first-party support surface describing setup, Bluetooth pairing through the RayNeo app, Smart Crown controls, a heads-up dashboard, live captions/translation, voice memo/AI summary, teleprompter functions, and RayNeo AI features;
- the RayNeo iO product page currently describes the product as coming September 4;
- the RayNeo GT Series page identifies separate GT and GT Max configurations and currently describes the line as coming September 4;
- RayNeo's launch announcement states that both product lines will be demonstrated at IFA 2026.

These are manufacturer claims and product-state observations. GlassesResearch has not independently measured performance or received retail units.

## Why there are no new GLS numbers yet

This update **does not admit iO, GT, or GT Max to the canonical purchaser-history ledger yet**.

GlassesResearch distinguishes an announced product with a stated future availability date from a product with a documented paid acquisition path. At verification time, the first-party pages we checked provide launch/availability messaging and update signups, but we did not verify a paid preorder or ordinary purchase route for these three products.

That means the announcement itself is verified and publishable, while canonical admission remains pending. If RayNeo opens a paid preorder or purchase path before September 4, the catalog can be updated then; otherwise the acquisition boundary should be checked again at launch.

## What we are not claiming

This publication does **not** establish final retail pricing, purchaser fulfillment, independently measured display or battery performance, public SDK access, owner-controlled firmware, cloud independence, repairability, ordinary-optician serviceability, or cross-model hardware equivalence.

No Report Card score transfers from an existing RayNeo product to iO, GT, or GT Max.

## Why it matters

The announcement adds three materially different product targets to a major smart-glasses vendor's roadmap at once: lightweight everyday HUD/AI use on one side and higher-immersion display glasses on the other.

It is also a useful test of the GlassesResearch publication boundary: **news can be verified and worth alerting readers about before a product qualifies for a canonical GLS identity.** Verification and catalog admission are related, but they are not the same decision.
