# Lucyd Armor enters the canonical catalog as GLS-0159

**Published:** August 17, 2026  
**Status:** Verified

Lucyd Armor is now part of the GlassesResearch canonical purchaser-history catalog as **GLS-0159**.

## What changed

Discovery reconciliation showed that Lucyd Armor was not merely another fashion-frame alias inside the broader Lucyd lineup. It is a distinct smart-safety-eyewear product line with its own product identity and use case, so it warranted its own canonical record rather than inheriting identity or scores from Lucyd Lyte-family models.

At the time of admission, the canonical purchaser-history ledger reached **158 models or explicitly marketed product generations**.

## What we verified

- Lucyd publicly markets **Lucyd Armor** as a distinct product.
- It has a documented purchaser path from Lucyd.
- Its identity is distinct enough to justify its own GLS record.
- It should not inherit report-card scores from other Lucyd models unless evidence directly supports that transfer.

## What remains unknown

Canonical admission does not establish every hardware, software, privacy, owner-control, cloud-independence, or hackability characteristic. Any field without sufficient evidence remains unknown rather than being inferred from related Lucyd products.

## Why it matters

This is the kind of change the GlassesResearch discovery conveyor is intended to surface: a product can be easy to miss when it sits inside an existing brand family, but a distinct purchaser-facing product should still receive a stable identity so future evidence, comparisons, aliases, and report-card data attach to the correct model.
