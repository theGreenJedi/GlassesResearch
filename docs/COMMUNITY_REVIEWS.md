# GlassesResearch Evaluate

You do not need to learn GREP, download a worksheet, or already know how GlassesResearch organizes evidence. **Evaluate** walks you through one useful examination at a time and turns the session into a structured evidence package.

<div class="community-review-principles">

## What the application does

Evaluate keeps the research machinery underneath the interface. It resolves retail aliases to canonical GLS records when possible, adapts optional tasks to the tools you have, keeps objective observations separate from impressions, and prepares both a human-readable submission and a machine-readable evidence package.

Your unfinished answers are stored only in your browser on this device. Photographs remain local until you deliberately attach them during submission. Evaluate does not turn a community observation into a GlassesResearch lab finding.

## After you submit

A maintainer checks device identity, provenance, disclosure, internal consistency, and usable evidence. Accepted submissions receive a stable review ID such as `GR-CR-0042`. Persistent public contributors may also receive a stable contributor ID such as `GR-C-0017`.

Persistent contributor history is **provenance, not authority**. Prior good work never makes a new claim automatically true. Community scores remain separate from the canonical GlassesResearch Report Card, while objective observations remain individual claim records that can be corroborated, disputed, superseded, or promoted under the [Evidence and Confidence Standard](EVIDENCE_STANDARD.md).
