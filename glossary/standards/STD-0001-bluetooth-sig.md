# STD-0001 — Bluetooth SIG

## Identity

- **Type:** Standards body and product-registration database
- **Website:** [Bluetooth SIG](https://www.bluetooth.com/)
- **Search tool:** [Launch Studio qualification search](https://launchstudio.bluetooth.com/Listings/Search)
- **Model relevance:** Potential W610 company, chipset, and product-declaration records
- **Reliability:** Primary source for Bluetooth qualifications; absence of a result is not proof of absence
- **Last checked:** 2026-08-03

## Why it matters

Bluetooth qualification records can connect legal company names, product families, controllers, profiles, and declaration IDs. Searching requires aliases: brand names, app operators, suppliers, chipset vendors, and model strings.

## Notes before visiting

Search results may identify a platform or module rather than the final retail product. Preserve declaration IDs and exact applicant names, then connect them to other evidence before assigning a manufacturing role.
