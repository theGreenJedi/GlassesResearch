# STD-0002 — FCC Equipment Authorization

## Identity

- **Type:** United States RF equipment-authorization database
- **Official search:** [FCC Equipment Authorization System](https://www.fcc.gov/oet/ea/fccid)
- **Model relevance:** Potential source of internal photos, test reports, labels, RF modules, and applicant identities
- **Reliability:** Primary regulatory source
- **Last checked:** 2026-08-03

## Why it matters

A matching filing can provide unusually strong evidence: internal and external photographs, block diagrams, operational descriptions, test reports, labels, frequencies, and the legal applicant. These materials may also reveal shared modules across rebrands.

## Notes before visiting

A retail model name may not appear in the filing. Search packaging labels, manuals, charging cases, app-operator names, supplier names, module IDs, and any `FCC ID` markings found during teardown. A module filing does not automatically identify the final-product manufacturer.
