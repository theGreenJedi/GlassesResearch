# ORG-0004 — Shenzhen Qingcheng Wireless Technology

## Identity

- **Known name:** Shenzhen Qingcheng Wireless Technology Co., Ltd. (深圳市青橙无线科技有限公司)
- **Type:** Smart-wearables solution / design-house lead
- **Smart-glasses relevance:** Allwinner V821 and V881 AI-glasses solution ecosystem
- **Confidence:** Strong solution-house evidence; exact W610/W630 design ownership and physical manufacturing role unresolved
- **Last checked:** 2026-08-22

## Why it matters

Coverage of an Allwinner AI-glasses ecosystem event reports that Qingcheng Wireless general manager Huang Lianghui presented a **“V821 Complete Glasses Solution” developed jointly by Qingcheng Wireless and Allwinner**. This is materially stronger evidence of upstream smart-glasses engineering involvement than a reseller page or generic OEM claim.

The lead remains active in 2026: coverage of Allwinner's July 2026 ecosystem conference reports that Qingcheng Wireless demonstrated a new-generation **V881 AI camera/display glasses solution**.

These sources make Qingcheng Wireless a high-value candidate for tracing V821/V881 platform design authority. They do **not** prove that the company designed or manufactures every W610/W630-labelled product using Allwinner silicon.

## Critical identity boundary

Do **not** automatically merge this entity with [ORG-0001 — Shenzhen Qingcheng Future Technology / HeyCyan](ORG-0001-hecyan-qingcheng-future.md).

The names are similar and both appear in the wider smart-wearables / smart-glasses investigation, but the current evidence does not yet establish whether they are:

- the same legal entity under translated/alternate naming;
- related companies;
- companies sharing personnel or software;
- customer/supplier partners; or
- unrelated organizations with similar names.

That relationship is an open research question.

## What remains unresolved

- Whether Qingcheng Wireless originated any specific W610/W630 PCB or mechanical design.
- Whether it owns production firmware source/signing for those commercial families.
- Whether it owns or controls any relevant eyewear molds.
- Whether SMT and final assembly are performed internally, by a related company, or by subcontractors.
- Whether and how Qingcheng Wireless relates to Shenzhen Qingcheng Future Technology / HeyCyan.
- Which current retail products directly descend from the V821/V881 solutions it demonstrated.
