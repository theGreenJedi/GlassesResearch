## Non-negotiable publication rule

Only procedures verified working on an identified model, hardware revision when available, firmware version, toolchain, and test date may be published as working guides.

Everything else must remain clearly labeled **Not Verified Yet**, **Community Confirmed**, **Hypothesis**, **Historical**, or **Disproven**.

> Popularity is not verification. Reproducibility is verification.
